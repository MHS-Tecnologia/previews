About Drain-Flo

Drain-Flo is a locally owned drain and sewer service company launched in 2025 by Eric Gubitoso, a technician with more than a decade of real-world experience in pipe repair, sewer diagnostics, and excavation work. Based in Duryea, Pennsylvania, it works throughout Lackawanna and Luzerne Counties and serves the broader NEPA region.

It has quickly built a strong reputation with consistent 5-star Google reviews. Drain-Flo operates with a straightforward approach rooted in honest communication, dependable workmanship, and fair pricing. As a family-run business, the focus is on treating customers with respect and delivering results that last.

What we do

Complete drain and sewer services for residential and commercial properties, from simple clogs to complex underground repairs, using professional-grade tools including hydro jetting systems and excavation equipment.

No matter the size or scope of the job, the goal remains the same: provide reliable service, use the right equipment, and get the system working properly again.
