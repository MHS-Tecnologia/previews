The design-tracks, side by side

Easy 1-2-3

Three steps from the first meeting to the keys.

  1. Meet and greet

    We get aligned with what your project needs. Deciding your design-track sets the path and helps estimate the cost.

    Key elements: your time, our experience

  2. Design to budget

    We match your financial capability to the right floor plan for your project and your lot. Live anywhere you wish, bank with any lender you want.

    Key elements: your opinion, our time

  3. Approval

    You approve and sign, your lender approves, HTB clears it with city, county and state. Then the job-site goes live, and many long days later we hand over the keys.

    Key elements: your patience, our masterpiece
