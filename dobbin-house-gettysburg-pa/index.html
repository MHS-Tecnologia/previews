Dine in Gettysburg’s oldest house.

Rev. Alexander Dobbin built this stone house in 1776. Today it is a candlelit restaurant, a walk-in tavern, a gift store and an inn next door.

The Gettystown Inn

Nine guest rooms in Civil War era homes next door, furnished to look like 1863. Breakfast is served in the parlor of the Dobbin House.

Guided house tours in 2026

For the 250th year of the house, an hour-plus tour runs almost every day: the basement spring, Mrs. Dobbin’s kitchen, and a hideout on the Underground Railroad.
