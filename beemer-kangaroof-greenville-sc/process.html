What to expect, hop by hop

A roofing project can feel overwhelming. Here is what happens, in order, when you work with us.

Call for a free inspection(864) 412-2573

  1. Initial call and scheduling

    Our team listens to your concerns, answers your questions, and schedules a convenient time for an inspection. We’ll also tell you what to have ready, such as insurance details if storm damage is involved.

  2. Roof inspection and honest assessment

    A roofing specialist performs a thorough inspection, takes photos, explains what we find, and gives you an honest assessment. No pressure, just straightforward facts.

  3. Clear estimate

    We provide a written estimate that outlines recommended work, materials, and pricing, and walk you through every line item.

  4. Project planning and scheduling

    Once you approve the work, we schedule the project and keep you updated. You’ll know when materials will arrive and when our crew will be on-site.

  5. Professional installation

    Our licensed and insured crew arrives on time, protects your property, and gets to work.

  6. Cleanup and final walkthrough

    We thoroughly clean up nails, shingles, and debris, then walk through the property with you to confirm everything meets your expectations.

  7. Ongoing support

    Our commitment doesn’t end when the project is complete. With warranties, maintenance guidance, and responsive customer service, we continue to stand behind our work.
