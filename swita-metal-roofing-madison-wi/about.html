Madison’s trusted metal roofing family

Swita Metal Roofing is owned by Mike and Mary Swita of Madison. We have lived in Wisconsin much of our lives, and we treat every customer and team member as family.

Call Mike and Mary608-298-7334Email for a free estimate

Mike

Mike has sat across the table from more than 3,500 homeowners over the past 15 years. Before co-founding the company with Mary, he was the leading sales executive for a quality metal roofing contractor for 11 years.

He is regularly invited to speak at home shows and Metal Roofing Alliance events on trends and advances in metal roofing.

Mary

Mary has a career of more than 25 years in educating and caring for others, and she is the client care backbone of Swita Metal Roofing.

For Mary, every client is a chance to help one more Wisconsin family discover the lifetime strength and beauty of metal roofing.

Nick

Nick Cindric is the newest member of the team. He used to guide seniors through Medicare options, and he brings the same mindset to roofing: he takes the time to understand each client’s needs, then recommends a roof to match.

The family

Mike and Mary have one son, Will, a recent Mechanical Engineering and MBA graduate. We are a family-owned and operated business, and so are our chosen manufacturers and installers.

The business is built on integrity, courtesy and attentiveness.

Where we work

We are based in Madison and serve the entire state of Wisconsin, including Appleton, Sheboygan, Fond du Lac, Oshkosh and the surrounding areas.
