Speciality coffee

  • Turkish coffee (4oz)$7Jorg enjoyed this traditional African-Arabian method in the highlands between Yemen and Ethiopia, where the coffee tree comes from. Finely ground beans are brewed in a copper kettle (a cezve), with water just below the boiling point, and not filtered. You get a timer: after three minutes, stir the floating grounds down and pour. Served “orta”, with only a small spoon of sugar; ask for more, or for cardamom.

VT = vegetarian, VG = vegan. Please ask us should you have concerns: items may contain allergens, caffeine, meat, egg, nuts, milk and cream. Preparations may be undercooked and could present a health issue for the vulnerable.
