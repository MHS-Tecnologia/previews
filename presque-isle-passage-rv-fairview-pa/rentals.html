Houses, the Lodge and a houseboat

Bigger groups, from a 10-guest farmhouse to a houseboat set on its own site.

The Farmhouse

Sleeps up to 10
  • Two main-floor bedrooms, each with a queen bed
  • Upstairs: one open room with two twin beds and two sets of twin bunks
  • Full kitchen and a full bathroom on each floor

At the front, across from the office, next to the playground.

The Cottage

Sleeps up to 9
  • Queen bedroom and a bedroom with twin over full bunks
  • Four twin beds downstairs
  • Table with 6 chairs, full bathroom

At the front, behind the office and near the pool.

The Apartment

Sleeps up to 8
  • Three bedrooms: a queen, a queen with a twin, and two twins
  • Full bathroom with bathtub and shower
  • Futon, couch, table with 4 chairs

At the front, in the same building as the main office.

The Studio

Sleeps up to 4
  • One large room with a queen bed and a futon
  • Kitchen area, full bathroom

At the front, across from the office, attached to the Farmhouse.

The Lodge

Sleeps up to 8
  • Two bedrooms with queen beds, twin bunks in a sleeping nook
  • Two futons; electric stove (no oven)
  • Full bathroom plus a half bath

At the back of the campground near the tenting area.

The Landshark

Sleeps up to 4
  • A houseboat placed on a site and rented like a cabin
  • One big room: futon, a table that folds into a bed, two twin bunks
  • Half bath only (no shower)

Along the side of the campground past the pool.

Listed capacities are from Presque Isle Passage’s rental pages. Rates include 2 adults except where noted; additional guests over 18 are $3 per night. Ask the office for current rates and the cancellation policy.
