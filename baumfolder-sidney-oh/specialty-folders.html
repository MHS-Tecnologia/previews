When a standard folder can’t, they build one that can.

Baumfolder’s engineers design folders around non-standard sizes, complex folds and materials beyond paper, with intuitive HMI controls and gear drives built to last.

Request a custom quote Call 800.543.6107

Every specialty machine is configured to the job.

  • Paper sizes: non-standard dimensions and custom formats
  • Fold configurations: simple to complex multi-fold patterns
  • Materials beyond paper: parchment, tinfoil, poly bags, cardstock and other substrates
  • Applications: pharmaceutical inserts, work instructions, product packaging, education material and direct mail

Six machines they have built.

The material, the problem the customer had and what the machine changed, as Baumfolder publishes them.

BAUM 714 XV folder, gatherer

Pages gathered before folding

Built from the rugged BAUM 714 series. Customers choose single sheets or multiple pages, more than 2 fold plates, a bigger feeder and stacker, or folding in line.

For bond, glossy paper, newsprint and cardstock, with fold configurations approved by the FDA. It answers a lack of skilled operators by keeping the folding simple.

BAUM 714 with tabber

Fold and tab self-mailers in line, to USPS rules

A BAUM 714 in line with a Kirk Rudy tabber. Load once and have a finished product: half fold, letter fold and tab, or half fold, cross fold, then tab. Meeting USPS rules for self-mailers saves postage.

BAUM 20 in line with Spedo unwinder and forms cutter

Unwind, cut and fold in one operation

A Spedo unwinder and forms cutter with a Baum 1620 INF, 8-page station and a 5-foot conveyor. Paper is touched only when the roll is loaded and when the product is packed. For bond, glossy and parchment paper.

Lean automation: fewer people, more throughput and less waste, because nobody carries paper from the cutter to the folder.

Output
800,000 to 2,000,000 a month

Sheet size and paper

BAUM K20 knife folder

The last fold on bulky booklets

Runs in line with booklet makers, saddle stitchers, floor model buckle folders and tabletop folders, and makes the last fold when it exceeds what a buckle folder can do. Print, bind and fold the booklet in line, without handling it again.

Fold orientation is down. At order you can choose an up fold, straight delivery instead of left, or right-hand delivery.

Poly bag folder

Return packaging, folded 2 to 5 times

A Baum continuous feeder, a series of Baum knife folders and a stacker, for poly bags, Tyvek and paper bags. The folded bag goes into a label feeder on an automation line; a bad fold stops the line, and folding by hand needs people who are hard to find.

Labor savings
5 to 10 operators
Folds
2 to 5

Pile feeder with 4 knife folders

10″ round parchment into a 2.6″ square

A Baum IPF feeder, four K14 knife folders and a horizontal stacker feed, fold and stack round paper for commercial print, packaging and food. The feeder canister is made to fit the product, which matters with slick stock like parchment.

Baumfolder worked with the customer on a fold and fold size that fits the funnel on their automation line.
