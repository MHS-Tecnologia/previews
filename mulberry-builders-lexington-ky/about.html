About

Meet the Mulberrys

A licensed general contractor and home builder based in Lexington, owned and operated by Chris and Chellie Mulberry.

Old made new, a house made a home

Chris and Chellie are Lexington natives. They knew each other from middle school and reconnected in a real estate class in the spring of 2006, while studying at Bluegrass Community and Technical College: Chris in architecture, real estate and business, Chellie in real estate and business.

Chris had begun rehabilitating abandoned properties around Lexington in 2005. Chellie joined in 2007, they married in 2009, and Mulberry Builders grew from there.

They are the parents of three boys and one girl. As business owners and busy parents, they know how much a remodel can disrupt a household, so the team works to make the process as smooth as possible and get you back to enjoying your home.

Chris MulberryPrincipal builder and head of business development; leads construction and client relationships

Chellie MulberryPrincipal designer, focused on spaces clients truly love

The Mulberry team

Remodeling your home or building a new one cannot be done by one person alone. Our team of builders, designers, managers and trade partners take great pride in their work, and genuinely enjoy working together. We know a healthy workplace shows up in the quality of what we build.
