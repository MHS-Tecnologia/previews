A wedding on the lawn, under the trees, by the lake.

Steve and Lynn got married at Cozy Cove in September 2005. It is private and picturesque, with plenty of room for your group, and your wedding party can walk to their cabins.

Call to plan your wedding

What we supply

  • Wedding area, including gazebo and Christmas tree
  • Dressing areas
  • Picnic tables
  • Complimentary pontoon rides at the reception

What we help you with

  • Tuxedo rental
  • Overhead canopies
  • Flowers, bouquet and decorations
  • Wedding cake and food for the reception, including a chef
  • Preacher or court appointed representative
  • Photographer and music
  • Helpers and assistants

Beverages are sold at the Lodge. You can also buy alcoholic beverages from Landmark Liquor: we arrange delivery and provide refrigeration.
