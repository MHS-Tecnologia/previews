The Keweenaw

We know all the best spots to make your stay an adventure to remember. Let us make your dinner reservation or pre-book your sunset cruise or an afternoon at the Spa! Please stop by the resort office for more information.

Places to go

  • Isle Royale National Park
  • Golf at the Historic Keweenaw Mountain Lodge
  • Brockway Mountain Drive
  • Estivant Pines Nature Sanctuary
  • Fort Wilkins Historic State Park
  • Copper Harbor Lighthouse Ferry Tour
  • Hunter's Point Park
  • Horseshoe Harbor Preserve
  • Delaware Copper Mine Tour
  • Clark Mine
  • Copper Harbor Historic Cemetery
  • Beginning of Highway 41

Things to do

  • Collect/rock hunt
  • Collect minerals (we rent metal detectors, inquire at the resort office)
  • Find waterfalls
  • Mountain biking world-class trails
  • Ride ATVs
  • Fishing
  • Scuba diving
  • Hiking
  • Swimming
  • Bird watching
  • Kayaking
  • Tour the gift shops
  • Get a massage, take a yoga class, or health workshops
  • Tour ghost towns
  • Mini golf at Into the Woods
