Policies

Cancellation

If you cancel within 48 hours of the day you are scheduled to arrive, you will lose your $100 deposit. If you cancel anytime prior to that, we will return $85 of your $100 reservation fee, keeping $15 for processing fees.

Dogs

A $25 per dog, per stay fee will be added. Dogs must be housebroken, no dogs on the furniture, and dogs cannot be left alone in the rooms. After a swim in Lake Superior, ask for Doggie Towels.

Bicycles

If keeping your bike in your room, please ask us for bike carpet protectors. For dirty bikes we have a bike washing station. Just ask!

Motorcycle guests

Old towels to wipe your bikes down, just ask. You can also park your bikes under the breezeway for protection from the weather.

Smoking

Michigan State Law prohibits smoking in all public facilities. We do have a $150 fine for guests that smoke within our buildings. You may smoke on our grounds; please be courteous to other guests.
