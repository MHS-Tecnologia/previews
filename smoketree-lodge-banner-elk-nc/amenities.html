Amenities

Our warm, rustic, mountain decor makes it an irresistible place to unwind after a full day of hiking, golfing, or snow skiing.

  • Indoor heated pool

    Relax by the indoor pool after an adventurous day in the High Country.

  • Whirlpool and Jacuzzi

    Next to the indoor pool.

  • Exercise room

    Exercise equipment for an invigorating workout. His and her saunas, too.

  • Game center

    Pool table, arcade games, foosball and ping-pong. The kids will really enjoy the game room.

  • Laundry facilities

    On site, for longer stays.

  • The deck

    A long wooden deck with tables runs along the lodge.

Ask housekeeping

The Housekeeping Department will furnish any of these on request during office hours:

Blenders, mixers, ironing boards, irons, large knives, cribs and sheets, blankets, pillows and cases, flashlights, box fans, corkscrews, spatulas, tongs.
