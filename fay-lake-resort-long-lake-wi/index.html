Long Lake, Wisconsin, deep in the Nicolet National Forest

Cabins that stand over the water

In the resort’s words: “7 of our 10 cabins extend over the lake so you can fish right from your deck.” A 14-foot rowboat comes with every rental.

The only resort in the state with cabins over the water

That is how the resort puts it. Nine of its ten cabins were built on wood cribs in the water in the 1920s, 30s and 40s.

By 2009 the cribs were sinking, so the family began putting some of the cabins on steel piles: 52-foot piles driven into the lake bed, 38 to 43-foot piles into the land, and steel H beams welded on top.
