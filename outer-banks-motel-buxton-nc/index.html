The ocean out front, the sound across the street

Hatteras Island is a strip of sand between two waters. The motel sits on the ocean side of Highway 12, so you get both.

East

The ocean

Steps from the beautiful sandy beach, for swimming, sunbathing, fishing and surfing.

The motel

Highway 12

Across the road

Dillon's Corner, owned by the same family, for tackle. Restaurants, shops and groceries within walking distance.

West

Pamlico Sound

Serene water to boat or crab, and for little ones not quite ready for the ocean waves.

What Hatteras Island has to offer

Everything at the motel, for every guest

Guests in every unit, beach or village, share the amenities at the motel.

  • Saltwater pool
  • Hot tub
  • Library with books and puzzles
  • Fish-cleaning station
  • Coin laundry
  • Free WiFi in the motel lobby
  • Linens and towels provided
  • All rooms non-smoking
