Flo’s directions

From the Long Island Expressway, exit 73 (Tanger Outlets on the right).

  1. East on Route 58, Old Country Rd, 3.3 miles.
  2. Old Country Rd becomes Main Rd, Route 25.
  3. Continue 14.3 miles east on Route 25. After about 14 miles, pass a gas station on the right.
  4. Right (south) onto Oaklawn Ave.
  5. Left onto Pine Neck, 0.3 miles.
  6. Right onto N. Bayview, 1.2 miles.
  7. Left at the stop sign, still N. Bayview.
  8. Less than a mile to 9680 N Bayview Rd. Mailbox on the left.
  9. Make a right into the driveway: the first white house on the left.
