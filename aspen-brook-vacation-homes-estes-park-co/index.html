On the property

Fish the Big Thompson

You may fish the section of river that runs through Aspen Brook during your stay. A fishing license is required for anyone over 16. The office can point you to local guides.

Picnic and play area

Picnic tables, a playground and river access for Aspen Brook guests and residents, first come, first served. Open 9 am to 9:30 pm. Children must be with an adult.

Wildlife

Hummingbirds, deer, elk and the occasional moose are all part of the experience.
