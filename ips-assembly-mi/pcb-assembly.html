Quick-turn and prototypes

Prototype quantities of 1, 5 or 25 boards are built on the same SMT lines, with the same 3D SPI, 3D AOI and X-ray inspection used for production work, so a prototype that passes here builds the same way at volume.

There is no minimum order quantity. When a design is ready to scale, the same program, feeders, inspection recipes and documentation carry into the production release, and pricing steps down with quantity.

Material can be kitted, where you supply the parts, or turnkey, where IPS buys everything and you get one purchase order and one supplier managing availability and alternates.

Design support and DFM

Most production problems are baked in before purchasing starts: a footprint that does not match the part, a BOM line that is obsolete. DFM review and BOM validation are part of new product introduction for turnkey builds; larger engineering tasks are quoted as NRE.

  • Gerber, ODB++ and assembly drawing review
  • Footprint and polarity verification
  • Panelization, fiducial and tooling recommendations
  • Solder process selection
  • Test point and fixture planning
  • BOM scrub for obsolete and long-lead parts
