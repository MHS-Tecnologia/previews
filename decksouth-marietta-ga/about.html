We transform outdoor living spaces

Decksouth’s legacy is expert craftsmanship and intentional design, built on hard work, dedication and a true passion for beautiful outdoor living spaces.

Call (770) 452-3325

Started in 1998 with an office and an idea.

In 1998, our founder John Lea started Decksouth with just an office space and an idea. Since then, we’ve designed and constructed more than 2,000 backyards.

During that time we’ve built more than backyards. We’ve built a reputation for high quality and stellar relationships with our customers, and our commitment to quality shows in our materials, techniques and office culture.

Our frame

The frame is the element that supports the deck. Our mission, vision and core values are the frame that supports Decksouth. Our mission is to deliver an innovative, high-quality custom design and build experience, from concept to completion.

  • Trust

    We keep our commitments to each other and our customers.

  • Integrity

    We do what we say even when nobody’s looking.

  • Respect

    We treat people with dignity and value their contributions.

  • Excellence

    We strive to not only meet but exceed customer expectations.

  • Innovation

    We believe in setting trends instead of following them.

  • Collaboration

    We value and cultivate a winning partnership with our clients.
