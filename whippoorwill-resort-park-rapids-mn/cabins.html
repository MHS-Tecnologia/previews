In every cabin

Dishes, cups and glasses, silverware, pots and pans, coffee maker, toaster, microwave and linens.

Grills, picnic tables and lawn chairs are spread around the resort. A loaner closet has a blender, electric mixer, crockpot, pancake griddle, and an iron and ironing board.

Firewood is sold at the resort. Please don’t bring your own: it spreads disease.

Spring and fall prices are for two people in a 1 or 2 bedroom cabin and three in a 3 bedroom cabin. Each extra person is $10 a night or $50 a week.

Deposits

  • Summer: $250 for the cabin suite or a two bedroom, $350 for a three bedroom.
  • Spring and fall: $150 per cabin.
  • Campground: $75 a week, or $50 for stays under seven nights.

Cancellation

Your deposit, less a $50 service charge, is refunded if the cabin or campsite is re-rented at the full rate for the whole period. Cancel within 90 days of arrival and you are liable for the reservation if it cannot be re-booked.

Good to know

  • All rentals subject to 7.375% sales tax.
  • Summer extra person: $20 a night or $100 a week.
  • Check-in 3:30 p.m., check-out by 9:00 a.m. No early check-in or late check-out.
  • No pets at the resort or the campground.

Booking is a phone call

When you are ready to book, call or email Jim and Jill. If the week you want is taken, ask for the wait list: reservations do change.
