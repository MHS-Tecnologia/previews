Roomy campsites on the Nanticoke River, family run since 1974.

A smaller park in Nanticoke, Maryland, where most sites are 50′ by 50′. A sandy beach, a pier for fishing and crabbing, and weekend entertainment.

What the river has seen

The property’s story, as the family tells it on its own site: from the people who lived on the river to next season’s campers.

See sites and seasons

  • On the river

    The Nanticoke Indians lived along this river, protecting and growing their families.

  • The 1700s

    The property dates back to the 1700s.

  • The Civil War

    Civil War soldiers camped in the surrounding fields.

  • At the wharf

    The Nanticoke Steamboat ran off the wharf area of the camp, carrying people and trade to Baltimore and other places.

  • The legend

    The pirate Bluebeard is said to have travelled the waters of the Nanticoke River.

  • 1974

    The campground has been a family operated business since 1974.

Room to spread out

Most sites are 50′ by 50′. The family’s aim is the traditional camping experience: time with family and friends, close to the water.
