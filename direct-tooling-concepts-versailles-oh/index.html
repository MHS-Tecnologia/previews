From die designs in 1994 to a 20,000 sq. ft. shop.

“It is the mission of Direct Tooling Concepts, LLP to simply be good at what we do. This we believe will lead to a mutual success with our customers resulting in loyalty, growth and profitability.”

The company’s mission statement
  1. 1994

    Dave Berger founds Direct Tooling Concepts in Versailles as a sub-contract die design source.

  2. 2001

    Eric Barlage and Dave Berger start Direct Wire Service. Its first machine is a wire EDM, cutting tooling DTC designed.

  3. 2006

    All operations move into a new 6,000 sq. ft. building on Subler Drive.

  4. 2015

    The building doubles to 12,000 sq. ft.

  5. 2018

    The two companies combine as one business: Direct Tooling Concepts, LLP.

  6. 2023

    Construction starts on an 8,000 sq. ft. addition, bringing the floor to 20,000 sq. ft.
