1982

Two islanders, on the grounds of an old seafood operation

Reggie and Ann Stubbs were both born and raised on Chincoteague. They opened the Island Resort in 1982 on the grounds of a former seafood operation, and it is still family-owned among the chain hotels.

Shops, restaurants, craftspeople and art galleries are within walking distance. Waterfowl feed in the salt marshes just outside your door.

Pier, pools and a gym

  • Free continental breakfast at the award-winning Breakfast Café
  • Indoor and outdoor swimming pools
  • Hot tub room
  • Gym with Hammer Strength machines, plus a cardio room
  • 600 ft. pier and observation deck
  • Landscaped garden with fishponds
  • Free guest laundry
  • Accessible routes to the pier, deck and breakfast area
