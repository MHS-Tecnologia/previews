Aqua Arcade

Sheet04of 06
WaterLake Charlevoix
TownEast Jordan, Michigan
CompletedFall 2023

A Lake Charlevoix family cottage, revitalized and kept in character.

“Aqua Arcade” stands as a cherished Lake Charlevoix family retreat, steeped in generations of memories. Recently revitalized, the owners were steadfast in preserving the timeless charm of the cottage. Rustic exterior wood cladding meets a burst of vibrant colors and playful themes in every room. Blending seamlessly with its natural surroundings, the cottage embodies lakeside bliss. Outside, the expansive covered porch offers the perfect place for enjoying the lake vistas, while hanging out by the outdoor fireplace.
