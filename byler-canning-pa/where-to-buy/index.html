Home / Where to buy

Two ways to get a jar.

Order online and it ships the next business day, or pick one up at one of over 500 specialty food stores nationwide.

Order online

  • Orders ship within 1 business day by UPS Home Delivery, which takes 3 to 4 days.
  • Free shipping on orders of 12 jars or more.
  • Shipping across the United States.
  • Gift certificates are available.

Online storePrices, cart and checkout stay on Byler’s current store. They are not part of this demo.

Pick one up in a store

You can find Byler products in over 500 specialty food stores nationwide, among them Giant Eagle, Valesky’s, Brigiotta’s and Sander’s Markets.

Byler keeps a map of the stores that carry its jars. Not sure there’s one near you? Ask.

Help put Byler’s on the map.

Noticed a store selling Byler products, or one that has stopped? Send the store’s name and city. Once the details are confirmed, its pin goes on the map, or comes off it.
