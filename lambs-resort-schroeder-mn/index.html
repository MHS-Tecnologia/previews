Every campsite has a color and a number

Lamb's has the only campground on Minnesota's North Shore of Lake Superior with sites right on the lake. Pick a color for your rig or tent, then a lake site or one tucked in the woods. Lake sites have a three-night minimum.

Twelve cabins, known by their numbers

All are full housekeeping: kitchen, bathroom with shower, bedding and towels, new pillow-top queen mattresses, and a fire ring and charcoal grill outside. Check-in 3 pm, check-out 11 am.

Log cabins on the Cross River

The smallest spaces, and the first cabins built on the property, in the 1920s. Often called the Honeymoon Cabins: the sound of the Cross River Falls surrounds you.
