About the academy

James Flood opened Westlake Music Academy on April 1st, 2019, bringing his 40-some guitar students with him.

From 40 guitar students to over 700

In just 7 years WMA has grown into a school of over 700 students, with 40 teachers offering lessons in 12 different instruments.

James’ vision was a school of excellence, with teachers who are degreed musicians and experts on their own instrument. But being a good musician is not enough: they have to be teachers too, with a love and a gift for teaching.

Becoming a good teacher takes practice, just like becoming a good musician. So all WMA teachers meet twice a month to discuss approaches, problem-solve for one another and give presentations.

Why we do what we do

Playing music is a skill within everyone’s reach, and learning to play an instrument well is deeply satisfying. It is not just for now: it is a life-long gift.

Learning an instrument also teaches that if you work hard at something and persevere, you will get it, and that lesson carries into work, community and home.

Recognition

Westlake Music Academy was the winner of Cleveland Magazine’s Best of the West 2020: Music Instruction, and Lessons.com Best Piano Lessons in Lakewood 2024.
