Good to know

  • Check in no earlier than 4:00 PM. Check out no later than 9:00 AM.
  • All summer rentals are Saturday to Saturday.
  • Please bring your own bath and kitchen linens.
  • All rentals are charged 12.25% tax for state, county and town taxes.
  • The lodge is non-smoking. Smoking inside, or cigarette butts left outside their proper container, means a cleaning fee from your security deposit.

Reserving your week

Contact Sarah to ask about your dates and make a reservation. A $1,000 deposit is required for weekly prime-time stays, and a $750 deposit for 3-night stays or off-season weeks.

Cancellation and refunds

Your deposit is refunded as long as you let them know no later than 30 days before your stay. Full payment is due no later than 2 weeks before your stay. If you cancel within 2 weeks of your stay, 50% of your total stay is refunded, unless they are able to fill the vacancy.
