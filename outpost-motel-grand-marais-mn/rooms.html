Rooms and 2026 rates

Our rooms will remind you of a north woods cabin, with a great lake view. Fourteen units, all non-smoking, and a pet free property.

Motel rooms

Knotty pine walls, hardwood floors and spectacular views of Lake Superior, giving them the rustic feel of a cabin on the lake.

  • One queen size bed, two single beds, or two queen size beds
  • Private bathroom and in-room coffee maker
  • A small TV that receives six stations via satellite
  • One night stays are fine in the queen, two twin and two queen rooms

Kitchenette suites

A living room, a kitchen area, one or two bedrooms, and a private bath for each bedroom. Bedrooms have one queen size bed and living rooms have sleeper sofas.

  • Stove, sink, refrigerator, microwave, coffee maker and toaster
  • Cooking and serving utensils
  • Every kitchenette has a lake view
  • Two night minimum stay

The loft apartment

An upstairs apartment in a separate building, with a beautiful lake view out the front and a forest view out back.

  • One bedroom with two queen size beds
  • A living and dining room with kitchen facilities along one side
  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Two night minimum stay

Ask Todd and Holly about the loft: 218-387-1833 or info@outpostmotel.com.

Good to know before you book

Longer stays

15% off all stays of 5 or 6 nights. 20% off all stays of 7 nights or more.

Add ons

Roll away bed, $10.00 per night.

Minimum stays

The standard queen rooms, the two twin bed room and the two queen bed room allow one night stays. The kitchenette suites and the loft apartment have a two night minimum.

Pets

The Outpost Motel is pet free. Pets may not stay in the vehicle either.

Cancellations

Kitchenette suites and the loft: at least seven days notice. Motel rooms without kitchens: at least 48 hours notice. Without it, your card is charged one night (rooms) or two nights (kitchen units), plus tax.

Early check out

You are charged for the nights you reserve, whether or not you stay all of them. There is no credit for early departure.
