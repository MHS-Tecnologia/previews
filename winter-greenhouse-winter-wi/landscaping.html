How a larger project starts

  1. Gather as much as possible about the site and your needs. A questionnaire and a site plan to draw your measurements on can be picked up at the greenhouse.

  2. A visit at your place to look over the areas. They take photos and measurements.

  3. Computer-aided drawings and before-and-after images, made to fit your site.

  4. A consultation at the greenhouse to go over the plan and images, with quotes for plants, materials and installation, if you want it installed.

Plants can control dust, prevent soil erosion, block the wind, provide shade, save energy, minimize sound and increase privacy.

Whether you are considering a large project, need a hand cleaning up in the fall or help diagnosing a problem, Winter Greenhouse is there to help get the job done.

Their guarantee

All their trees and shrubs have a 100% replacement guarantee for one year from the date of installation. Each job gets a follow-up visit later in the year, or the following year.
