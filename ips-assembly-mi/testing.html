Why 3D inspection changes the outcome

2D inspection sees shape; 3D inspection measures height and volume. That is the difference between spotting a bridge and measuring whether a joint has enough solder to survive vibration.

3D SPI catches paste problems before parts are placed, and 3D AOI measures position, height and solder fillet on every component after reflow.
