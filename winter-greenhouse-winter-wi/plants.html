Plants in the greenhouse

A large variety of high quality plants, and every year a list of new ones. These are some of the 2026 introductions from their in-store catalog.

Local store currently closed for the season. Smaller plants are in the online shop year round.

New annuals

  • Begonia, iCandy Sunset

    Annual

    Large double flowers in vibrant colors against dark chocolate foliage. Flourishes in part sun and shade.

  • Calibrachoa, Magic Double Grapefruit

    Annual, Millionbells

    Opens lemon yellow, then takes on soft pink tones in the summer sun.

  • Cosmos, Cherry Chocolate

    Annual

    A new rosy color, flowers up to 3 inches, and a rich cocoa scent.

  • Hydrangea, Fairytrail Fresco

    Hanging baskets only

    Mop-head blooms that turn vibrant blue, foliage that turns deep red in fall.

  • Petunia, Inferno Metallic Orange

    Annual

    Fiery red-orange petals with a deep, metallic purple sheen at the center.

  • Snapdragon, Yellow Red Heart

    Annual

    Early blooms, long-lasting flowers and an amazing candy-like scent.

New vegetables

  • Cauliflower, Hipster

    Vegetable

    7-inch heads in an intense, deep purple that runs down into the stems.

  • Cucumber, Quick Snack

    Vegetable, only sold in baskets

    Compact vine for containers; early, bitter-free cocktail cucumbers.

  • Squash, Green Lightning

    Vegetable

    Patty pan squash, 40–45 days, light green with dark green stripes.

New perennials

  • Allium, Bee’s Knees

    Perennial, Ornamental Onion

    Golf-ball sized lavender purple flowers from July through August. Pollinator magnet.

  • Astilbe, Chocolate Shogun

    Perennial, False Spirea

    Chocolate-bronze foliage behind pink-blushed flowers.

  • Clematis, Elpis

    Perennial, pruning code 3

    Bold deep red flowers with yellow anthers, for tight spaces.

  • Delphinium, Hunky Dory

    Perennial, Larkspur

    Blooms all summer. Zone 4b hardy. A great cut flower.

  • Echinacea, Raspberry Ripple

    Perennial, Coneflower

    Double raspberry pink flowers, 4–4.5 inches across, mid to late summer.

  • Chrysanthemum, Hardy White Daisy

    Perennial, Mum

    White flowers into fall. Hardy mums can withstand severe cold.

New trees and shrubs

  • Abies koreana, Ice Breaker

    Korean Fir

    A colorful dwarf whose needles curl to show their waxy white undersides.

  • Acer rubrum, Armstrong Gold

    Maple

    Narrow, columnar red maple that glows golden, then orange, in fall.

  • Apple, Kudos

    Apple

    A Honeycrisp and Zestar cross from the University of Minnesota. Needs a pollinator.

  • Aronia, Berry Scape Red

    Chokeberry

    White spring flowers, then fruit from yellow to orange to brilliant red.

  • Buddleia, Mop Top

    Butterfly Bush

    Weeping branches of pale purple flowers, blooming in spring.

  • Hydrangea, DreamCloud

    Hydrangea

    Pure white flowers that bloom and rebloom late into the season.

The printed catalog

The full in-store catalog lists every plant with its pot size. Winter Greenhouse will happily send a free, printed copy to anyone who intends to visit the greenhouse and nursery. Sign up before October for that year’s catalog; otherwise next year’s goes out beginning in February.

Signing up also brings:

  • A $10 credit for every $250 of total purchases, applied to your next purchase.
  • $10 virtual bonus bills for every $100 spent during April and May, applied in July for up to 50% of your plant purchases.
  • Returns without a receipt: they keep track for you.

Special orders

A specific color or a certain number of plants, a unique tree or shrub, hanging baskets reserved with a special design: call or email and they will do their very best to help.

Please give as much advance notice as you can. After May 1st, a 15% fee applies to orders left in their care for more than a week.
