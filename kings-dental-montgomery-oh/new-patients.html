Welcome, new patients

Here is everything you need to know before your first visit.

Call 513.770.0175Email info@kingsdent.comMon to Fri, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, both offices

What to expect at your first visit

  1. Warm welcome

    Our front desk team will greet you, help you complete any remaining paperwork, and answer your questions. We recommend arriving 15 minutes early.

  2. Comprehensive exam

    A thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and oral health. This typically includes digital X-rays, an oral cancer screening, and a review of your medical and dental history.

  3. Professional cleaning

    One of our hygienists provides a gentle yet thorough cleaning to remove plaque and tartar buildup.

  4. Personalized treatment plan

    We discuss any findings and create a customized treatment plan if needed, and walk you through your options and costs before any treatment begins.
