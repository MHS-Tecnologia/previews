Arrive by road, or by river

Twin Gables sits in the Skamokawa National Historic District, on the lower Columbia Water Trail, a short walk from all local attractions.

By road

From Interstate 5, take the Longview exit and continue 30 miles west on State Route 4 until you reach Skamokawa. The highway crosses the Skamokawa Creek Bridge: first right after the bridge, at the shingled house with the red roof and white fence.

Off-street parking is just outside our front gate. Locked storage is available for bicycles.

Twin Gables from the parking area.

By river

The Twin Gables private dock is just a few steps from the front door of the inn and 100 yards from the Columbia River. At 27 feet, it is the perfect platform for small cabin cruisers or fishing boats.

A public launch ramp just across the road makes kayak launching easy, right from your car top or trailer. For day-long river paddles, we’ll pack you a lunch.

The entrance to Skamokawa Creek, off the lower Columbia.

Agnes’s Room and Edna’s Room

Our rooms are named for the daughters of Andrew and Hilma Peterson, who built and lived in their home on Skamokawa Creek in the 1890s. Both look out over the creek.

Breakfast at the time you choose

Need your breakfast at 5 am? – no problem!

Fresh ground and brewed coffee is delivered outside your door an hour before breakfast. Then a starter of fruit salad, and a main course such as French toast or a savory egg dish, with home made bread.

Hot beverages are available 24 hours, and you’ll find a welcoming snack in your room on arrival. Optional lunches and dinners are available at an extra charge.

Breakfast, Simple Suppers and more

Your innkeepers are Kathleen and Stevan Morgain

We restored this historic home in Skamokawa with the aim of sharing the experience of rural life in our tiny corner of the world. With just two inn rooms, you’ll enjoy attentive service from friendly but not obtrusive innkeepers.

For families with children and/or pets, and those who prefer to do their own cooking, we also offer a separate vacation rental apartment next door to the inn.
