Six homes in Manteo

The cottages

All of them are centered around the town of Manteo, full of charm, tastefully decorated and outfitted with every comfort you expect for a relaxing stay.

Clemons Cottage

404 Budleigh Street, Manteo

This two-bedroom, one-bath cottage is the perfect retreat to enjoy Roanoke Island. Years ago, it was moved from the nearby beach to Manteo, where it served as home to many of the original families on the island.
