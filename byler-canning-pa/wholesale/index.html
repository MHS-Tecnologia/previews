Private label: your name on the jar.

Create your own brand without the hassle of production. You bring the brand; Byler takes care of the canning, quality and fulfillment. Choose from over 60 handcrafted products: pickles, relishes, specialty items, homemade jam, jelly and more.

  1. Talk through your brand, the label design and the products you’ve picked.
  2. Byler creates label proofs for your review and revises them with your feedback.
  3. Approve the final design and place your order.
  4. Byler prints and applies the labels, so the jars arrive ready to sell.

For the label: your logo, a product name and store contact information. Add a short bio, a barcode or a nutrition panel if you like, and share any graphics or font preferences.

Terms: private label minimum 20 cases per product, label design from $125 per label. Unlabeled products: $750 minimum per order.
