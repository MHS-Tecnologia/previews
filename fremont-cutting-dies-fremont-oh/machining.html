Home / Machining & fabrication

Contract machining, from one feature to the whole part.

The equipment that builds Fremont’s dies takes outside work too. Have parts machined complete, or just a feature added, and save on equipment, people and wear and tear.

Your material or ours.

Fremont can buy the material for the job, or machine material you supply.

  • Mild steel
  • Tool steel
  • Aluminum
  • Brass
  • HDPE
  • ABS
  • Delrin

On the floor: vertical machining centers, a CNC lathe, a manual lathe, wire EDM, surface grinders, a Blanchard grinder, Bridgeports, a CNC rule processor, and manual and hydraulic bending equipment. As long as the part fits the machines, it can be made.

The machines, by the numbers.

  • 17″ × 24″ × 12″

    Wire EDM

    Approximate travel, for intricate shapes with tight tolerances.

  • 42″

    Blanchard grinding

    Chuck size: a uniform flat finish on large components.

  • 16″ × 32″

    Surface grinding

    Chuck, with a table load of about 1,500 lb, for finish and thickness control.

  • 80 ton

    Sharp edge cutting press

    24″ × 72″, 4-post. Die try-outs and runs under 100 impressions.

  • 2pt–4pt

    CNC auto bending

    Every steel rule die, from 0.937″ to 2″ high.

  • CO2 laser

    Laser cutting

    Thin gauge mild and stainless steel. Short runs of some substrates are cut with no die at all.

How a machining job runs.

  1. You get a clear price and lead time for the project.
  2. On approval, Fremont orders the material or receives yours.
  3. The parts are machined to your specifications.
  4. The order ships, is delivered, or is ready for pickup.

Custom fabrication.

From a single piece to small production runs, built to your specifications.

Outsource welding

MIG and TIG welding across a wide range of materials.

Work-in-progress racks

Heavy or light gauge, stand-alone or stackable. They can be tethered into a train, with casters and pull handles or fork pockets for a fork truck.

Adjustable fixture check tables

Built to your spec. The featured example is 48″ × 96″, adjusts from 30″ to 42″ high, and rolls on locking swivel and fixed casters.
