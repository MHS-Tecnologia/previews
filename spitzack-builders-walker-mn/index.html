The Garbin

Garage plus cabin equals Garbin. Gar + bin = Garbin

The Spitzack-Built Garbin is an increasingly popular way to enjoy your lake lot. Use it as a weekend getaway, or live in it until you build the house, then keep it as the guest cottage.

How a Garbin works

Timber frame, our specialty

Timber frame homes are built around the wooden trusses, posts and beams that form the exposed frame of the structure.

The result is a warm, welcoming home with rustic appeal, versatile enough to marry elegance with northern lakes living.
