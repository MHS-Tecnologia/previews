From one open-top mixer to a fleet.

Frank Casilio was a concrete contractor in Bethlehem. In 1938 he bought a Ford with a 1.5 cubic yard open-top mixer to help his own jobs, and delivering ready-mixed concrete grew into the business. Four generations later, it is run by his granddaughter, Maria Medei.

Read our history

  • 19413 cubic yards. The first drum mixer, a Worthington.
  • 196710 cubic yards. A Mack DM 600 tri-drive with a Jaeger mixer, one of the first.
