The pool, the beach and the building

Treasure Island sits on the quiet east end of Thomas Drive, near St. Andrews State Park.

Pool deckBeachBuilding and parking

Pool deck

The tropical beachfront pool area has a 120 ft long heated pool, a separate children’s pool, lounges, deck chairs and gazebos. A BBQ and picnic area sits at the east end of the pool deck, with gas grills for guests.

Beach

Step down to 420 feet of white sand beach on the emerald water, with parasailing, windsurfing, jet skis and banana boats. From early March through mid-October, renters get free use of the beach service: two lounge chairs with an umbrella on the sand.

Building and parking

Drive right up to the 6th floor, unload your car and park without using the elevators. 612 has a covered reserved parking spot on the second parking level, close to the elevator.

  • Secure covered parking garage
  • Five separate covered walkovers, one on the 6th floor
  • Four high-speed elevators
  • Fitness room with an ocean view
  • Game room for children and teenagers
  • In-house security, available by phone
