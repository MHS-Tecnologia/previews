BAUM 20 8-page and 16-page stations

Right-angle cross folds, complex and accordion folds

Added to the BAUM 20 1st station for right-angle cross folds. The 2nd station can also run in tandem, in a straight line, for roll and accordion folds that need more parallel folds than station 1 gives.

With BAUM power adapter boxes they also run on their own, in line with inkjet tables, forms cutters and creasing machines.

BAUM 20 INF inline folder

Folding for cutter and bursting applications

Based on the BAUM 20 pile feed folder, without the sheet feeder and control box, so a burster or cutter feeds sheets straight onto the register table. Made for high-volume mailing, with fan-folded forms or roll-feeding lines with cutters.
