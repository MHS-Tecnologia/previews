Finding the lodge

5683 Belden Lane
Boulder Junction, WI 54512

The address changed in the fall of 2012; the lodge did not move. If your GPS can’t find the new address, use the old one: 10473 Boulder Lane, Boulder Junction, WI 54512.

From Milwaukee

  1. Take I-94 West, then I-39/51 North to Minocqua.
  2. Continue through Minocqua on Highway 51 to Highway M north.
  3. Take Highway M north (right). It dead-ends into Highway K, where K and M become the same road.
  4. Turn left on Highway K/M.
  5. Continue through the downtown area, then stay on Highway M (right).
  6. Take Highway M to Boulder Lane and turn left.
  7. Take Boulder Lane to the first street on your left, the newly named Belden Lane.
  8. Turn left on Belden Lane. The lodge is just above the tennis court, overlooking Boulder Lake.
