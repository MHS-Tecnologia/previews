Two small greenhouses in a backyard.

That's how Larry and Roseanna Schulte started, growing vegetable and flowering plants for customers in the local area, with all their children helping along the way.

Our story

We moved to our present location in 1978 and grew into a full line of trees, shrubs, evergreens and perennials. Most of the nursery stock is started from small cuttings and grown to saleable size right here, because locally grown matters to us. Other plants come from growers locally and around the country.

Two of Larry and Roseanna's sons, Dave and Dan, now have ownership in the business, and in spring and summer most of the family works here. In 2023 Schulte's Greenhouse and Nursery celebrated 60 years.

We have nurserymen on staff, certified by the Minnesota Nursery & Landscape Association, which is affiliated with the University of Minnesota. Staff attend seminars and conventions to keep up with plant trends and new ways to fight insects and diseases.

Schulte's is a member of Minnesota Grown, the Minnesota Nursery and Landscape Association, Shop St. Michael, the I-94 Chamber of Commerce and the International Plant Propagator's Society.

Going green

Our part in controlling waste:

  • Customers can bring in used nursery containers, and we reuse them.
  • We recycle all of our cardboard and aluminum cans.
  • Straw used for winter covering is spread on the field in spring, dried and baled again.
  • We compost all our used soil, plants and other organics.
  • Controlled-release plant food on all our plants, so there is no excess runoff.
  • Some plants are grown in biodegradable pots.
  • Most of our utility carts are electric.
  • We changed out the lighting in all of our buildings to save energy.
