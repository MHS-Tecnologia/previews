The lodge, floor by floor

Five bedrooms, three bathrooms and plenty of living space on three levels. Non-smoking, air-conditioned, with free wireless internet, and open to rent all year.

Jump to Third floor Main floor First floor Outside

Third floor

The rest of the bedrooms, a 3/4 bathroom and a newly renovated full bathroom. Each bedroom has a closet, a dresser and a mirror.

Fish Bedroom

Queen + bunk beds · Faces the lake

In the front of the lodge, with a great view of the lake and the tennis court. A Queen bed and a set of bunk beds for the kids.

Bear Bedroom

Queen · Faces the lake

Also faces the front, with a view of the lake and the woods around the house. A Queen bed.

Bear Bathroom

3/4 bath · Back of the lodge

Between the Bear and Deer Bedrooms. A 3/4 bathroom, newly renovated.

Deer Bedroom

Two twins · Faces the woods

In the back corner of the lodge, with views of the woods and nature around it. Two Twin beds.

Wolf Bedroom

Queen · Faces the woods

Faces the back of the lodge, with a view of the acre lot behind it. One Queen bed.

Tree Bathroom

Tub, shower, double sink · Back of the lodge

Renovated in the spring of 2015. Faces the back of the lodge and has a tub, a shower and a double sink.

Main floor

The best views in the house: both rooms have floor-to-ceiling windows.

Living Room

Fireplace, lake view · Faces the lake

In the front of the lodge, with a spectacular view of the lake and wildlife through floor-to-ceiling windows. Our second fireplace, a leather couch, love seat and recliner, plenty of books for all ages, and another TV.

Kitchen

Double oven, dishwasher · Faces the woods

In the back, with a view of the woods; from the table you can see the lake through the Living Room. Pots, pans, utensils, dishes and silverware are provided, with a double oven for large groups, a dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker and refrigerator. All you need to bring is the food.

First floor

The Moose Bedroom and its bathroom, the Family Room, and the Mud Room.

Family Room

Fireplace, 100+ movies · Faces the lake

Overlooks the lake and the tennis court. One of our two fireplaces, a sectional couch, a smart TV with a Blu-Ray player and over 100 DVDs and Blu-Rays (more than 50 for kids), and a table for the board games and puzzles.

Moose Bedroom

Two queens · Faces the woods

Faces the woods behind the lodge. Two Queen beds.

Moose Bathroom

Shower · Back of the lodge

Attached to the Moose Bedroom, with access to the game room. Includes a shower.

Mud Room

Washer, dryer, sink · Back of the lodge

Reached from the back of the house. A washer and dryer, an extra fridge, a sink and a high chair. Cleaning fish at that sink? Please come in through the outside door.

Outside and in the garage

The equipment room in the garage holds bicycles, safety vests for the boats, sand toys, cross-country skis, and the oars for the aluminum boat, canoes and kayaks.

The sandy area

A small sandy area for the kids to play in, a paddle boat, an aluminum fishing boat, 2 canoes and 3 sit-top kayaks.

Tennis and pickleball

A tennis court with pickleball lines and a basketball hoop, just below the lodge.

The backyard firepit

Behind the lodge, with a back deck, gas grill and picnic table nearby.

The equipment room

In the garage, which is also where snowmobilers store their gear: the lodge is on Snowmobile Trail 15.
