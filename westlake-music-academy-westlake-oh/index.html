How lessons work

Tuition
$38 per half-hour lesson, whatever the instrument, age or level. A one-time $25 registration fee per household, and the first month is prorated.
Commitment
Weekly lessons, paid month to month. No large upfront payments and no long semester commitments.
Missed lessons
Makeup classes on Fridays and Saturdays.
Parents
Welcome to sit in on any lesson. Children do better, and stick with lessons longer, when a parent is in the loop.
Safety
A security camera in every lesson room (no audio), an observation window, and two nationwide background checks for every teacher.
