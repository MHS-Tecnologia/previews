Rental reservations

  • 2-night minimum; holidays are 3 nights.
  • Reservations are paid in full when you book.
  • Cancel at least 30 days ahead, or within 24 hours of booking, for a refund. A $25 handling fee and a 5% fee apply to all cancellations.
  • Bring your own sheets, pillows and towels.

Seasons and holidays

Off-season rates apply before Memorial Day Weekend and after Labor Day Weekend.

Holidays and special events: Memorial Day Weekend, the 4th of July and Labor Day Weekend.

Check in for all campsites and rentals is 3:30 PM. Check out is 11:00 AM.

Park rules

For the safety and enjoyment of all campers, the park asks you to follow these rules.

  • Check-out. Check out for all rentals and RV sites is 11:00 am. There are no refunds for early departures.
  • Quiet hours. Midnight to 7:00 AM.
  • Speed limit. 5 MPH on campground roads at all times.
  • Picnic tables. One picnic table per site. Do not move picnic tables from the site.
  • Pets. Dogs must be kept on a leash at all times. Do not leave pets unattended. Please clean up after your pets.
  • Parking. Park on the gravel, not the grass. Each rental has parking for two vehicles; each campsite has parking for a vehicle plus a camper or RV.
  • Campfires. Allowed on campsites in fire rings only.
  • Pool. Open 10 am to 9 pm, weather permitting, and subject to change. No lifeguard; swim at your own risk. Anyone 16 and under must be with an adult over 21.
  • Alcohol. Must stay on your site or at your rental. Prohibited under 21.
  • Trees. No cutting of any trees, at any time. Do not drive nails into any tree.
  • Water. No vehicle washing. No washing of clothes, dishes or utensils in restrooms.
  • Guests. All campground guests must check in at the office. Overnight guests are $3 per night.
  • Messages. Posted on the message board outside the office door.
  • Trash. Use the dumpster at the campground exit.
