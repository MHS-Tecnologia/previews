A local, family-focused business

Dann and Julie Van Dyke have owned Cottage Works since the beginning, in April 2009. Their mission: products and services that help achieve the perfect work/life balance.

Call 231.233.7010Request a service

Lake-living experts

After being cottage owners and weekenders in Mason County for five years, and living full time on Lost Lake (Custer) and Hamlin Lake (Ludington) for the past 20, they’ve learned a lot about lake living that they like to share with clients.

Dann is a degreed mechanical engineer from Grand Valley State University. He worked 17 years as a designer, project engineer, project manager, customer support manager and engineering manager, and he also has his builder’s license and many years of building experience.

“We do not beat around the bush and give you false promises.”

Focused on making each summer special

Each year starts with the excitement of spring. The dock and lifts are placed for the water depth. Maybe you need a new dock, or a patio so the grandkids have more room to fish and play. Boats come out of storage and are launched. A seawall, deck refinishing, siding power washed, screens replaced, painting: the crew can take care of anything on your to-do list.

Working through the winter

Let Cottage Works use their building expertise to handle any inside renovation during the winter months. A kitchen or bathroom update, or new flooring, done while you enjoy your winter away.
