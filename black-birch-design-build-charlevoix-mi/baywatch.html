A contemporary home on the rocky coastline of Lake Michigan.

Standing on the rocky coastline of Lake Michigan, this clean and contemporary home epitomizes modern luxury with amenities tailored for both relaxing and entertaining. It showcases clean lines, high quality materials, and thoughtful craftsmanship throughout. The kitchen island is a chef’s dream, massive enough to cook for an entire soccer team, or host the best cocktail party. When you’re done, head down to the golf simulator to perfect your swing, enjoy a bottle of perfectly kept wine from the wine room, or unwind in the private sauna. Top off the evening by sitting out on the expansive back porch to take in the best of Michigan’s sunsets.
