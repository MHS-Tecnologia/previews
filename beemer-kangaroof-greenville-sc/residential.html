Roof repair

Even a small leak or damaged shingle can quickly turn into costly interior damage. We deliver long-lasting repairs, not temporary fixes.

  • Leaky roof repair at the source
  • Missing, cracked, curled, or wind-damaged shingles
  • Flashing, chimney and vent repairs
  • Storm and wind damage
  • Nail pops, small gaps and minor shingle issues

Most repairs are backed by a 1-year repair warranty.

Roof replacement

When repairs aren’t enough, we install durable, energy-efficient roofing systems built for heavy rain, hail and intense summer heat.

Most asphalt shingle roofs last 15 to 20 years. Most Greenville replacements are completed in 1 day, and installations are backed by up to a 10-year workmanship warranty.

  • Free roof inspection and estimate
  • Material selection
  • Expert installation
  • Clean-up and final check

Roof inspections

We check your roof from the inside out: shingles, flashing, ventilation, attic spaces and storm-related weaknesses. Then we show you photos of what we found and explain whether repair, maintenance or replacement is the best option.

  • Exterior roof check
  • Interior evaluation in the attic
  • Storm damage assessment
  • Clear recommendations

Roof maintenance

A strong roof doesn’t just happen. Routine maintenance stops leaks and moisture before they spread, can add years to the life of your roof, and reduces the risk of major repairs.

Roof tune-ups

A tune-up goes further than a yearly check-up: hands-on repairs and targeted sealing in a single service. Our standard roof tune-up starts at $640, which covers the majority of homes in the Greenville area.

  • Full roof inspection by a trained technician
  • Sealing exposed nail heads
  • Repairing loose shingles or tabs
  • Securing flashing and roof penetrations
  • Caulking around vents, pipes, chimneys and skylights
  • Debris removal from valleys
  • A detailed report of findings

Gutter services

Your gutters protect your roof, foundation and landscaping from water damage. We install durable, seamless gutters, repair leaks and sagging sections, and clean out leaves and debris so you can stop climbing ladders.

Skylight services

Leaky or foggy skylights can lead to water damage and lost efficiency. We install, repair and replace skylights, and we understand how they interact with your roofing system.
