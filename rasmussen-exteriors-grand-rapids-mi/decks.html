Decks

As an outdoor gathering spot for your family and friends, a deck is a great way to add value and function to your home.

Call 616.742.5110Email for a free quote

Wood decks

Traditional wood decks are a budget friendly option that, if maintained properly, can last for years. We offer staining services that will protect your deck against the elements.

Composite decks

We also install composite decking products, such as Trex, that are low maintenance alternatives next to wood. Trex offers a 25-year warranty and won’t rot, fade, stain, or need seasonal staining.

With many color and design options, including customizable railing systems, it is simple to build your dream deck.

What we install

  • Treated wood decks
  • Deck staining
  • Trex composite decking
  • Custom railing systems
