BAUM 714XLT Ultrafold, 2nd station

Compact 8-page right-angle, cross-fold and booklet folder

Runs on its own or with the 714XLT. Alone it makes letter, fan, double parallel and single folds, or works as a perf and score machine. In line with the 714 it makes 8-page signatures and right-angle folds for newsletters, brochures and documents sized for postal rules or envelopes, in one pass.

Pivoting idler rolls in the flow-thru design remove gap setting. Baumfolder calls it much needed equipment for the instruction and pharmaceutical market.

Tabletop folder accessories

Expand what a BAUM 714 can do

Sound cover (FK2003978 parallel, FK2003958 8-page): a large cut in noise, with easy access to the folder attachments.

Adjustable slitter shafts (FK2002600/50): micro-perforating by setting the fine-tooth blade depth. Fits select 714 XLT and 714 XA folders, not the right-angle folders.

714 XLT Ultrascore (343-105): for heavier stocks that curl in the folding rollers. The sheet runs straight through the Ultrascore assembly.

Micro perf kit (263-898-BG-01): a very fine perforation that tears clean. For the Perf/Score attachment and adjustable slitter shafts only.

Adjustable cart (55849-007): replaces the fixed cart under the 2nd station, so it can run in line or at a right angle with scoring, creasing, folding and printing machines.
