Dann and Julie Van Dyke

They have owned Cottage Works since the beginning, in April 2009. Five years as cottage owners and weekenders in Mason County, then 20 years living full time on Lost Lake in Custer and Hamlin Lake in Ludington, taught them a lot about lake living.

Dann is a mechanical engineer from Grand Valley State University, with 17 years in design, project and engineering management, and he holds his builder’s license.
