Things to do at camp

Weekly arts and crafts, special theme weekends, and plenty of onsite activities for the entire family.

Every week

  • Arts & crafts
  • Horseshoes
  • Playground
  • Volleyball
  • Board games
  • Heated pool
  • Boat rental
  • Movie night
  • Miles of hiking trails

ATV trails are for seasonal residents only.

Theme weekends

The campground’s last published events calendar (the 2024 season) gives the flavor:

  • ATV parade (Water Wars), campground BBQ and a band
  • Halloween ATV parade, costume contest and haunted trail
  • Meet & greet, cookie swap and tie-dye
  • Food trucks and field games day
  • Bingo, movie nights and pot luck dinners
  • Scavenger hunt and rock painting
  • Chili cook-off

Call 518-494-5555 for this season’s dates.

Around the area

Tin Teepee is 20 miles north of Lake George Village, The Great Escape and Splashwater Kingdom, and 60 miles south of Lake Placid.

  • Schroon Lake Chamber
  • Adirondack Museum
  • Fort Ticonderoga
  • Fort William Henry
  • Great Escape
  • Lake George Steamboat Co.
  • Natural Stone Bridge & Caves
  • Ausable Chasm
  • High Falls Gorge
  • Whiteface Mountain
  • Olympic Center & Museum
  • Santa’s Workshop North Pole
