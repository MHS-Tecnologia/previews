What’s the difference?

The good news is you don’t need to know. We do.

Remodeling
Changes the structure or form of a space, like a new floor plan. Right when a room doesn’t work for your life because it’s too tight or doesn’t flow.
Renovating
Restores a space or brings it up to current standard. Keep the kitchen’s layout, update it with new cabinets, fixtures or windows.

Keep the up-north feel

A renovation makes a space work in the modern world while keeping much of the feel of the original. A family home of many years, or a cabin you just bought that doesn’t suit you: we can make it a state-of-the-art home with its up-north character intact.

Updating a single room, like the kitchen, bathroom or living room, makes it more comfortable and adds to the resale value of the property.

Kitchens and baths first

Homeowners remodel kitchens and bathrooms more than any other rooms: the kitchen is where life happens. Whatever room you want to change, we’ve been remodeling and renovating area homes for four decades.
