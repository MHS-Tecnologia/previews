Boats in summer, Trail 15 in winter

The lodge sits on Snowmobile Trail 15, with an attached garage for your sleds and gear. In summer, everything for the water is already here.

  • A paddle boat and an aluminum fishing boat
  • 2 canoes and 3 sit-top kayaks, with safety vests
  • Bicycles and cross-country skis
  • Tennis and pickleball court, basketball hoop
  • A small sandy area for the kids, sand toys and a backyard firepit
