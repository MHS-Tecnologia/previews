Cabins and condos

Log cabin

The Cherokee or The Stuart

Sleeps up to 12 · 3 bedrooms · 2 baths

Two-story log cabins with two fireplaces, perfect for ski parties and large groups.

One private bedroom with a queen bed, a semi-private loft with a queen bed and a futon, and a master suite with a king. A sleep sofa in the living room and another in the master suite. Full kitchen, washer/dryer, open beam ceiling, whirlpool tub in the master suite, gas log fireplace in the living room, electric fireplace in the master suite, and a large front porch with rocking chairs.

Log cabin

The Elkhorn

Sleeps up to 8 · 2 bedrooms · 1 bath

A log cabin with a gas log fireplace and a large front porch with rocking chairs.

One private bedroom with a queen bed and a semi-private loft with a queen bed and a futon. Sleep sofa in the living room, full kitchen, open beam ceiling, gas log fireplace, large front porch with rocking chairs.

Log condo

Lodgement Condo

Sleeps up to 8 · 2 bedrooms · 1½ baths

A log condo with a stone fireplace and its own balcony.

One bedroom with two double beds, the other with a queen bed. 1½ baths, sleep sofa in the living room, full kitchen, stone fireplace, private balcony.

House cabin

The Cody House

Sleeps up to 7 · 1 bedroom

A house cabin on a wooded lot: plenty of privacy and all the comforts of home.

One bedroom with a single bed and a bunk bed, plus a sleep sofa and a Murphy bed in the living area. Full kitchen, brick fireplace, washer/dryer, large deck.

Weekender cabin

Weekender Cottage Duplex

Sleeps up to 6 · 2 bedrooms · 1 bath

Made for a weekend getaway. Two cottages to a building, each on one story.

Two bedrooms, each with a double bed, one bath, and a sleep sofa in the living area along with a kitchenette.
