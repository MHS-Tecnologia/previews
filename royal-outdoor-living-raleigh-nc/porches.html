Two screening systems

SCREENEZE

A sleek, minimal porch screen: durable screen fabric held taut by an aluminum frame. It keeps insects out while keeping your view and the airflow.

Eze-Breeze

Flexible vinyl panels you open, close or vent. It turns a screened porch into a three-season room in cooler months, with protection from wind, rain and pollen.

Why add one

  • More usable outdoor space for family and guests.
  • The outdoors without the bugs or the weather.
  • A safe, enclosed space for children and pets.
  • Curb appeal and value for your home.

Sunrooms

Their designers work with you on materials, rooflines and layout, so the new room looks like it belongs to the house. Royal handles every step, from permits to the final touches.

Questions they hear

Will I need a building permit?

In most cases a screened porch needs one. It depends on the location and scope, and Royal handles permits and inspections for every project.

How long does a screened porch take?

It depends on size, complexity and materials. On average, several weeks to a few months. You get a detailed timeline for your project.

Can I customize the design?

Yes. They work with you on a porch that suits your preferences and fits your home.

Sunroom or screened porch?

A sunroom is enclosed with glass and often insulated, protecting you from temperature extremes and pollen. A screened porch keeps the open air and keeps the bugs out.
