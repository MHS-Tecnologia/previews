Services / Timber harvesting

A harvest plan built around your woodland.

For over 30 years, private and commercial landowners have trusted Metzler's professional foresters to plan and carry out timber harvests for value, wildlife and recreation.

Request a site visit Call 717-667-2924

From the first walk to the last landing

For landowners looking to monetize the timber on their property, Metzler's foresters develop a customized plan that meets your specific wants and delivers maximum value.

Staff and crews are regularly trained in Sustainable Forestry Initiative (SFI) programs.

  1. Meet on siteAn on-site meeting to understand the goals you have for your timber and to review the process.
  2. Cruise the landForesters cruise the property to determine the species, health and quantity of the timber available.
  3. Plan the harvestA harvesting plan that produces a healthier forest and improves road access, with recommendations that use Metzler's market relationships to maximize your return.
  4. Retire the job siteEvery landing is seeded and your property returned to its pre-harvesting state, in as good or better condition than when the job began.
