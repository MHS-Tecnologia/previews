Thunderbird Gift Center

Whether you're looking for that perfect Copper Harbor keepsake, a sweatshirt to bundle up in front of the campfire or a book to learn about the history of the area, we've got you covered!

On the shelves

Thunderbird Gift Center features a large selection of copper and gem jewelry by local artists. We feature the largest selection of Isle Royale Greenstone Jewelry, our Michigan state Gem, beautiful Larimar and local gems and minerals.

Dig a little deeper and you'll find selected antiques, coins, & old bottles from the area.

  • Copper and gem jewelry
  • Isle Royale Greenstone Jewelry, our Michigan state Gem
  • Beautiful Larimar
  • Minnetonka Moccasins and sandals
  • Antiques, coins, & old bottles
  • Soaps and lotions
  • Quality clothing
  • Pocket & hunting knives
  • Bone feather jewelry
  • Notecards and prints from local artists
  • Children's toys and a complete local history book section

Metal detector rentals. We also have daily metal detector rentals available. Inquire at the Minnetonka Resort Office.
