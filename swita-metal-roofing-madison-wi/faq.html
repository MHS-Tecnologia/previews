Are metal roofs noisy?

Our metal roofs are not any noisier than an asphalt shingled roof, and most homeowners notice little to no difference. If you hear rain on your roof now, you will hear it with your metal roof, but at a slightly higher tone. We have yet to receive a complaint about rain noise with our roofs.

What does a metal roof cost?

Expect a quality metal roof to cost 2–3 times a quality asphalt shingled roof. For an average roof of 25–30 squares, that is about $25k–35k, and smaller roofs cost less. Your price depends on roof size, tear-off, steepness, underlayment, ventilation, the metal and style you choose, and more. We give you a guaranteed price once we have assessed and measured your roof.

Can it go over my existing roof?

Yes, as long as you have no more than one layer of asphalt shingles. If that layer is curling, crumbling, leaking or in disrepair, we advise a tear-off.

How does it handle hail?

Our steel roofs have passed Impact Testing with the highest possible rating of H-4. Built of investment-grade 24–28 gauge steel, they are extremely resistant to hail, even large hail.

How does it handle high winds?

Our roofs lock on all four sides and between panels, and they are anchored to the roof deck with stainless steel screws. Most metal roofs are guaranteed to withstand winds in excess of 130 mph.

How does a metal roof stay watertight?

The key is a hidden fastener system where the elements cannot break the fastener down. Interlocking panels, and snap lock systems for standing seam, hide all the screws and fasteners.

How long will it last?

Most metal roofs, properly installed, will last 40–60 years or more. With quality metal, installation and paint finishes, your metal roof will probably outlast the house.

Will it look shiny?

Certain colors can, especially black on a sunny day. There may be some gloss right after installation, but it soon dulls to a natural, flat look. Crinkle and textured finishes reduce glare even more.

Can I walk on it?

Yes, but you probably won’t need to. Our roofs are virtually maintenance-free and shed ice, snow and debris. Extreme caution is always advised on any metal roof, especially one that is wet, steep, or covered in snow and ice.

Can I expect energy savings?

Over the past 15 years, many of our customers have reported and documented energy cost savings of 25%–45%. Your actual savings will vary with your air conditioning use, your attic venting and your insulation.

How long does installation take?

The average metal roof takes 3–7 days, or slightly more if your roof is complex or very large.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes, if you own the home. If you need a quote for a home you may purchase, we charge a fee of $125 for that estimate.
