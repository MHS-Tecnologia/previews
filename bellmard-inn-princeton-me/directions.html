Driving

From Bangor

I-95 north to Route 9 at Bangor. Route 9 east to Baileyville, then left onto Route 1 north into Princeton. The Bellmard is on the left, next to the Baptist Church.

From Lincoln

I-95 north to Lincoln (exit 227). Turn right off the ramp onto the three-mile access road, take the second left onto Route 6 east, and stay on Route 6 through Lincoln to Topsfield. Turn right on Route 1 south toward Princeton and follow it 10 miles. The Bellmard is on the right, a bit after the bridge.

Flying

Bangor International Airport is about a two-hour drive.

Pilots

Private planes can land at the Princeton airport (FAA identifier PNN), a mile and a half from the inn, with two 4,000-foot runways and 100LL fuel around the clock with a major credit card. Let the inn know in advance and they will meet you and bring you over.

What’s in town

Princeton has two gas stations, a grocery store, a hardware store, a bank, a health care clinic, a post office, a restaurant and three take-out places, the municipal airport, public boat landings on Lewey and Pocomoonshine Lakes, and a public library for genealogy research and used books.

Within half an hour: Baileyville, Calais, Grand Lake Stream, Indian Township, Waite and the New Brunswick border. Eastport is an hour away and Houlton an hour and a half.
