Our Process

Designed to gather the right information to build you a new backyard with timeless beauty and lasting value, with your satisfaction in mind at every step.

Call (770) 452-3325

  1. Consultation

    It all starts with a phone conversation.

    We want to get to know you. Your goals, your wish list, your schedule and your budget. Together we’ll discuss what is important to you and set a clear expectation for the project.

  2. Original Design

    Custom designs built to perfection.

    We visit your property to gather detailed measurements, elevations, photographs and notes, then create a high-quality 3D conceptual plan. Once a design is approved, you receive a detailed proposal covering everything from pre-construction to your move into the new space.

  3. Permit Process

    We take care of permits so you don’t have to.

    Your construction drawings can run from 11 to 30 or more pages, depending on the project. Three different sets of eyes review the plans, then a third-party engineer firm approves and stamps them, and we take them to the permit office in your jurisdiction.

  4. Customization

    Customize and make it your own.

    Log into your client portal to make project selections such as material, color and finish. When you’re ready to begin, you sign the proposal digitally from the comfort of your home.

  5. Build

    Your dream backyard comes to life.

    With the permit in hand, we walk the project one last time before we break ground. Your schedule, selections and documents stay in the client portal while our master craftsmen bring the design to life.

Our promise

If you have any additional questions or concerns, we’ll work with you to resolve them quickly. Our number one priority is your satisfaction.
