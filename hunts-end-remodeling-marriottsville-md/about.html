Design first

At Hunt’s End the belief is that every space can benefit from proper design, which in turn improves everyday quality of life. That philosophy, together with a commitment to the highest level of customer service and quality of work, is why the company exists: to not compromise on those values.

Over years of practice Michael has built an excellent reputation among building professionals, many of whom are partners of Hunt’s End. Those relationships are how the company makes sure of quality workmanship on your project.

Michael Birner

Michael is a residential architect with over 25 years of remodeling experience: more than 17 years as vice president and architect of another remodeling firm, and now president and owner of Hunt’s End Remodeling. He is a graduate of the University of Colorado’s Environmental Design department.

Born in Baltimore, raised in Severna Park and a long-time resident of Howard County, Michael is a second-generation designer, the son of one of Maryland’s top kitchen designers. That background made applying an architect’s training to design/build remodeling a natural step.

He understands both what customers need and what construction requires, and enjoys the challenge of creating beautiful, useful spaces, often in ways many remodelers may not see.

“While importance is always placed on high quality, establishing a working relationship with the customer is critical to producing a stellar, and enjoyable, project.”Michael Birner, President, Architect

Credentials

Michael completed EPA Lead certification training and Green Builder Certification, and applies that knowledge to every project. Remember: the greenest home is the one you already own.

He is one of only 20 Certified Graduate Remodelers (CGR) in Maryland, a professional designation focused on the business management skills behind a professional remodeling operation.

REMODELING Big50

In 2017 Remodeling Magazine inducted Hunt’s End into the REMODELING Big50. Each year since 1986 the Big50 has named remodeling companies that set exceptionally high standards for professionalism and integrity through business practices, craftsmanship and impact on their community or the industry.

“We are honored to receive this distinction. The award recognizes excellence and leadership, and we are privileged to be named to this select group of remodelers.”Michael Birner, Owner
