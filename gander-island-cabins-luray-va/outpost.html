The Outpost

The stand-alone cabin, on a different property from the rest: a wooded lot close to river access points and hiking trails, just 10 miles from the town of Luray.

Inside is a fully equipped kitchen, a large living room with a beautiful stone fireplace, two full bathrooms and two private queen bedrooms.

Outside, a hot tub sits on the patio, with a picnic table, fire ring and charcoal grill in the yard. Shenandoah River Outfitters, for canoeing, kayaking and more, is close by.
