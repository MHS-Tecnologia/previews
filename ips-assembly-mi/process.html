What happens at each station

Descriptions come from the PCB assembly page on the IPS site. Stations it does not describe are listed by name only.

  1. New product introduction

    The order is received and the BOM process begins, starting with validation of every part number.

  2. Procurement, receiving & inspection

    Components are bought and received, with X-ray component counting machines and automated quantity logging into IPS's custom ERP.

  3. Kitting & programming

    All material is hand picked onto a dedicated job cart, with the board stencil in its own slot, and the cart goes to “Kit Ready”. Meanwhile the programming team loads the lines with the job: board size and placement positions.

  4. Feeder loading

    Smart production scheduling moves the cart to its SMT line, where component reels are loaded and bare boards go onto trays for paste.

  5. Screen printing

    Solder paste is applied automatically to the right side of the bare board.

  6. 3D solder paste inspection

    Built-in 3D cameras confirm the paste went on completely, with nothing in excess.

  7. Surface mount assembly

    Each high-speed SMT line is fed programming data with precise placement instructions.

  8. First article inspection

    The first piece off the line is verified by two machine operators against the supplied BOM, checking placements and polarities.

  9. Reflow, oven profiling

    Oven profiles are created for a consistent reflow at your volume.

  10. Feeder unloading

  11. Automated optical inspection

    Every board goes through 3D AOI with high-shutter cameras, to catch what the eye cannot see.

  12. X-ray inspection

    The assembly runs through the X-ray machine to confirm BGA connections and proper soldering.

  13. In-process inspection

  14. Solder: wave, selective, manual

  15. De-panel

  16. Conformal coating

    Semi- and fully-automated coating, applied by the conformal coating team.

  17. Box build

    Custom box builds, including OEM. IPS can source the components beyond the PCB to assemble the box. Box builds

  18. Testing: functional, in-circuit

    Each piece is tested before it is finalized: functional testing for a 100% pass rate, or in-circuit testing per customer requirements.

  19. Final inspection & finished goods

    IPC-certified final inspectors check the product before it moves to finished goods, held in the climate-controlled facility or sent to packaging.

  20. Packaging

    The packaging team picks the most cost-effective protection, including ESD bubble wrap or bags. Customer-supplied components are counted and packed for return.

  21. Shipping

    Hand delivery to your team in Metro Detroit, pickup at the Livonia facility, or UPS.
