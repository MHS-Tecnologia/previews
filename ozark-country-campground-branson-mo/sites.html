Campsite information

Every site is a full hook-up with water, sewer, electric and cable TV. Choose shade, open sky for your satellite, or a spot by the meadow.

Call to reserve (417) 334-4681Site lengths on the park map

The A and B sides

The sites on the A Side are exceptionally shady. Each site is a full hook-up with water, electric, sewer and cable TV, and 50 amp service sites are available.

The A Side stretches back to the B Section, where you will find 50 amp pull-thru sites in the trees.

Satellite is not available in this section because of the trees.

The G side

If you have a satellite dish, request a G side site to receive your signal. Sites in the G section are all 50 amp pull-thrus without trees.

The grassy meadow

The grassy meadow surrounds the laundry and bathhouse, the swimming pool and the pavilion.

You are welcome to exercise your pets here. All we ask is that they are on a leash at all times, and that you clean up after them so the next campers can also enjoy the meadow.

Before you book

  • All sites are full hook-ups: water, sewer, 30 or 50 amp electric, cable TV and free high speed wifi.
  • Sorry, no tent sites, and no tents allowed on any site.
  • Open year round. A long term section for the year or the season is open all the time.
  • Discount coupons are handed out at check-in.
