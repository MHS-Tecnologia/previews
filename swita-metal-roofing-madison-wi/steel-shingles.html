Which profile suits your roof?

A rule of thumb: lower pitched roofs look better with lower profile shingles, and steeper roofs with higher profiles. Mike will help you pick what looks best on your home.

KasselWood and KasselShake

KasselWood is a low-profile steel shingle in a variety of colors that can reproduce the look of cedar, slate or standard shingles. Its low weight means it can often go over your existing roof, adding a layer of insulation.

KasselShake is the high-profile choice, with the thick, random look of hand-split shakes. Its interlocking panels can withstand winds up to 120 miles per hour. Both are made from American-made steel.

Centura

Low-profile shingles for homeowners who want a look that fits in with the neighborhood. Centura shingles will never rot, warp or become waterlogged, and they are engineered to withstand high winds, resist hail and shed snow.

Every 100% American-made Centura roof carries a Lifetime Limited Warranty, transferable for up to 40 years after installation, plus a 30-year fade and chalk warranty.

EDCO

Medium-profile steel shingles that resemble slate or shake, with just the right amount of texture. EDCO has served homeowners since 1946, and its products are backed by a lifetime, non-prorated limited warranty.
