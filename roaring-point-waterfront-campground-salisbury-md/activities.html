Things to do

Fishing, crabbing, swimming and relaxing are the favorites here, with entertainment on most weekends.

On the water

  • Fishing. A Maryland license is required.
  • Crabbing. A license may be required.
  • Sandy beaches and swimming along the Nanticoke River.

Licenses: Maryland DNR, toll-free in Maryland 1-877-620-8DNR; out of state 410-260-8DNR (8367), weekdays 8:30 to 5.

Around camp

  • Horseshoes, basketball, volleyball and croquet
  • On most weekends: crafts, bingo, tournaments, live music and a good old-fashioned hayride
