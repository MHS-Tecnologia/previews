What to expect from training

Hands-on experience

Learn by doing: Flow-Liner® equipment, CIPP liners, and Neofit®+Plus EPP for pressure pipe.

Expert instruction

Proven techniques, troubleshooting and real-world know-how for sewer, drain and pressure pipe projects.

Comprehensive coverage

Straight runs, bends, multiple pipe diameters and challenging jobsite conditions.

Business and ROI

How to estimate, bid and sell lining and EPP projects as a high-margin, in-demand service.

Quality and safety

Every job meets industry requirements and delivers long-lasting results your customers can trust.

Two ways to train

On-site training

We come to your location and work directly with your crew, on your equipment and real projects.

In-house training

Join us at Flow-Liner’s facility in Zanesville, Ohio for an immersive, structured learning environment.

After training

Certified Installers get ongoing technical support, access to our CIPP systems and the ability to offer Neofit®+Plus EPP, for both gravity and pressure pipes. Financing is available for installers.
