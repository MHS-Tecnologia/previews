Hike, bike, fish, swim, hunt agates, canoe, kayak, storm watch

And commune with Lake Superior. Schroeder sits midway between Duluth and Thunder Bay, near Tofte and Lutsen, so the whole North Shore is within reach.

Call 218-663-7292

Things to do

Hike

Superior Hiking Trail: take the spur trail up the Cross River to reach the main trail, then head northeast to the Temperance River or southwest to the Cramer Road.

Temperance River State Park is one mile away, one of the most popular hiking areas on the North Shore. Sugar Loaf Cove, eight miles southwest, has interpretive trails, a cobblestone beach and 1.1 billion year old lava flows.

Further: Tettegouche (20 miles southwest, with the highest waterfall in Minnesota), Cascade River (20 miles), Judge C. R. Magney and its Devil's Kettle (45 miles), and Gooseberry Falls on your way up from Duluth.

Bike

The paved Gitchi Gami State Trail runs from Schroeder, at Lamb's, to Lutsen through Temperance River State Park, about 12 miles.

The Britton Peak mountain bike trails are 7 miles up the Sawbill Trail, with single track for beginners and advanced riders.

Fish

Throw a line in the lake or the river for a chance at salmon, trout, walleyes and northern pike.

Lamb's has a map of lakes and streams within 20 miles and can tell you where to fish from shore and where you'll need a boat. The Taconite Harbor boat launch is 2 miles away.

Swim

Local swimming holes, some safe for smaller children: ask at the office.

Lake Superior: brrrrrrr, but folks jump in, usually older kids on a dare. Grand Marais has an indoor pool, 30 miles away.

Hunt agates

Any of the Lake Superior beaches can yield treasures.

Canoe and kayak

Some folks put in right off Lamb's beaches. There is a small public landing half a mile away at Father Baraga's.

Canoes, kayaks and bikes can be rented 4 miles up the road in Tofte, or on the edge of the BWCA on Sawbill Lake. BWCA day permits are free and self-issued at the entry point.

Storm watch

The biggest waves on this shore come with strong, sustained winds from the east. Pay attention if you want to catch them.

Shop and eat

The Schroeder Baking Company is within walking distance, with coffee, milkshakes, pizza, wraps and scones. The Cross River Heritage Center next door has a window on years gone by, and a gift shop.

Lamb's own gift shop has sweatshirts, mugs and more. Groceries, gas and an outfitter are 4 miles northeast in Tofte; Grand Marais is a beautiful 30-mile drive.
