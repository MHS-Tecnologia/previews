From die designs in 1994 to a 20,000 sq. ft. shop.

Dave Berger started Direct Tooling Concepts in Versailles as a sub-contract die design source. Direct Wire Service followed in 2001 with one wire EDM, cutting the tooling DTC designed. In 2018 the two became one business, and its ISO 9001:2015 certification covers the services of both.
