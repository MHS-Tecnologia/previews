Your base in the North Country

Although your days will be near filled with the fishing Black Lake has to offer, Black Lake is just minutes away from many North Country attractions.

The 1,000 Islands

Visit the 1,000 Islands region on the mighty St Lawrence River, aka “The River”. It is famous for its scenic views, boating and fishing. Take a boat tour of not only the American side of “The River” but the Canadian side as well. Some can't-misses are the Boldt and Singer Castles.

Museums

  • The Antique Boat Museum in Clayton, with its display of hundreds of locally built wooden boats
  • The Frederic Remington Art Museum in Ogdensburg
  • The Morristown and Hammond Museums, on the diverse history of the region

More to see

  • Huge lakers and ocean going freighters passing through the Eisenhower Locks in Massena
  • Gouverneur, the birthplace of LifeSaver candies
  • Gift and antique shops with the work of local artists and craftspeople
  • The Adirondack Mountains, a short drive away
  • Two casinos within an hour's drive: the Akwesasne Casino, just north of Massena, and the Charity Casino in Gananoque, Ontario

We will make every effort to ensure that your holiday is one that will have lasting memories.
