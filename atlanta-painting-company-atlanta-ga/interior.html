Interior questions

What type of paint is best for high-traffic areas?

Hallways, kitchens and living rooms benefit from durable, washable paints with higher sheen levels, such as satin or semi-gloss. They are easier to clean and resist scuffs better than flat paints.

How do you protect my furniture and flooring?

Drop cloths, plastic coverings and masking protect furniture, floors and fixtures. Furniture is carefully moved or grouped, and floors are protected with layered materials.

Can painting increase my home's value?

A fresh coat can improve your home's appearance and make it more appealing to buyers. Fresh interior and exterior finishes can also make a home more move-in ready.
