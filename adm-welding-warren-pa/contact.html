What to include in a bridge request

  1. Company or organization, contact name, phone
  2. Bridge length
  3. Minimum width
  4. Capacity: describe the vehicle or equipment as well as the weight, e.g. “Three-axle articulated dump, weighing 70 tons”
  5. Description of use, e.g. “bypass for public traffic during bridge replacement project” or “contractor access for pipeline construction project”
  6. Bridge type: permanent, temporary, or both/unsure
  7. If temporary: rental, purchase, or both
  8. If renting: how soon the bridge is needed
