Two families from St. Charles, Illinois

Glenn and Judy Mastalski and Sarah and Rob Klemm are the proud owners of Lake View Lodge. After 20-plus years of vacationing in the Boulder Junction area, they are thrilled to have a place to call their own, and to share with you.

Glenn and Judy are the parents of Sarah, Rachel and Michael. Sarah and Rob were married in July 2011 and have two children, Kaylee and Jon. Both families live in St. Charles, Illinois.

“We look forward to making your vacation the best it can be!”
The Mastalskis and the Klemms

What has changed since 2009

  1. 2009

    The Mastalski and Klemm families buy the lodge in the spring and fully renovate it.

  2. 2015

    The Tree Bathroom is completely renovated.

  3. 2016

    The lodge is painted.

  4. 2017

    A new back deck, with room for a grill and an eating area looking out into the woods.

  5. 2018

    New queen mattresses, and new pillows, sheets, comforters and blankets for every bed.

  6. 2019

    New dock palettes and a newer entrance to the dock. A new fridge and microwave.

  7. 2020

    The Bear Bathroom is completely renovated. A gas grill, new deck chairs, a picnic table behind the house, and a new washer and dryer.

  8. 2022

    New dishes for the kitchen, and new dressers for the Fish and Moose Bedrooms.

  9. 2023

    New couches for the upstairs living room.

  10. 2025

    The parking area is resurfaced, a new kayak arrives, and pickleball lines are painted on the tennis court.
