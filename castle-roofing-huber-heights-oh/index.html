Questions we hear

Are your estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free, and we offer same-day estimates.

How long does my installation take?

Most installations take one day.

Do you take credit cards?

Yes, and we don’t charge fees. We also take cash, checks, American Express, Discover, MasterCard and Visa.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available through Synchrony.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We work with insurance companies and help with the claim and its coordination, which is common after storm damage.

Should I repair my roof or replace it?

We start with a detailed inspection that looks past surface damage: decking condition, flashing, ventilation and the age of the material. Then we tell you whether a repair will really extend the roof’s life or whether a replacement is the better long-term value.

How often should I have the outside of my home inspected?

Once a year, and after severe weather. Annual inspections catch small problems on the roof, gutters and siding before they become costly repairs.
