Who they are

In 1987 Don and Sue Moser started Moser Roofing and have not looked back. In the 38 years since, it has become one of the longest standing roofing contractors in the Mid Willamette Valley.

“Serve people well, and the rest will follow.”

They measure success not by the number of roofs installed, but by doing the right thing regardless of the situation. At Moser Roofing, you are not just another project: you are a neighbor, a friend, and someone they are honored to serve.

Professional excellence

Each roof is inspected by one of their roofing experts on completion, and they will not ask for payment until both they and the customer are satisfied with the work.

Servant-hearted approach

They listen first, serve with humility, and treat your home as if it were their own.

“There is more to a roof than shingles and nails. It’s protection for the people and memories beneath it.” Every full roof installation is backed by a lifetime workmanship warranty. Simply put: they stand behind their work.

Get a free estimate

For a free estimate or to schedule a consultation, contact Moser Roofing. They serve Salem and the greater Willamette Valley.
