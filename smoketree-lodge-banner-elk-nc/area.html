The High Country, minutes away

Enjoy the peace and solitude of the Blue Ridge Mountains, or the many activities the High Country offers.

Everything on the post

If you're a lover of the great outdoors, take in the hiking trails that are literally within walking distance of Smoketree Lodge.

Trout fishing in the streams just down the road, horseback riding, championship golf and whitewater rafting. Canoeing and rock climbing, too.

Within minutes of all of the High Country's snow ski resorts, Grandfather Mountain, Blowing Rock and Linville Caverns.
