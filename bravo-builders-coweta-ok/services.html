Our services

We design each part of construction to meet the needs of each client. Whatever it takes, Bravo Builders is here for you.

Preconstruction

Precision planning for project success

During preconstruction, Bravo Builders works with the design team and the owner’s team to make sure cost, constructability and scheduling are addressed during the critical decision-making stage of design, which leads to a smooth construction phase.

We develop a feasible, cost-effective budget through value analysis, constructability reviews, tailored bid packages and phasing plans. As a team, we identify the challenges and define the solutions early, rather than meeting costly issues in the field.

Design/Build

Streamlining your vision

The design-build delivery system puts the client in the driver’s seat. Working in tandem with the architect from the outset, Bravo Builders can design and build your facility in a shorter time than traditional construction methods.

Because construction runs alongside design, we work with the owner and designers to define the function, budget and schedule as the drawings are developed. As your single point of contact, we help you streamline the process with a sound plan and a solid budget.

Construction management

Your goals are our priority

As construction managers, Bravo Builders uses specialized project management techniques to oversee the planning, design and construction of a project, from its beginning to its end. Our experienced specialists oversee:

  • Scheduling
  • Safety
  • Cost estimating
  • Design
  • Quality assurance
  • Value engineering
  • Commissioning
  • Construction inspection
  • Risk management

Our priority is to protect our clients’ goals by controlling a project’s time, cost and quality, managing all project risks under one contract.

General contracting

Traditionally competitive

General contracting remains one of the most efficient ways to complete a project. With this traditional approach we have delivered high quality facilities to happy customers time after time, building a loyal base of repeat clients.

Whether in the open bid market or as a select general contractor, Bravo Builders combines competitive pricing with exceptional service and management, while keeping the highest standards of quality.
