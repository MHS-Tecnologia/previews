Plan your visit.

The house is open during restoration. Enter through Manuel Plaza, at 35 East 4th Street. Open Wednesday to Sunday; closed major holidays.

Wednesday to Sunday, 1 to 5 p.m. Last entry 4:30. No reservations: buy tickets at the door. Plan on 45 to 60 minutes, with the printed booklet or the digital guide on the Bloomberg Connects app. Docents answer questions.

Wednesday to Sunday. The period rooms and the rear garden with a guide, 60 to 75 minutes. Reservations are strongly recommended, through the museum’s online ticketing; walk-ins join if space allows.

Getting here

29 East Fourth Street, New York, NY 10003, between Lafayette Street and Bowery, bordering Greenwich Village, the East Village and SoHo.

Subway
N or R to 8th Street. 6 to Astor Place. B, D, F, M to Broadway/Lafayette.
Bus
M103 to 4th Street. M1, M2, M3 to 4th Avenue and 8th Street.
Parking
None at the museum. Street parking, meters and commercial lots nearby, including Icon Parking, 445 Lafayette Street, and Edison ParkFast, 375 Lafayette Street.

Accessibility

The tour climbs four floors: 72 steps inside, plus 7 steps to enter. There is no elevator, the house is not wheelchair-accessible, and it is not air-conditioned. Modern chairs are in most rooms.

Blind or low vision

Docents describe the rooms and answer questions. A large-print tour is available. Sighted guides come in free, and service dogs are welcome.

Deaf or hard of hearing

The written self-guided tour is always available, and docents will answer questions in writing. Sign language interpreters come in free.

House rules

Large bags, backpacks and shopping bags are checked at the admissions desk. No smoking, no pets. Groups of 10 or more must book a private visit.

Questions about access: nyc1832@merchantshouse.org or 212-777-1089.
