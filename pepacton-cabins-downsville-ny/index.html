Log cabins on the East Branch of the Delaware River

Five log cabins, log cabin lodges and a loft on Hood Lane in Downsville, NY. The lawn runs down to the riverbank.

Measured from the cabin door

The trout water starts on the lawn. Everything else a fisherman, hunter or hiker comes for is within ten miles.
