“Where to start? The staff was friendly and helpful. The room was clean and comfortable and had a private balcony with a drop-dead gorgeous view. The breakfast was a lot better than most places I have stayed. And the hot tub (that my back loves) has to be the best of anyplace I have ever stayed! We are definitely coming back.”

David K

“The Island Resort more than exceeded our expectations. … we did not realize we were sitting right on the bay and the views were breathtaking! The room was spacious and immaculate! The atmosphere at this place.... truly the best of both worlds - Resort amenities with a Bed and Breakfast charm. … So close to all the attractions and downtown - but we barely left the resort grounds!”

Kymberly Kitao

“Loved our stay here! Location was amazing, close proximity to activities but far enough away from some of the busy areas; perfect if you are looking to relax a bit! … Room was spacious and clean and had a beautiful view of the bay. Megan Welby
