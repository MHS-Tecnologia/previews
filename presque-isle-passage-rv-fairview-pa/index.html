Fairview, PA. Formerly Hill’s Family Campground.

RV sites, tent sites and over 30 rentals, 500 feet off I‑90.

Exit 18, seven miles from the Presque Isle beaches on Lake Erie. Pull-thru sites for any size RV, cabins, yurts, tiny homes and one houseboat. Pets welcome.

From the entrance to the pond

Every rental has its own spot in the park. This is the order you meet them in, driving in off Sterrettania Road.

  1. At the entranceThe office and store, laundry and firewood. The Apartment, the Farmhouse, the Studio, the playground and the Cottage.
  2. Past the poolDeluxe Cabins 1 to 7, Cabins 1 and 2, and the Landshark houseboat, along the side of the campground.
  3. Through the middleRows of RV sites: full hookup, full hookup buddy sites, monthly and seasonal.
  4. At the backThe Pavilion, Park Models 1 to 5, RV rentals 1 to 3, Cabins 3 to 9, the Lodge, both yurts, the tent area and the pond.
Pick a rental on the map

One of the rentals is a houseboat

The Landshark is a houseboat placed on a site and rented like any other cabin. It sleeps up to 4: a futon, a table that folds into a bed and two twin bunks, plus a kitchen with a stove, microwave and small refrigerator.

Half bath only, no shower. It sits along the side of the campground past the pool.

See where the Landshark sits

A site for any size RV

With renovations over the past years, there’s a site for any size RV, even with a towed vehicle (65 feet or longer). Most sites are pull-thrus, with 30 and 50 amp options. Full hookups include electric, water, sewer and Wi‑Fi, and every site has a picnic table and a fire ring.

Tent campers get several wooded, open field areas, rented first come, first served.
