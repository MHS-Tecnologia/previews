What dental implants are

Titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. Once the implant integrates with the bone, a custom-made crown is attached.

Implant options

Replace one missing tooth with a single implant and crown.
Implant-supported bridges
Replace several consecutive missing teeth with an implant-supported bridge.
Full-arch / All-on-4
Restore an entire arch of teeth using just four strategically placed implants.

The implant process

  1. Consultation and treatment planning

    A comprehensive examination, including digital X-rays and a 3D scan of your jaw. Together we create a treatment plan tailored to your goals and budget.

  2. Implant placement

    The titanium post is placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure, under local anesthesia. If needed, bone grafting may be performed at this stage.

  3. Healing and osseointegration

    Over the next 3 to 6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone. A temporary restoration may be provided during this period.

  4. Final restoration

    An abutment is attached and a custom-made crown, matched to the color and shape of your surrounding teeth, is placed on top.

Cost and financing

The cost varies with the number of implants, whether bone grafting is required, and the type of restoration. We offer financing through Cherry Financing and accept most dental insurance plans.
