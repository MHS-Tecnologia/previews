One plant for the board, the harness and the box.

Come at any stage of the project. IPS builds turnkey, low and high volume, high mix, and puts the board, its cabling and its enclosure together under one roof.

  • PCB assembly

    Surface mount, thru-hole and mixed technology, inspected to IPC-A-610 Class 2 and 3.

    PCB assembly capabilities
    • Fine pitch components, down to 12 mil
    • BGA and micro-BGA packages
    • Double-sided and intrusive reflow
    • Wave, selective and hand soldering
    • Lead-free (RoHS) and water-soluble
    • Standard 24-hour turn for prototypes

  • Cable & wire harness

    Point-to-point cables, multi-branch harnesses and wire-to-PCB, built to IPC/WHMA-A-620 by a dedicated harness team.

    Harness capabilities
    • Automated wire cut, strip and crimp
    • Connector overmolding
    • EMI shielded cable builds
    • MIL-SPEC wiring
    • Serialization and full traceability
    • Same-day and quick-turn prototypes

  • Box build & integration

    PCB assemblies, harnesses, power and I/O integrated into enclosures and sub-assemblies, functionally tested and packaged.

    Box build capabilities
    • Custom enclosures
    • System integration
    • Functional testing
    • Packaging with quality reports

Testing & inspection

3D solder paste inspection, 3D AOI, X-ray and functional test.

Repair & rework

Replacing BGAs, connectors, microprocessors, LEDs and ICs, and finding the root cause of failures.

Design support & DFM

A designated PCB design team reviews layouts to cut failures before the first build.

Quick-turn prototypes

Quantities of 1, 5 or 25, built on the production lines with full inspection.
