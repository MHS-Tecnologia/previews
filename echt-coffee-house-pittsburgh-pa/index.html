Liquor Lab and Tasting Room

Stop by at the right moment and you can watch the distilling. The liquor and cocktails are served upstairs in the lounge; the tasting room is available for events.

The Liquor Lab

Roasted here, in small batches

ECHT slow-roasts single-origin coffee beans in small batches on a fluid-bed roaster, for precise heat and temperature control. The baristi work the grind, brewing temperature, flow and pressure for every extraction.

Take-home beans, roasted in-house: 500g bags, freshly ground if you like, $22.
