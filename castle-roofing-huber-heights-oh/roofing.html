From the first look to the final inspection

Every job starts with a free, same-day estimate. We look past the surface damage: the decking, the flashing, the ventilation and the age of the material. Then we tell you plainly whether a repair will do or the roof needs replacing.

We recommend a full tear-off over laying shingles on top of an old roof, for the best result. We work with your insurance company when the damage came from a storm, and we back our work with 5-year and 10-year labor warranties.

When you call, ask about our CertainTeed SureStart PLUS 4-Star roofing warranty.

Signs it may be time for a new roof

  • Your roof is over 20 years old
  • Shingles are curling, cracking or missing
  • There are granules in your gutters
  • You see water damage in the attic
  • Your energy bills keep going up

New roofs we install

Asphalt shingles

The most popular roof for homes here, in many colors and styles. With good installation they last 20 to 30 years.

  • 3-tab
  • Architectural
  • Impact-resistant
  • Energy-efficient cool roof

Cedar shake

A natural, rustic look and a long life, when it is installed with the right techniques. We source high-quality shakes.

Metal

Durable and energy-efficient, with a modern look. Standing seam and metal shingle systems, installed watertight.

TPO flat roofs, for homes and businesses

For flat and low-slope roofs: a reflective, energy-efficient membrane that resists UV, chemicals and punctures. We install, repair and service commercial roofs.
