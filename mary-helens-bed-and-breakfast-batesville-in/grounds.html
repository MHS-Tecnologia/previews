The grounds and the loft

Spectacular views throughout the year, and plenty of secluded spots to sit and enjoy nature at its best, from chairs on a sandy beach to swings under the trees.

Outdoors

Listen to the birds sing and the fountain raining down on the pond. Sit on the front porch with your favorite book to the tranquil sounds of the garden pond. No matter where you end up, you won’t be disappointed with the peace that will surround you.

Three acres of professionally landscaped ground, maintained by Jim.

The loft and convenience room

Set aside to make your stay more comfortable. Take a seat at the antique roll top desk, in the family for generations. Help yourself to soft drinks, juices and bottled water from the stocked mini fridge, and to chips, crackers, popcorn and fresh home-baked goods. Morning coffee and tea are there for early risers, with books and DVDs for your entertainment.
