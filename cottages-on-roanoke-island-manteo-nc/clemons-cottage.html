The cottage

Years ago, the cottage was moved from the nearby beach to Manteo, where it served as home to many of the original families on Roanoke Island. Now it is an enchanting private cottage for visitors to the island.

Surrounded by picket fences and arbors, the cottage’s private garden is a place to relax, think back on the day and plan tomorrow. Everything in Manteo is a step away: the boardwalk on the waterfront, restaurants, shops, art galleries, jewelers, potters and museums.

The beaches are only minutes away. Well-maintained bikes make it easy to explore the bike trails around Roanoke Island and the attractions an easy ride away.

Policies

Check-in and occupancy
  • Check-in is after 4:00. Check-out is before 10:00.
  • Maximum occupancy 2.
  • Neither parties nor events are allowed.
Payment and cancellation
  • 50% of reserved dates is due at the time of reservation. The balance is paid 30 days before arrival.
