Things to do

The High Country of Western North Carolina is one of the most popular outdoor destinations in the Southeast. We sit between four ski slopes, several golf courses and countless hiking trails.

Next door

Fun ’n Wheels Family Race Park

A go-kart race track right next to our motel, with an arcade, a small Ferris wheel for children, bumper cars and a gyro. Open daily from Memorial Day to Labor Day, weather permitting.

Along the Blue Ridge Parkway

Hiking

The Parkway makes the trails easy to reach, from multi-day backpacking trips to easy day hikes. Moses H. Cone and Julian Price Memorial Park are a favorite: the Boone Fork Loop Trail, the Tanawha Trail and the carriage trails.

For something gentler, the Lee & Vivian Reynolds Greenway Trail near the Watauga County Recreation Complex is a 3-mile paved path for walking or jogging.

A mile to twenty miles

Golf

Nine golf courses lie within twenty miles of Boone. Willow Creek, a nine-hole course, is just a mile down Highway 105. Boone Golf Club is minutes down the road, and Red Tail Mountain is just across the Tennessee line in Mountain City.

Four slopes nearby

Skiing

Appalachian Ski Mountain, Ski Beech, Hawksnest and Sugar Mountain. Skiing, snowboarding, tubing and ice skating, so everyone in the family finds something on the snow. Our big log cabins were made for ski parties.

Only minutes from Boone

Grandfather Mountain

One of North Carolina’s most famous ridges, a wildlife preserve and a hiker’s paradise, with trails from gentle paths to backcountry hikes. Its wildlife habitats hold cougars, bald eagles, black bears and river otters you can watch from above and below the water.

In the mountains

Tweetsie Railroad

The historic Number 12 train takes park-goers through the mountains every day, with a deer park, rides for thrill-seekers and shops on site.

In Boone

Appalachian State University

Nearly every home game at The Rock sells out, so get tickets early. The university also hosts concerts, plays and the Appalachian Summer Festival.
