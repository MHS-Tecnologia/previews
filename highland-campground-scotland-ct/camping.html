Sites & facilities

At Highland our 90 acres provide a natural forest camping environment, yet we also provide you with modern facilities.

What every stay includes

  • Spacious sites116
  • Natural forest90 acres
  • Water and electric hookups20 and 30 amp
  • Fire ringson all sites
  • Swimming pool26′ × 50′
  • Stocked fishing pond
  • Recreation center with free WiFi40′ × 80′
  • Clean rest roomsfree hot showers
  • Playground, dog park, laundry facilities

Plenty to do

There’s plenty to do at Highland Campground with planned activities every weekend. We bring families together through a variety of sports, games, entertainment, and other planned activities.

Or perhaps you would enjoy sitting around a campfire, fishing our stocked pond or a dip in the pool.

The campground map also marks horseshoe pits, volleyball and badminton, shuffleboard, bocci, a rec field, hiking paths and a covered bridge.
