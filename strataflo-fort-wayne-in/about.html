Our story

  1. 1939Strataflo becomes known for its innovative and durable molded rubber poppet, which seals better and lasts longer than O-rings.
  2. The metal poppetA precision-machined metal poppet solves the problem with metal-to-metal seals, and succeeds in oil, gas and steam.
  3. TodayMore than 80 years of precise manufacturing and rigorous testing, still refining through state-of-the-art technology.
