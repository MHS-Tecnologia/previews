Answers from Byler’s own FAQ.

How long do the jars last?

Up to three years unopened on the shelf. After opening, keep them in the fridge, and for the best flavor use them within 6 to 8 weeks.

When will my order arrive?

Orders ship within 1 business day by UPS Home Delivery, which takes 3 to 4 days.

Byler products are in over 500 specialty food stores. See where to buy.
