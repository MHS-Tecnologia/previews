Eat, drink and sleep in Newberry, all under one roof

Zellar's Village Inn is a restaurant, a full-bar lounge and lodging at 7552 M-123, in Michigan's Upper Peninsula. Home cooked food and fresh pies, open year-round, seven days a week.

Three doors, one building

Open year-round, Z's welcomes snowmobilers, hunters, fishing groups, families, organizations and tour buses.

The restaurant

Breakfast, lunch and dinner, with dishes for seniors, vegetarians, children and big appetites. Homemade pasties, broasted chicken and Lake Superior whitefish.

Hours, specials and the full menu

Z's Lounge

Full bar, specialty drinks and beer, noon to 2 am. Seating for 75 to 100, a dance floor, pool, and live bands on some nights.
