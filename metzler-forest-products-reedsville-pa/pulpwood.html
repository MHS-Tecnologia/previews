Services / Pulpwood purchasing

A steady market for your pulpwood.

Metzler buys pulpwood and firewood poles at the Reedsville yard. Call for current species and pricing, and call before you bring your first load.

Call 717-667-2924

At the yard

  • An unattended scale system for pulpwood deliveries
  • A well-maintained yard and trained staff, for safe unloading and quick truck turnaround
  • Prompt weekly payments for large-volume pulpwood and firewood poles

Deliveries

Monday to Friday6:30 am – 5 pm
WeekendsNo deliveries

Loads hauled Monday to Friday are paid the following Monday. The driver needs to give the correct business name and mailing address for the payment.

Call before your first load

All material, and the log yard rules
  • Reasonably free of mud
  • Properly trimmed
  • Reasonably straight, free of crook and excessive sweep
  • Free of fire damage and not charred
  • Free of excessive punk
  • All trucks swept off before they leave the log yard
  • All truck drivers wear a hard hat in the log yard

Prices and specifications are subject to change without notice.
