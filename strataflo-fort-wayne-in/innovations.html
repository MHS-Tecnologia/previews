The Strataflo difference is inside

Three parts work as one system: a molded poppet, a seat that lets it open wide, and a cast guide called the Blade.

The poppet

More than 80 years ago, Strataflo developed a rubber poppet that eliminated the need for O-rings. The patented design fully molds rubber over a solid steel insert, a strong seal that has proven itself ever since.

It is the defining innovation in the company's history, and it is used in every standard check and foot valve, including the Strataflo Fusion™ line.

The Blade™

A cast feature in select standard check valves. Valves commonly have a guide; the Blade also improves flow efficiency by using less material in the cross section, and adds metal where it matters, at the center guide.

That keeps the valve strong and the poppet stable, especially in Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) applications.

The seat

The seat and poppet system lets the seal open wide even in low-flow conditions, and stay stable all the way to maximum flow.

By design the poppet instantly opens away from the seat, so low flow cannot damage it. As flow increases, the cast bar guides the poppet up to full capacity. That is why Strataflo valves have been VFD compatible since long before VFD was invented.

Then a metal poppet, for oil, gas and steam

The rubber poppet earned the respect of military engineers, pump manufacturers and well professionals. Strataflo then designed a precision-machined metal poppet to solve the problems of metal-to-metal seals, and it became a success in oil, gas and steam applications.
