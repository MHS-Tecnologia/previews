Rooms

Our guest rooms are individually decorated and appointed with period antiques. Each room has its own private bath. The Tea Rose Room is on the main floor.

Call 1-800-815-0250Email the inn

Regency Room

King bedWhirlpool tub and shower/bath

This elegant room was once the original master bedroom/bath when the home was renovated in 1909. It has the original wardrobe closet and several original bathroom fixtures. It is a large, light-filled room with an ornate iron bed, fireplace (non-working) and views of the garden. There is also a view of the Cape Fear River from an adjacent porch.

Wedgewood Room

King bedClaw-foot tub and shower/bath

Shades of blue and yellow mix with white to create a classic look in this spacious room with a view of the garden. There is also a view of the Cape Fear River from an adjacent porch. This room, filled with the afternoon sunlight, features a handsome, four-poster cherry bed, antique English armoire and dresser, and two cozy reading chairs.

Heritage Room

King bedShower/bath

Richly decorated in colors of terra cotta, cream and blue, this large luxurious corner room is appointed with a New Orleans-style iron “gate” bed, cozy sitting area and antique gentlemen’s writing desk, all of which create an exotic, worldly feel.

Carolina Room

Queen bedShower/bath

This charming second-floor room features an ornate iron bed and a unique staircase that leads to the bathroom. A crewel-work loveseat in the bay window makes a cozy reading corner.

Indochine Room

Queen bedShower/bath

Inspired by the beauty and grace of the Orient, this corner room is bright and sunny. A four poster cherry bed, Victorian reading chairs and hues of yellow and red are all warm and inviting.

Tea Rose Room

Queen bedShower/bath

Conveniently located on the main floor, this romantic room is decorated in shades of rose, teal and ivory. Large wing-back chairs in the bay window make a perfect spot for reading or conversation.

Rates

Call for current prices. Off-season rates November to February. Ask about the Military, Police, or AAA discount for a multi-night stay.

Two-night minimum stay on weekends. Pricing and minimum stays vary with certain holidays and festivals. All in-season weekends (March to October 31) are two-night minimum stays.

Corporate rates are available Sunday through Thursday nights only. Full rates apply to Friday and Saturday nights year round.
