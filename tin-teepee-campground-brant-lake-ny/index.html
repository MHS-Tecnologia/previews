Family camping on 125 acres of the Adirondack Park.

75 large full hook-up sites, a 40-acre pond and miles of hiking trails, in Brant Lake, New York.

Call 518-494-5555Sites & rates

Twenty miles north of Lake George. Sixty south of Lake Placid.

Tin Teepee sits on privately owned land inside the Adirondack Park. The Brant Lake and Schroon Lake public boat launches are minutes away, and so are a long list of area attractions.

75 large sites, every one with full hook-ups.

  • 50 amp electric service
  • Water and septic
  • Cable
  • A picnic table and a fire ring at every campsite

Tent campsites are $40 a day or $250 a week. Call the office for full hook-up rates and seasonal camping.

Swim, rent a boat, then a board game by the fire.

Swim in the big heated outdoor pool, rent a boat for the afternoon, join movie night, or borrow one of the campground’s board games for a family night by the fire. Theme weekends and onsite activities run all season.
