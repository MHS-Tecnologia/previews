Not sure which one fits?

Most projects are happy with a custom floor plan and the standard HTB finish. Others want interior and exterior designers to settle every color, perspective, mood and feel.

Bring your ideas and plans, finished or not. They are welcome, but by no means required. If you don't see something you like, we keep organizing plans until you do.
