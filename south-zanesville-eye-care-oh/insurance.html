If you don’t see your plan listed here, give us a call and we’ll be happy to help. Our staff is always available to answer questions about your benefits.

Vision insurance is not medical insurance

Medical insurance protects you against the cost of unexpected eye injuries or disease. Vision insurance is a wellness benefit: it covers routine eye care, prescription eyewear and other vision services at a reduced cost. For large families especially, vision insurance can lower the yearly cost of exams and glasses.

Paying for your visit

We accept cash, checks, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard and Discover.

CareCredit

As a service to our patients, we offer the CareCredit card, which lets you begin care right away and pay for it over time with monthly payments.
