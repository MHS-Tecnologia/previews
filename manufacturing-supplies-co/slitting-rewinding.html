Four ways to take a roll apart

Self-adhesive tapes, labels, laminates, textiles, non-wovens and flexible packaging, converted to the widths and lengths a plant needs.

  • Slitters-rewinders

    FEBA turret and duplex

    Semi- and fully automatic. Used for carton sealing, paper, medical, sport, automotive, aerospace, construction, foam and protection tapes.

  • Loggers and log winders

    FEBA automatic logger

    Converts large quantities of material from the original jumbo roll down to smaller logs.

  • Multi-blade slitters

    Antares strip cutters, IMESA multi-blade, ICZ 1600/2100 E LCA

    Antares, on the market since 1967, cuts strips from 150 to 2000 mm in synthetics, leather, rubber and other non-ferrous materials.

  • Slitting and sheeting

    International LC series and sheeters

    Works alone or as a complete unwinder, slitter, sheeter and rewinder line for foam, rubber, non-wovens, films and technical textiles.
