Standing seam

Clean vertical panels and hidden fasteners, in widths made for homes. It sheds snow and ice, and it looks right on a house.

Call Mike and Mary608-298-7334Email for a free estimate

A home, not a warehouse.

Standing seam panels run vertically down the roof, which is why so many commercial buildings choose them. We use narrower widths for homes. They reduce oil canning and give you the clean lines without a “commercial” look.

The snap lock system hides the screws and fasteners, which is what matters most for staying watertight. The roof also sheds snow, ice, water and debris easily.

It is an investment that adds long-term value to your home.

Planning solar? Start with the roof under it.

Solar panels are only as good as what they rest on. With a partial roof replacement, we put standing seam on just the section where the panels will go, so you won’t have to remove and reinstall them later. If you replace the rest of your asphalt later, we can do that too.
