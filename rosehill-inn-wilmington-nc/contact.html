Getting here

The inn is easily reached by highways 17, 74, 76, 117, 421 and Interstate 40. It is less than a 10-minute drive from the airport. Guests who come by boat on the Intracoastal Waterway will find ample dockage on the Cape Fear River three blocks from the inn.

The Atlantic Ocean is a 20-minute drive away.

From Interstate 40 East

  1. As you approach Wilmington, you will see a sign that reads “Expressway Ends Ahead.” Look for the Route 17 exit.
  2. Cross the highway and exit right onto a clover leaf ramp (stay in the left lane).
  3. At the stoplight at the bottom of the ramp, turn left onto Route 17, also known as Market Street.
  4. Continue south on Market Street about 4 miles, into the historic district.
  5. Turn left on Third Street and go up the hill 1½ blocks, past Dock Street.

From Route 17, going south

  1. Route 17 South becomes Market Street approaching the downtown historic district.
  2. Turn left on Third Street and go up the hill 1½ blocks, past Dock Street.

From Routes 17, 74, 76, going north

  1. Drive over the Cape Fear Memorial Bridge and stay in the left lane.
  2. At the first stop light, turn left onto Third Street.
  3. Continue on Third Street for 7 blocks to Dock Street, staying in the left lane.
  4. Make a U-turn around the median at Dock Street.
