ASME Y14.5 standards

Coffee stained hand sketches are welcome.

“We at SEM are familiar with both coffee stained hand sketches and thoroughly engineered drawings with tight tolerances, GD&T, and assemblies within assemblies within assemblies.”

Current drawings adhere to the latest version of ASME Y14.5 standards of dimensioning and tolerancing. With SolidWorks and Autodesk Fusion, SEM generates 3D models quickly and accurately, and creates the CNC programs to machine your parts to the accuracy you need.
