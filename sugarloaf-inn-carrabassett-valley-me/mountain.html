Sugarloaf Mountain, out the front door

Maine’s second-highest peak, surpassed only by Katahdin, and the largest ski area east of the Rockies.

4,237 ftsummit
1,360skiable acres
162trails and glades

Winter

Skiing

Gentle groomers for beginners to steep expert runs like the famous White Nitro. Brackett Basin adds tree skiing and backcountry access. High-speed lifts and snowmaking keep the days long.

Nordic skiing

The Sugarloaf Outdoor Center grooms over 90 kilometers of cross-country trails, classic and skate, with the Bigelow Range as a backdrop. Rentals and lessons on site.

Snowshoeing

Marked trails at the Outdoor Center for every level, and the Maine Huts & Trails network for longer days. Rentals at the Outdoor Center.

Fat biking

The Narrow Gauge Pathway runs flat along the Carrabassett River; Maine Huts & Trails goes deeper. Rentals at local shops.

Summer

Mountain biking

Over 100 miles of maintained Carrabassett Valley trails, including Sugarloaf’s lift-serviced bike park. Every Inn room has secure bike storage.

Golf

Sugarloaf Golf Club, an 18-hole course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., set against the Bigelow Range.

Flagstaff Lake

Minutes away, for paddling, swimming and boating.

Hiking

Sections of the Appalachian Trail, Bigelow access points, and the family-friendly walk to Poplar Stream Falls.
