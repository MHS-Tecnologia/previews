A family nursery since 1892

Gardens of the Blue Ridge describes itself as North Carolina’s oldest licensed nursery, family owned since 1892.

Carrying on the Robbins tradition

The nursery carries on the tradition of E. C. Robbins and his son Edward P. Robbins, supplying quality wildflowers and native plants to customers in the U.S. and around the world.

It remains in the family today with Robyn Fletcher: great-grandson of E. C. Robbins, grandson of Edward P. Robbins, and son of Katy Robbins Fletcher. Robyn has worked in the business for 30 years, learning every part of it from his grandfather, alongside his brother Edward Fletcher, who later left to work in the herbal industry.

Today the nursery is returning to full independent ownership and is operating strictly by mail order, with no retail store, while it prepares its spring lineup of native plants.

What the nursery grows

Its specialty is wildflowers, ferns, native orchids, trees and shrubs, grown in its own beds and sent across the United States. Plants are listed by botanical name, because common names overlap.
