Complete Outfitting Package

Our most popular package.

It supplies you with the lightest, finest equipment available. We take care of all the details so you can relax and have fun.

Bunkhouse and breakfast

Simple bunkhouse accommodations for the night before your canoe trip: twin beds with a fitted sheet, electricity, and a dry space for packing. Bring your sleeping bag. Each of our nine bunkhouse rooms is private to your group, so you won’t be bunking with strangers.

Breakfast is French toast, fresh fruit, sausage, coffee, tea, yogurt and hot chocolate, at 7 a.m. in the dining hall, by reservation by noon the day before. Gluten-free and vegan options are available on request when you reserve.

ServiceCost
Bunkhouse$34 per person
Breakfast$15 per person

Bunk and breakfast includes one complimentary shower per person. Check-in is no earlier than 3 p.m. Call 218-388-2221 to check bunk availability; it isn’t posted online.

Canoes

Most Boundary Waters paddlers opt for one of our ultra lightweight Kevlar canoes: Northstar, Souris River and Wenonah. They must be loaded and unloaded while completely floating, so plan for wet feet.

  • Life jackets and paddles included
  • Transportation to and from Cross Bay, Larch Creek, Magnetic (Granite River), Saganaga and Seagull entry points
  • Shower and towel at end of trip
