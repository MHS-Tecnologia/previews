Five steps from first call to finished backyard.

  1. Consultation

    We determine your goals, wish list, schedule and budget.

  2. Original Design

    We measure the property, then create high-quality 3D conceptual plans.

  3. Approval Process

    A third-party engineer approves the structural drawings before we submit for a permit.

  4. Customization

    Log into your client portal to choose material, color and finish.

  5. Build

    Once we have the permit and your selections, our master craftsmen bring it to life.
