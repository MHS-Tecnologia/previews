Directions

East Bay Cabins is at 1615 Birchwood Drive, St. Germain, WI 54558, on Little St. Germain Lake.

From Minocqua and Woodruff

Coming up I-39 / STH 51.

  1. STH 51, WoodruffNorth through Woodruff to STH 70.
  2. STH 70East on 70 to STH 155.
  3. STH 155Left on 155, three blocks to Birchwood Drive.
  4. 4.1 milesBirchwood DriveTurn right. The cabins are 4.1 miles in.
  5. Road sign, on the rightBirchwood Drive, mile 4.1.

Coming from Eagle River on STH 70 or 45: head west on STH 70 through St. Germain to where 70 meets 155, then follow the same last stretch to Birchwood Drive and the road sign.
