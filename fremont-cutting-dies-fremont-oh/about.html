Home / About

Over 40 years in the die industry.

Fremont Cutting Dies, Inc. is in Northwest Ohio. It makes steel rule, Viking, forged, contour trim, machined, specimen and match-metal dies, and takes on machining, fabrication and welding, all from one shop.

Made in-house, so quality stays in-house.

Fremont works as a one-stop shop for cutting dies and fabrication. Because the products are made in-house, the company controls the quality and the service, on small projects and large ones.

Design is in-house too, from 2-D to 3-D. The equipment runs from manual to fully automated.

Every die is checked for workmanship and accuracy against Fremont’s own strict internal guidelines before it leaves the factory, and inspection uses ISO 17025 certified equipment.

To maintain positive growth in all aspects of the company by exceeding our customers’ expectations of craftsmanship, value and service.
