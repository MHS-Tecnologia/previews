What a replacement looks like with us.

Most residential replacements are done in one to two days, weather permitting. Every step follows the manufacturer’s specifications so your roof qualifies for full warranty coverage.

  1. Tear-off and decking

    We remove the old layers and inspect the decking, correcting it where needed.

  2. Underlayment, flashing, shingles

    New underlayment and flashing, ventilation upgrades, then your chosen shingles or metal.

  3. Cleanup and walkthrough

    A full magnet sweep for nails, debris hauled away, and a walkthrough of the finished work.
