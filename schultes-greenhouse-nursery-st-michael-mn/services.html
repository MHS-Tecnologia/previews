We sell more than plants.

Our staff makes sure you know how to care for your yard and garden before you leave the store: how to care for your plants and how to use fertilizers, pesticides and other products.

Services

Plant diagnosis

Questions about insect damage, diseases or anything else? Bring in a sample of the affected leaves. A staff member will help diagnose the problem and develop a treatment plan.

Planters and containers

Bring in your planters or choose from the pottery in the store, and we'll help design and plant the right combination with your favorite flowers. Winter greens, spruce tops and sticks-n-stems add color all winter long.

Memorials

Plants for memorials, from a single tree to a complete memorial garden. We also have religious statues, plaques and cemetery stands.

Delivery

We deliver trees, shrubs and plants for special occasions, birthdays, anniversaries, memorials and convenience. Stop by or call (763) 497-3747 for details.

Pot recycling

Bring your old plastic pots and flats to Schulte's. We will happily recycle them.

Warranty

If any tree, shrub or evergreen fails to survive the first year from the date of purchase, it will be replaced once. Exceptions: animal or mechanical damage, vandalism, abnormal weather, plants in above-ground containers, and under or over-watering.

Help with your landscape

Our staff can help lay out a flower bed and show you what a combination of plants will look like, for a rock garden, a rain garden or any other spot.

Doing it yourself? Our help is free. Bring pictures of the area, scale drawings and how many hours of sun it gets.

Want it done? Consulting, design, installation and maintenance are available through contractors, with onsite consulting in St. Michael and the surrounding communities.

Gift cards

Sold in any denomination, with no expiration date. They buy vegetables for fresh produce all summer, hanging baskets and planters, bird feeders and much more.

For anniversaries, birthdays, Christmas, Easter, Father's Day, Mother's Day, funerals, a new house, weddings, day-care providers, friends, parents, grandparents, neighbors and teachers.
