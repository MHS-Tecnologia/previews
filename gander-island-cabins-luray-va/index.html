Seclusion and adventure, on a working family farm

Nestled between the Blue Ridge Mountains, along the Shenandoah River, the cabins are the ideal retreat to soak up all of nature’s wonders. Luray Caverns is in the town of Luray, 5 minutes away.

A canoe and two kayaks at the river cabins

The Mother Goose, the Goose and the Gander each come with a canoe and two kayaks, a horseshoe and corn hole game, and private riverfront access.

Sign and send the waiver before your stay to get the combination for the canoe lock. Adult life jackets are provided; bring the right size for children. Weight limit is 230 lbs per boat.

Your hosts, the Gander family

The cabins are part of a farm in the Shenandoah Valley that dates back to the 1700s. David, Lisa and Katherine Gander, the 5th generation, raise cattle and grow hay and grains on 250 acres of land, carrying on the traditions of generations.

They look forward to showing you the cabins and sharing the splendors of the Shenandoah River, the valley and the mountains.
