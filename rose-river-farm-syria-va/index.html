Private fly fishing on the Rose River. Five rods a day.

Cold, spring-fed, catch-and-release trout water in Madison County, Virginia, with three yurt-style cabins for overnight guests. An hour and forty-five minutes from Washington, DC.

The rules of the water

Their stretch of the Rose River is some of the purest water in Virginia, tested regularly for water quality and aquatic insects. A river-rock bottom with very little sediment, and plenty of natural trout food. The rules keep it that way.

Four kinds of trout live here: wild Rose rainbows, wild brook trout, wild brown trout and stocked jumbo rainbows. The water is very wadeable, so it suits beginners and children (fly fishing only, please) as much as experienced anglers.

Fishing, guides and lessons

  • Fly onlyStrictly on the fly, catch and gentle release.
  • Five rods a dayThe farm limits the stream to five rods, so the water stays uncrowded.
  • Book four rodsand the stream is reserved for your party only.
  • $100 rod feeper person. Every visitor signs a waiver.

Nymphs can be very effective all year, often fished as a dry-dropper: the nymph on 1–2 feet of 5X tippet under an attractor dry fly.

Their tackle: a 4 or 5 weight rod, 7 to 9 foot leaders, 5X tippet. The Farm Fly Shop, in the big white barn with the green roof up the hill from the fishing gazebo, stocks guide Alan’s own proven ties.

Stay the night in a yurt

Three cabins, Hill Top, Pond View and Mountain View, built as modern takes on the Mongolian yurt in cedar, glass and steel. They sit on a private 20-acre parcel catty-corner to the farm.

  • Two bedrooms, each with two double beds, and two full baths
  • Full kitchen, and a fly-tying table with a Regal vise
  • A stocked pond only for cabin guests: bass, bluegill and trout, catch and release
  • $275 a night plus tax, for 1 to 4 guests
