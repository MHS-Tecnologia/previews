How a home service call goes

The five steps Dual Temp Home Services follows on every visit.

  1. You call or email

    Share a few details so they can dispatch the right technician with the right equipment.

  2. A certified technician comes out

    Same-day scheduling in most cases, ready to diagnose the problem.

  3. You get a written estimate

    No hidden fees, no pressure. Honest numbers before any work begins.

  4. The work gets done

    Trucks travel fully stocked, so most problems are resolved in a single visit.

  5. They check it and clean up

    The system is verified before they leave, and the work area is cleaned completely.
