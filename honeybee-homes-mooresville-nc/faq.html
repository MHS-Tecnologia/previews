Questions we hear often

Costs, timelines and permits for kitchens, bathrooms and additions around Lake Norman. Are we licensed and insured? Yes, fully.

Kitchen remodeling

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in the Lake Norman area?

A kitchen remodel in the Lake Norman region typically ranges from $20,000 to $60,000 for mid-range updates, while high-end or full layout reconfigurations can cost upwards of $80,000 to $175,000+.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchen remodels take between 4 to 8 weeks for standard projects, though major structural reconfigurations can take 8 to 12 weeks.

Is it worth remodeling my kitchen before selling?

Yes, kitchen remodels often yield one of the highest returns on investment for Lake Norman homes, significantly boosting buyer interest and resale value.

Can I live in my home during a kitchen remodel?

Yes, most homeowners remain in their homes during construction by setting up a temporary kitchen space with basic appliances.

Do I need permits for a kitchen remodel?

Permits are required in North Carolina whenever you alter structural framing, plumbing, or electrical lines. Cosmetic updates like replacing cabinet doors or paint generally do not require permits.

What cabinets offer the best value?

Semi-custom plywood-constructed cabinets with soft-close hardware offer the best balance of durability, customization, and cost-efficiency.

What countertop material is best?

Quartz is currently the top choice for Lake Norman homeowners due to its non-porous nature, low maintenance, and high durability compared to granite.

Should I keep my existing kitchen layout?

Keeping your existing layout saves significant money on plumbing and electrical labor. However, changing the layout is worth it if the current design hinders workflow or home value.

Can walls be removed to create an open floor plan?

Yes, non-load-bearing walls can be removed easily, while load-bearing walls require proper beam support designed by a licensed contractor or engineer.

What adds the most value in a kitchen remodel?

Updated cabinetry, durable stone or quartz countertops, modern energy-efficient appliances, and improved lighting generate the highest value return.

Bathroom remodeling

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

Guest or hall bathroom remodels in the area average $15,000 to $35,000, while primary suite luxury renovations range from $35,000 to $70,000+.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

A full bathroom renovation generally takes 2 to 4 weeks, while complex primary bathroom overhauls can take up to 6 weeks.

Should I replace my bathtub with a walk-in shower?

Converting a primary bath tub to a tile walk-in shower is extremely popular and highly desired by modern home buyers. However, keeping at least one bathtub in the home is recommended for family resale value.

What is the best tile for a bathroom?

Porcelain tile is the overall best choice because it is waterproof, slip-resistant, easy to clean, and extremely durable.

Can you make my bathroom more accessible?

Yes, contractors can incorporate curbless walk-in showers, grab bars, wider doorways, and floating vanities for full accessibility.

Do I need permits for a bathroom remodel?

Yes, permits are required in North Carolina if the work involves moving plumbing lines, adding electrical outlets, or modifying structural walls.

What bathroom upgrades increase home value?

Custom tiled walk-in showers, double vanities with stone counters, improved lighting, and updated plumbing fixtures yield the strongest returns.

Should I install heated floors?

Heated tile flooring adds a luxurious feel and comfortable warmth during winter months, making it a very desirable upgrade for primary bathrooms.

Can plumbing fixtures be relocated?

Yes, plumbing fixtures can be moved, though doing so requires opening subfloors or walls and will increase overall labor costs.

What is the difference between a renovation and a remodel?

A renovation restores or updates existing elements (e.g., fresh paint or new vanity), whereas a remodel changes the structural layout or function of the space.

Home additions

How much does a home addition cost?

Home additions typically cost between $150 and $300+ per square foot depending on the complexity, finishes, and foundation requirements.

Is it cheaper to build an addition or move?

Building an addition is often cheaper than moving when factoring in current interest rates, real estate commissions, closing costs, and moving fees in Lake Norman.

How long does a home addition take?

Ground-up home additions usually take between 3 to 6 months from excavation to final inspection.

Do I need an architect?

While not always mandatory, an architect or design-build general contractor is essential to ensure plans comply with local building codes and structural loads.

Can my existing HVAC handle an addition?

It depends on your current unit's capacity, though installing a ductless mini-split system is often the most cost-effective heating and cooling solution for an addition.

What types of additions add the most value?

Primary bedroom suites, expanded kitchen areas, and main-floor living spaces generate the highest appraisal boosts.

Can I build over my garage?

Yes, provided the existing garage foundation and wall framing are structurally sound enough to support a second-floor load.

How close can I build to my property line?

Setback requirements vary by local municipality zoning laws (e.g., Mooresville, Cornelius, or Charlotte), so your contractor must verify local property lines before design.

Will my addition match my existing home?

A skilled general contractor will carefully match rooflines, siding, brickwork, trim, and interior finishes so the addition looks original to the house.

Can I live in my home during construction?

Yes, since the majority of addition work takes place on the exterior before opening the connection to the existing interior home.
