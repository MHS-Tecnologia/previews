Neofit®+Plus potable water

Lead-free water without digging up the yard.

Neofit®+Plus is an NSF 61 approved, structural expandable pressure pipe (EPP) that re-pipes leaking and lead water service lines from ½″ to 2″ ID, in lengths up to 300 ft, often through a single small access pit.

Ask about a pilotBecome a Neofit installer

Not a coating. No epoxy, no resin.

EPP does not need the host pipe to keep its pressure-tested strength. It forms a continuous barrier between the existing pipe and the drinking water, which makes it a fit for lead replacement programs.

Installed in thousands of water service lines throughout the world. The smooth-bore, thin-wall pipe gives the same or increased flow capacity, and the line returns to service immediately.

0% lead detected

We installed Neofit+Plus inside a lead service pipe, tested it following AWWA’s field guide for hydrostatic testing of water service lines, and then analyzed the water for lead. None was detected. Documentation is available on request.

The same PET lining was used in the first U.S. pilot installation in lead service lines in 2008. Those lines were retested in 2016, after sitting idle for eight years, with excellent results.

A 200 ft copper line under a heated driveway

A homeowner in Pittsburgh had a leaking 1-inch copper service line running about 200 feet down a steep wooded hillside, partly under a newly installed heated concrete driveway.

A Flow-Liner Certified Neofit+Plus Installer rehabilitated the full 200 feet, through about 25 feet of elevation change, with one pit at the curb stop and the interior shutoff as the second access point. No trenching, no hillside excavation, and the driveway was never touched. The line was tested and back in service the same day.

In the field

With our dealer and engineering partners, we installed Neofit+Plus in a 1″ lead water service line in Wauwatosa, Wisconsin and a 1″ copper line in Van Meter, Iowa.

Questions we hear

From water authorities, ratepayers and homeowners.

How long will it protect my water from lead?

A third-party lab, using scientifically proven methods, estimates the high-quality PET in our product will exceed 100 years in service.

What about BPA?

Our EPP material is virgin PET. PET does not contain BPA, so it will not contaminate your drinking water.

Will it reduce the flow rate?

Only marginally. In most cases the smooth wall is an improvement and actually increases the flow rate.

What’s next: the Main Connection System

An upcoming addition that installs Neofit+Plus in ½″ to 1″ service lines from inside 8″ to 12″ water mains, with one access at each end of the main. We are looking for pilot projects.
