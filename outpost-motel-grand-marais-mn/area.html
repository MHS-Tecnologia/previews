Eighteen acres between the lake and the forest

Family owned and operated, The Outpost sits on 18 mostly wooded acres along Lake Superior's North Shore.

Just across the road is a sand and pebble beach on Lake Superior, perfect for walks on the beach, rock hunting, picnics, a roaring beach bonfire, or just relaxing to the sounds of the waves.

Behind The Outpost is the Superior National Forest. A hiking trail from the motel joins the Superior Hiking Trail, which follows the shoreline for more than 120 miles through boreal forest. A short walk away is Kadunce Wayside Park, where scenic trails follow the deep gorges and waterfalls of Kadunce Creek.

The property has a creek running through it, a swing set and play area for younger guests, and plenty of room for picnics. The adirondack chairs face the lake: coffee in the morning, a drink in the evening. Even in winter some hearty souls take a seat with a hot drink.

Out the door

  • Snowshoe and cross country ski trails, with a snowshoe trail right out the door
  • The Superior Hiking Trail and other trails, discovered and undiscovered
  • Wildlife and birding all around the motel
  • Wildflowers in the spring and summer
  • Fishing: guests have caught fish right from the shore in front of The Outpost
  • Mountain and fat tire biking, or a slow ride through town

A drive away

  • Artist's Point in Grand Marais
  • Devil's Kettle in Judge Magney State Park
  • Grand Portage Monument and Fur Trading Fort, and the falls
  • The Arrowhead and Gunflint Trails
  • Canoe trips into the Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness, and kayaking on the rivers, creeks and lakes
  • Shops, restaurants and art galleries in Grand Marais
  • Courses at the North House Folk School or the Grand Marais Art Colony
  • Performances at the Arrowhead Center for the Arts
  • Fort William Historical Park in Thunder Bay, Ontario
  • Isle Royale National Park
  • Hunting, and fishing on plenty of inland lakes

In winter

  • Cross country and downhill skiing
  • Snowshoeing: a workout, but awesome
  • Dogsledding day trips and dogsled races
