Built in 1848 by Henry Russell Savage

The house was built by a prominent Wilmington businessman and banker. It was also the home of Henry Bacon Jr., architect of the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, D.C.

Step through the front entry and see the Italian marble fireplace, ten foot tall oak pocket doors, and a pulpit staircase in quarter-sawn white oak. The wide wrap-around porch overlooks the original terraced gardens, with old fashioned porch swings and plenty of wicker chairs.
