Weddings for over 25 years

Weddings, reunions and company parties, catered in-house

Classic New England architecture, a carefully restored banquet room, floral and vegetable gardens, and a courtyard and patio, minutes from Hanover. Full catering and bar services for every event on site.

Four event spaces

The Banquet Hall200 seats

The main reception room, originally the barn. Weddings, company parties, conferences and business meetings. The courtyard and patio are right outside its doors.

The Courtyard & PatioPatio added in 2015

The patio is made for cocktail receptions. The courtyard can take a tent, with room for a dance floor and outdoor lighting for evening events.

The Carriage HouseBuilt-in bar

Once home to the carriages, attached to the barn. Basket-weave brick floors and historical charm make it a great place for a cocktail reception.

The Sun RoomBreakfast room

Where in-house guests have breakfast. Right for smaller gatherings: company parties, bridal and baby showers, birthday parties.

What the Inn hosts

Guest rooms can become breakout rooms for conferences and retreats, and 20 rooms upstairs mean your guests can stay the night.

  • Weddings & rehearsal dinners. Ceremonies and receptions.
  • Special events. Family and class reunions, bridal and baby showers, anniversaries, memorial gatherings, birthdays, bar and bat mitzvahs.
  • Conferences & corporate events. Sales meetings, retreats, award banquets, holiday and retirement parties, with A/V equipment.
  • Full catering. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, with full bar service.
