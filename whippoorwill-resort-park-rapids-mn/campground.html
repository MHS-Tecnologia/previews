The campground

A quiet campground nestled among the trees, with four sites: two with full hookups, one with electricity and water, and one with electricity.

Call 218-732-4639Email to book a site

On the grounds

  • “His” and “hers” restrooms and showers
  • A laundry room and a fish cleaning room
  • A dump station
  • A picnic table and a fire pit at every site
  • Dock space when available; call ahead

Firewood is sold at the resort. Please don’t bring wood in.

Booking is a phone call

When you are ready to book, call or email Jim and Jill. If the week you want is taken, ask for the wait list: reservations do change.

Call 218-732-4639Email info@whippoorwillresort.com
Deposit
Personal check, or Visa, MasterCard or Discover. A cabin is held 10 days while your deposit reaches them. Inside 21 days of arrival, a credit card deposit is required.
Check-in and check-out
Check-in 3:30 p.m. Check-out by 9:00 a.m.
Pets
Not allowed at the resort or the campground. They can suggest a local kennel.
