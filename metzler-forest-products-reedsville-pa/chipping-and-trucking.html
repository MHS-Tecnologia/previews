Contract chipping

Chipping services built for both quality and annual volume. Whether you need a reliable long-term chip mill or wood yard operator, or short-term capacity to supplement or replace your own, Metzler provides turnkey wood yard management: from receiving wood deliveries to transporting and delivering the finished product to your mill.

  • More than 30 years of experience supplying and operating within the paper industry
  • Long-term chip mill and wood yard operation
  • Short-term chipping capacity to supplement or replace your own
  • In-woods chipping, pioneered by Metzler in Pennsylvania in the early 1990s
500+loads of wood chips and other forest products, every week, across the Mid Atlantic.
