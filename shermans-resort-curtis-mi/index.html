Good to know before you call

  • Cash and checks only. No credit cards.
  • $250 deposit per cabin holds a reservation. Deposits are non-refundable for 3 or more reserved cabins.
  • Cancel a month and a half before arrival.
  • No pets.
  • Bring towels and paper products. Ice and firewood are for sale on site.
  • Not handicap accessible.
