Cabins on Lake Superior and the Cross River

Twelve cabins, all full housekeeping: a kitchen with stove, refrigerator, toaster, coffee maker with filters, pots, pans and dishes; a bathroom with shower; bedding and towels. New pillow-top queen mattresses in every cabin. Each has a fire ring and charcoal grill outside. Wi-Fi is available near the office.

1 2 3

Log cabins on the Cross River

Cabins 1 and 2 are one-room cabins with a queen bed and sleeper futon. Cabin 3 has a separate bedroom. They can sleep 4, although 4 adults are cramped. Perfect for a couple.

These were the first cabins built on the property, in the 1920s. Often called the Honeymoon Cabins, the sound of the Cross River Falls will surround you, “truly making one little room an everywhere” (John Donne).

Stays and deposits

Three-night minimum, unless the calendar allows for fewer. Check-in is 3 pm, check-out is 11 am. A $300 deposit per cabin is due when you reserve, by Visa, MasterCard or Discover. Stays of 7 days or longer get 15% off.

Dogs, yes. Cats, no.

Dogs are welcome but must be registered, never left unattended, leashed outdoors and cleaned up after. $50 per stay, two dogs per cabin at most. No cats, and no fireworks.

Cancellations

Give 30 days' notice before arrival for a full refund minus a $25 handling fee. Any other refund depends on renting the cabin for the whole period booked.
