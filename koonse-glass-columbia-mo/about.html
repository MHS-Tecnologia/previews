Giving back

Koonse Glass has been a staple in the Columbia community for more than 50 years, and the company is proud to call Columbia home.

It supports a number of local causes, from Scouting America and the Columbia Memorial Stair Climb to Alpha Hart Elementary and Our Lady of Lourdes Interparish schools.

Careers

Koonse Glass is a locally owned and operated glass installation company, and calls its employees its most valuable asset. The team offers ongoing training and mentorship, and as a small company, the chance to take on different roles.

Benefits include health insurance, retirement plans, paid time off and profit sharing. There are no immediate openings, but applications for glazier positions and office staff are reviewed at any time. Send the job application to customerservice@koonseglass.com.
