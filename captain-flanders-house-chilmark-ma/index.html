A whaling captain’s farmhouse in Chilmark

A renovated farmhouse built by whaling Captain Richard Flanders in the late 1700s, on 60 acres of rolling pasture overlooking Bliss Pond, Martha’s Vineyard.

Ask about a stay (508) 645-3123

Four rooms in the house, two cottages behind it

Pick a room on the plan to see it. The Stone Cottage was built by artist and stone mason Lew French.

Drawn for this page, not to scale: rooms are placed by floor, not by their real position in the house.

Breakfast, and the small things

Continental breakfast is served daily from 8 to 10 am: homemade breads and muffins, fresh fruit, cereal, coffee, tea and juice, with island-made honey and jams.

Then the sun porch, the pasture, a good book.

  • Continental breakfast, 8 to 10 am
  • Free wi-fi
  • Plush robes
  • Bottled water for each guest
  • Chilmark beach passes

Weddings on the farm, for decades

For years, couples have chosen to begin their lives together here. The country setting works for a ceremony as well as a reception. Write for availability, planning tips and wedding rates.
