Value isn’t the same as cheap

The kitchen is where most households gather, and a renovation comes with a long list of choices. Total Home stays with you through all of them, with complimentary estimates and 3D design services, then guidance on design choices and materials until the work is done.

Nobody wants to invest in a kitchen and end up saying it’s an improvement, but not exactly what they had in mind. Value means a remarkable product and the most for your money, even on a substantial investment. What that looks like differs from one person to the next.

What your kitchen could include

  • Custom cabinets in various wood types, including exotics
  • Prefabricated cabinets of such quality they seem custom-made
  • Unique granite, some with natural fossils
  • Modern laminate countertops at a more budget-friendly cost
  • Help with lighting, backsplash and appliance selections
