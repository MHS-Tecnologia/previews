Your stay

Rooms are neat, clean and inexpensive, and every one of them is non-smoking. Here is what is in them, and how the front desk works.

Call 610-377-1600

In every room

  • Air conditioning
  • Free high speed Wi-Fi
  • Phone with free local calls, data port
  • Refrigerator
  • TV with cable and free HBO
  • Serta bedding

In some rooms, or on request

  • Microwave and larger 32" flat screen TV
  • Coffee maker, hair dryer, DVD player
  • Pillow top mattresses in the King suites
  • Iron and board, hair dryer and coffee maker on request
  • Adjoining rooms on request

Arriving and leaving

Check in at 2:00 PM. Early check-in at 11:30 AM is available. Check out at 11:00 AM; for a late check-out, talk to the front desk.

Guests must be 18 years old with a valid ID.

If you don't like your room

After check-in you have 15 minutes to check your room. If you do not like it, please do not use anything in it and come back to the front desk. Your money will be refunded and we will search for other accommodations for you.

Paying

All major credit cards, cash, commercial checks, travelers checks, state vouchers and direct billing. Sorry, no personal checks.

Paying for someone else's room? Ask the front desk for the credit card authorization form, to fax back to 610-379-0194 before check-in.

Reservations

A valid credit card is required to make a reservation. Always ask for a confirmation or cancellation number. The cancellation policy depends on the number of rooms and special events, so ask about it when you book.

In the lobby

Complimentary continental breakfast and coffee, free ice, and a refrigerator and microwave for your use. Toiletries are for sale, and fax and copy services are on site.

Weddings, reunions, ski trips

Rooms suit any occasion, including weddings, family reunions and tourism. Ask about weekly and special group rates, and about ski packages for Blue Mountain, about 11 to 14 miles away. Gift and ski certificates are available.

Lost and found

We hold all valuables. Call us within 24 hours of your check out.

Privacy

We do not discuss any information about our customers.
