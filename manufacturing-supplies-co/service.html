Cutting pads and boards

Röchling Hydroma and MSC have supplied pure polypropylene cutting pads and boards for cutting soft and semi-rigid materials for decades, both with new die cutting machines and as replacements.

  • HY84 Perlamon
  • HY78 Natural
  • HY72 Red-Brown

Smooth on both sides, bright-pressed or planed, or roughened on one side for bonding to a plywood base.

Cutting belts

Habasit die cutting belts resist oil, abrasion and scratching, so they last longer with less downtime. Nylon belts suit hard-to-cut textiles, gaskets and carpet, with a black fabric cover or coverless where contamination matters.

MSC supplies belts for ATOM and Chiesa systems and conveyor belting for Freeman Schwabe, Herman Schwabe, Bruno, Hudson, USM and Associated Pacific presses, and offers field installation.
