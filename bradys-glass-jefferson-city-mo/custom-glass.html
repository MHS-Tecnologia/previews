Custom mirrors

We make mirrors to fit your space exactly rather than relying on standard sizes. All mirrors are cut and prepared in-house, so we can match your dimensions, shape and cutouts for fixtures or outlets, with edge finishing including beveled edges.

We make them for bathrooms and vanities, walls, replacements, and offices and retail spaces.

What to expect

  1. Consultation and measurementWe review your space and take accurate measurements.
  2. Quote and scopeYou get a clear estimate based on your project details.
  3. Fabrication and installationYour mirror is made in-house and professionally installed.
  4. Final reviewWe confirm everything is properly installed and aligned.

Because each mirror is made to fit, pricing varies with size, cutouts, edge finishing and installation complexity.
