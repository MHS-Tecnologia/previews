Engineering

The business began in 1994 as a sub-contract die design source. SolidWorks is the primary CAD software today, with Inventor and AutoCAD.

  • Die designProgressive dies, stage and hand-fed dies, for traditional stamping plants and for automated cells.
  • Machining and welding fixture design
  • Gages
  • Fixtures and tooling for automated and robotic cells
  • 2D AutoCAD design
