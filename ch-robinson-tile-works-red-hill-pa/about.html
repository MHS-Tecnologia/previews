A family business, one customer at a time

C.H. Robinson Tile Works has served homes and businesses in the Lehigh Valley from Red Hill, Pennsylvania, since 1999.

  1. 1991

    Chad Robinson begins a tile apprenticeship and learns the craft the right way, from the ground up. He works full-time for the owner of a third-generation tile installation business who expects perfection on every project.

  2. Red Hill

    Wanting a business of his own, Chad moves his family to Red Hill and buys a home that comes with a large storefront.

  3. 1999

    He opens the tile showroom on Main Street and grows the business one customer at a time.
