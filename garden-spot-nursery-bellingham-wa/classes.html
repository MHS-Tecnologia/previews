Classes and events at the nursery

Since we opened, we have offered classes and events to bring our community together. Please sign up by phone to reserve your spot.

Call 360-676-5480 to sign up

  1. November 7, 10am

    Lasagna Bulb Planter Make and Take

    It’s round 2 of our lasagna planter class! If you missed the first class, here’s your last chance to plant spring bulbs topped with fall pansies in this fun, hands-on workshop with Heidi. Enjoy waves of beautiful blooms from one pot next spring.

    Class fee $49.99

  2. November 14, 11am–5pm

    Holiday Open House & Gift Market

    Welcome the new season our favorite way, with a party! Enjoy treats, music, local gift vendors, and fun holiday activities while taking in the beautiful lights and decorations. Lots of photo spots, so dress up and bring the family!

    Free, all ages

  3. November 21, 10am

    Holiday Centerpiece Make and Take

    Make a stunning centerpiece with greens, cones, berries, twigs, and ribbon. It will be the talking point of any holiday gathering or a nice touch to your holiday decor.

    Class fee $19.99–$59.99, depending on size

  4. November 28, 10am

    Wreath Decorating Make and Take

    Decorate a classic evergreen wreath with berries, forest trimmings, and a big fancy bow. Our designers will help you create a beautiful wreath to adorn your door and bring you holiday cheer.

    Class fee $59.99

  5. December 19, 10am and 12pm

    Living Ornament Make and Take

    Build glass balls with Tillandsia, tiny lichens, mosses, twigs, and forest trimmings. These lovely ornaments are sure to become a favorite addition to your holiday decor. All ages welcome, fun for kids!

    Class fee $19.99

Who teaches

Evan is often found leading our classes on terrarium making. Though he is a terrariums wizard, his true passion lies in bonsai, and his favorite plant to bonsai is the ficus.

Paige, our owner, teaches a series of composting classes. “Taking care of our soil is so important, especially for our future food security.”

Heidi leads the lasagna bulb planter class. She has a deep love for flowers, a magical patio garden, and a thriving collection of 45 houseplants.

Our designers help with the holiday wreaths and centerpieces. Meet the whole team.
