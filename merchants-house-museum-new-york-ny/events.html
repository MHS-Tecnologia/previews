Tours, talks and a candlelit Christmas Carol.

Reservations are strongly recommended. Tickets for in-person events are emailed to you, with a reminder the week of the event. Paid tickets are refundable or transferable up to 48 hours before.

  1. Sunday, November 8
    1:30 p.m.

    Walking tour: the Tredwells’ world of 19th-century NoHo

    After the Erie Canal opened in 1825, wealthy merchant families moved “uptown” to what is now NoHo, then an exclusive residential enclave. Explore the Tredwells’ neighborhood and what life was like for the merchant class. 90 minutes, meeting next door in Manuel Plaza.

    $20, free for members.

  2. Sunday, November 15
    3 p.m.

    In the footsteps of Bridget Murphy: the life of an Irish servant

    A “back-stairs” house tour, co-sponsored by Village Preservation, telling the story of the Irish women in domestic service in 19th-century New York. Four floors, from the ground-floor kitchen to the fourth-floor servants’ quarters. 75 to 90 minutes.

    $20, free for MHM and Village Preservation members.

  3. November 24 to January 3

    A Christmas Carol at the Merchant’s House

    The 14th holiday season with Summoners Ensemble Theatre. Mr. Dickens, played by John Kevin Jones and Vince Gatton at alternating performances, tells his tale in the Greek Revival double parlor, among candles and period furnishings. 70 minutes, from Dickens’ own script.

    Tickets $50 to $130. All ticket sales are final.

  4. November 27 to January 7

    Christmas comes to Old New York

    A holiday exhibition. The house is dressed for the 1850s with evergreen swags, holly, mistletoe and poinsettias, with holiday gifts from the Tredwell collection on display.

  5. On view

    Abolition and the Underground Railroad in pro-slavery New York City, 1820s–1830s

    The museum’s discovery of the hidden passage, and the wider story of early abolitionism in the city.

Group and private tours

Groups of 10 or more must schedule a private visit, at least two weeks ahead. Private guided tours run Monday and Tuesday, 10 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., and Wednesday to Friday at 10 or 11 a.m. A 50% deposit confirms the booking. Write to programs@merchantshouse.org.

The house and the passage

Four floors of period rooms, the rear garden and the Underground Railroad passage. 60 to 75 minutes.

$20 general, $15 seniors and students.

Irish servants’ tour

The “back-stairs” tour, kitchen to servants’ quarters. 75 to 90 minutes.

$22 general, $17 seniors and students.

Culinary tour

Favorite foods, cooking, dining etiquette and entertaining in mid-19th-century New York. 75 to 90 minutes.

$22 general, $17 seniors and students.

Virtual tours can also be booked two weeks ahead, weekdays 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.: $150 for up to 30 people, $200 for 31 to 60, $250 for 61 to 100.
