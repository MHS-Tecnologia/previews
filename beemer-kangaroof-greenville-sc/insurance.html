When to file a roof insurance claim

Hail damage

Impacts from hail can crack shingles and create pathways for water to penetrate the roof.

Wind damage

Strong gusts can lift, loosen, or tear away shingles, leaving the roof vulnerable.

Fallen trees or debris

Heavy impacts can crack shingles and compromise the roof’s integrity.

Severe leaks

Severe leaks can quickly damage insulation, drywall, and even the home’s structure.

Storm damage

It isn’t always obvious. Even small problems can grow into costly repairs.

How we help with your claim

  1. Free roof inspection

    We carefully document damage with detailed notes and photos.

  2. Insurance claim assistance

    We guide you through the filing process and provide the documentation your insurance adjuster needs.

  3. Meet with your adjuster

    Our experts can meet with your insurance company to ensure nothing is overlooked.

  4. Roof repair or replacement

    Once approved, we complete the work quickly and professionally.

  5. Peace of mind

    You enjoy a safe, restored roof without the headaches of handling the claim alone.
