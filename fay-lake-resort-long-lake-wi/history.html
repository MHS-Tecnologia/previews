Pictures from long ago

Fay Lake Resort began in the 1920s as a family retreat. More cabins were built, and the retreat became a premier fishing resort. These are the resort's own historical pictures.

White Birch, then and now

From wood cribs to steel

Nine of the ten cabins were built on wood cribs in the water in the 1920s, 30s and 40s. Over the years the cribs sank, and more wood was added on top to keep the cabins level. By 2009 that no longer worked, so the family began putting the cabins on steel piles.

  1. All of the decks and docks were removed from the cabin. 52-foot steel piles were driven into the lake bed, and 38 to 43-foot piles into the land.

  2. Temporary cribs were built on the ice to hold the cabins, and a hydraulic lifting system raised them.

  3. Steel H beams were welded onto the piles, and smaller beams onto those. The cabins rest on the smaller beams.

  4. With cabins 3 and 4 done and cabin 2 not yet started, you could really see the difference. Then the decks and docks went back on.
