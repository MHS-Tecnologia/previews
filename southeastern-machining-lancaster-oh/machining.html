Blanket orders

Lower the price of a part you order again and again.

SEM can lower the price of manufacturing your part by distributing the workload over time. The costs of the initial production (engineering, making fixtures, custom tooling, and programming) are split up and factored into the price of your repeat business.

For example: a bushing in your machine wears down every couple of months and you order it on a regular basis. SEM may reduce the price of the initial part in exchange for an agreement to purchase a specific quantity over a specific amount of time, such as 15 bushings every 4 months.
