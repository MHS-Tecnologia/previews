Exterior questions

What type of paint is ideal for exterior surfaces?

Acrylic latex paints are commonly recommended because they offer strong adhesion, flexibility, and resistance to cracking or peeling over time. Satin and semi-gloss finishes are often used on exteriors for protection and cleanability.

How do weather conditions affect exterior painting?

Temperature, humidity and precipitation can affect drying time, adhesion and finish quality. We monitor forecasts closely to schedule work during optimal conditions.

How do you protect landscaping during painting?

This may include covering shrubs and flower beds with protective sheeting, moving outdoor furniture, and masking walkways and patios.
