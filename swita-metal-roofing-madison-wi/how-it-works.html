How it works

The estimate

60–90 minutes

That is an average appointment. We give you an exact breakdown of all the work to be performed.

A guaranteed price

Your quote is based on your roof size, pitch, complexity and product choice. We guarantee the finished job will cost exactly the quoted price for your chosen product.

No waiting around

You can typically expect your quote immediately, or within 24 hours of our presentation, with a timeline for the project.

The attic comes first.

We won’t start a metal roof without a thorough roof and attic inspection to assess moisture, heat loss and ice dam issues. That inspection also tells us whether your new roof can go over the existing one.

Metal is lightweight, so it can often go right on top of one layer of asphalt shingles, which keeps waste out of landfills. If the current layer is curling, crumbling or leaking, a tear-off is the better choice.

Hi-Flow Ridge Vent

Proper ventilation keeps energy costs down and helps prevent mold in the attic. Our Hi-Flow Ridge Vents use negative pressure to pull moisture and excess heat out. Our trained representatives will determine the most effective system for your home, because there is no one-size-fits-all solution.

Crews that only do metal.

Each crew member installs metal roofing exclusively, day in and day out, and has been doing it for the past 5–15 years. We screen every crew member, and we insist on factory certification and continuing education. We match the right team to the right job.

The average metal roof takes 3–7 days to install, or slightly more if your roof is complex or very large.
