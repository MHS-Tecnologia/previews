High-carbon biochar, made every day in Reedsville.

Clean, sustainable biochar tailored to Central Pennsylvania's soils, made from local sawmill co-products, for farmers, growers, gardeners and poultry barns.

Three products

Each one starts with pureChar. The other two blend it into Metzler's own compost and topsoil.

pureChar

Pure biochar, highly stable, with high carbon content.

In the barn it keeps bedding drier, cuts ammonia and odor, and makes a better fertilizer. In soil, properly inoculated, it holds water and nutrients. It also captures nutrients and heavy metals from stormwater, and can be mixed into concrete and asphalt.

USDA BioPreferred Certified, PA Preferred, recognized by Pennsylvania Certified Organic

earthChar

Soil blend: local screened topsoil, growChar compost and more pureChar.

A good place to start for raised bed gardens and planters, and a soil conditioner for existing plantings. In Metzler's test it took in 8 gallons of water in very dry conditions, where plain screened topsoil ponded.

growChar

Compost: local green waste and wood fines, spent mushroom substrate, a nitrogen fixer and pureChar.

For gardens and farms. Amend your current soil 25% to 50% by volume.

USDA BioPreferred Certified, PA Preferred, recognized by Pennsylvania Certified Organic

Bulk, super sack or bag

Buy biochar by the cubic yard for large applications, or in smaller amounts for landscaping and gardens. Have it delivered, or buy in person at the Landscape Center in Reedsville.

  • Loose bulk
  • Two cubic yard super sacks
  • Cubic foot bags

Landscape Center scoop prices for growChar and earthChar, and the pureChar super sack price, are on the Landscape Center price list.

Making biochar at Metzler sequesters carbon, and it cuts the propane used at the firewood heat-treating facility.
