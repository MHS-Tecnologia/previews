Roanoke Island, Outer Banks of North Carolina

Manteo and the island

The island experience: laid back but with a refined side, part of a welcoming community but with just the right amount of privacy.

Near the cottages

Some of the most popular attractions on the Outer Banks are on the island.

  • The Manteo waterfront

    A boardwalk links the waterfront area, with restaurants, shops, art galleries, jewelers, potters and museums.

  • Roanoke Island Festival Park

    Across a small bridge from the waterfront: an interactive museum, amphitheater, children’s programs, an Indian town and the reproduction ship Elizabeth II.

  • The Elizabethan Gardens

    A bike ride away. Besides their beauty, the Gardens host many events year round.

  • Fort Raleigh and Waterside Theatre

    Next door to the Gardens, where The Lost Colony is performed on summer nights.

  • North Carolina Aquarium

    Close by. You can easily spend hours in the cool, quiet aquatic environment.

  • The beaches

    Only minutes away: more than 100 miles of beaches, lighthouses and islands, with fishing, sailing, parasailing, kayaking and paddleboarding.
