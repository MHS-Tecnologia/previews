What you see from Manteo

You can see the ever-changing marsh grasses, the calm waters of Shallowbag Bay, the Roanoke Island Marshes Lighthouse, the boardwalk that links the waterfront area, the Elizabeth II sailing ship right across the bay and in the distance the thin line of the beach island.

At sunset the glow across the water makes everything shimmer.
