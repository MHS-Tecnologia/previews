Do it once, do it right.

A promise kept since 1986. That is forty growth rings, one for every year.

In 1986, Alan Metzler went to work with a pickup truck, a John Deere 640 cable skidder, a strong work ethic and a commitment to a principle of unwavering integrity. Alan and Jill Metzler founded the company on those principles.

Call 717-667-2924

Forty years of growth

In the early 1990s, Metzler pioneered in-woods chipping throughout Pennsylvania. That success paved the way for land clearing, excavation, forest products transportation, and mulch and firewood production.

Today Metzler employs more than 75 full-time employees and is one of Pennsylvania's premier forest products companies. From residential to commercial, from large job to small, every job matters and every detail counts.

  • Professionalism
  • Integrity
  • Innovation
  • Accountability

Safety first, and foremost

The number one priority on the job is the safety of the crews and the security of the worksite: crews look out for each other, stay in constant communication and plan each part of the job for safe and timely completion.

Staff and crews are regularly certified and trained in industry programs, and the safety record is verified by contractor certification programs.

  • Sustainable Forestry Initiative (SFI)
  • MSHA
  • OSHA
  • First Aid
  • CPR
  • ISNetworld
  • Avetta
  • BROWZ
