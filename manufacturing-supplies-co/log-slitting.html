Fully automatic lathe slitting, single and multi-shaft, for adhesive and non-adhesive tape, foam and rubber, textiles, foils, protection films, laminates, graphite and roofing membranes.

Four kinds of slitter

FEBA has engineered roll converting systems near Milan for more than 40 years. MSC is its North American agent.

  • Single knife log slitters

    FEBA SL and EMDS, IMESA single knife

    The blade finds the log’s outside diameter by sonar and centres itself by laser, whatever the log size or how much the blade has been ground.

  • Multi-shaft log slitters

    FEBA and IMESA multi-shaft

    High-volume slitting of adhesive and double-sided tapes, foam tapes, PVC dielectric tapes, polyester tapes, protection films and graphite.

  • Four-shaft twin blade lathe slitter

    FEBA 4L 400

    High-capacity automatic slitting of double-coated, transfer, foam, polyester, duct and masking tapes. The cycle is recipe driven.

  • Sawing systems

    FEBA single and 3-head toothed blade

    Cuts logs of PE and PPL protection tapes, coated and uncoated plastic films and silicone paper.

How the FEBA 4L 400 runs

A log container holds about 22 logs. The next two logs load automatically, and the cycle repeats from a stored recipe.
