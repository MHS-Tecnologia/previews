Gunflint Trail lakeside cabins

Five lakeside housekeeping cabins on the edge of the Boundary Waters, 47 miles outside of Grand Marais. We’re the only private property on Round Lake, so it feels like your own Boundary Waters cabin.

What comes with a cabin

  • A full kitchen (pots, pans, cooking utensils, dishes and silverware) and a full bathroom
  • Sheets, towels, kitchen utensils and firewood
  • A complimentary aluminum canoe; motor boat rental is available
  • Two docks and a sandy beach for swimming
  • Small charcoal grills on request (no fire pit or open burning on the property)
  • Breakfast in our dining hall, if you let us know the day before

Fish Round Lake for walleye, smallmouth bass and northern pike, or portage into neighboring lakes for brook, rainbow and lake trout.

Day trips from your cabin

A few of the paddles and hikes we send cabin guests on. Maps and pamphlets are in the office.

B.A. Point

Easy to Moderate – 1 hour round trip – no permit

Just past Cabin 2’s front porch. The trail hugs the shoreline of Round Lake out to a granite point: fish along the way, swim, string up a hammock. Pink lady slippers in spring, blueberries in mid summer.

Tuscarora Lake

Easy to Moderate – 6 miles – Free Day Use Permit

Paddle away from our dock and take two portages into Tuscarora Lake, for some fine lake trout fishing. One portage is a whopping 428 rods, and there’s a t-shirt in our Trading Post for those who finish it.

Snipe Lake Loop

Moderate – 5 miles – Free Day Use Permit

Seven lakes and six portages in a day. It starts 300 yards up our driveway and ends right back on our dock. Paddle quietly on Cross Bay and Snipe to catch a glimpse of a moose.

Magnetic Rock Trail

Easy – 3 miles round trip – Free Day Use Permit

Overlooks, spring wildflowers and summer berries, to a 60 foot monolith said to drive compasses wild. The trailhead is about a mile up the Gunflint Trail.

Little Rock Falls – Granite River

Easy – 4 miles – Free Day Use Permit

From Gunflint Lake, follow the international border to a waterfall with a good swimming hole at the bottom. Bring a book and a picnic.

Reserving a cabin

  1. Send a request with the cabin, your arrival and departure dates and the size of your group.
  2. We email you within 24 hours with instructions on how to complete your reservation.
  3. Your reservation is confirmed when we receive your deposit payment.

Three-night minimum stay in the cabins, seven nights in the Lodge. Check-in is any time after 3:00 pm; check-out is any time before 10:00 am. Pets in Cabins 2 & 3 only.
