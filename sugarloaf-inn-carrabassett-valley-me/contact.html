Getting here

From Portland, about 2¼ hours

Take I-95 North toward Augusta, then Exit 112B for ME-27 North toward Farmington. Continue on ME-27 North through Kingfield into Carrabassett Valley. Sugarloaf Access Road is on your left.

Via I-95 Exit 75

Take I-95 North into Maine, then Exit 75 for ME-4 North to Farmington. In Farmington, follow signs for ME-27 North toward Carrabassett Valley and turn left onto Sugarloaf Access Road.

Before you call

Is there a pool or hot tub?

Not at the Inn, but the Sugarloaf Sports and Fitness Center, a 3-minute walk away, has both.

Will the Brew Haus be open during my stay?

The Shipyard Brew Haus operates from mid-December to mid-April and during Homecoming Weekend. If your dates aren’t listed on their website, assume it’s closed.

Is breakfast included?

No. The Brew Haus serves breakfast during its season.

Are you pet friendly?

Inn rooms are not, but the townhouse option allows dogs.

Are there accessible rooms?

Yes, one ADA-compliant room (Room 103) on the 1st floor. It is located beneath the bar.

Can I bring an air mattress?

Due to fire marshal restrictions, air mattresses are not allowed. Cots are available for $35 per night.

What discounts are available?
  • Season pass holders: 10% during the season for any pass that includes Sugarloaf (e.g. Ikon, New England).
  • Groups booking 4+ rooms.

Call the sales concierge to book with these offers.

What is there to do in the off season?
  • Mountain biking and hiking
  • Kayaking on the Carrabassett
  • Flagstaff Lake: swimming, boating, paddleboarding
  • Antigravity Center: skate park, trampolines, climbing wall, basketball and gym
  • Zipline tours and axe throwing
  • Sugarloaf Sports and Fitness Center: pool, hot tubs, sauna, steam room
