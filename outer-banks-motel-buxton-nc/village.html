Village Cottages

A mile down the road from the motel, in the heart of Buxton village: more than a dozen one-, two- and three-bedroom cottages, several of them right on the canal.

Call (252) 995-5601Email obmotel@aol.com

Village living

Privacy, without giving up the shops, restaurants and attractions that give Buxton village its charm. Some cottages are efficiencies, some have full kitchens and living rooms, and some are standard rooms. On the canal you might fish, kayak or explore on a standup paddleboard.

These cottages bring you privacy and a touch of Hatteras Island history. Guests enjoy all the amenities at the motel: the saltwater pool, hot tub, library with books and puzzles, fish-cleaning station, free WiFi in the motel lobby and coin laundry.

Groups welcome. Most of the three-bedroom cottages can accommodate up to eight people. Three bedrooms not enough? Book a full duplex.

Find your cottage

All village units are about a mile from the beach: too far to walk for most people, but a quick drive to the beach access at the motel complex. To book a specific cottage, call (252) 995-5601.
