What the harness team does

Work with qualified engineers to produce wire and cable harnesses to your requirements, including wire-to-PCB.

All 12 capabilities
  • Custom cable and wire harness assemblies
  • Crimping, automated and manual
  • Soldered connections, leaded and lead-free
  • Heat shrink and braided sleeving
  • Multi-conductor cable processing
  • Wire cutting, stripping and labeling
  • Custom labeling and barcoding

  • Full traceability and serialization
  • Same-day and quick-turn prototypes

For defense programs

The IPS Defense Business Unit builds mil-spec cable and harness assemblies to IPC/WHMA-A-620 with M22759 wire and MIL-DTL connectors: sensor and pigtail harnesses, special-purpose electrical cable assemblies and power distribution wiring, built to print from government drawings.

ITAR registration and the defense record

Harnesses go into the box, too

IPS integrates the harnesses it builds into complete units: internal cable and harness routing and termination, with continuity and pull testing for harnessed units.
