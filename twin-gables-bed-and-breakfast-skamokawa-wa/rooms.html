Agnes’s Room and Edna’s Room

Our rooms are named for the daughters of Andrew and Hilma Peterson, who built and lived in their home on Skamokawa Creek in the 1890s. Breakfast is included for guests staying in the inn.

Call 360-795-3942Email us

Agnes’s Room

$140 per night not including taxes

  • Queen size bed
  • Bedside tables
  • Storage chest at the foot of the bed
  • The bathroom has a private shower. Sink is in the bedroom.
  • This room looks out over Skamokawa Creek
  • Complimentary conditioner, soap, and hair dryer
  • Free WiFi

Edna’s Room

$150 per night not including taxes

  • Four poster queen bed with high quality cotton linens
  • Bedside tables
  • Clothes wardrobe
  • Table & chairs
  • Chest of drawers
  • Private bathroom with shower
  • 40″ flat screen Internet connected TV with an online movie subscription
  • This room looks out over Skamokawa Creek
  • Complimentary conditioner, soap, and hair dryer
  • Free WiFi

In both rooms

Fresh ground and brewed coffee will be delivered outside your door an hour before breakfast. Breakfast will be served at the time you choose.

Hot beverages available 24 hours, and you’ll find a welcoming snack in your room on arrival.

For one of the two inn rooms, or the vacation rental apartment next door, call or send a message.
