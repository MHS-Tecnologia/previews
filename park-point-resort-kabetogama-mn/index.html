A roadless park you explore by boat

Kabetogama lies in the center of Voyageurs National Park, 219,000 acres of water and wilderness reached only by boat or hiking trail. Its rugged shoreline runs 20 miles long and 6 miles wide, and Namakan, Sand Point and Crane Lake are all within reach by boat.

Walleyes, northerns, smallmouth bass and crappies abound. Deer, bear and moose roam freely, and eagles soar above the tall pines.
