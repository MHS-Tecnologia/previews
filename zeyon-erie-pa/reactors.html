Size matters

Cost efficiency and the manufacturing of your reactor largely depend on readily available raw materials. Choosing a common size can increase your chance of corporate approvals and financing. Our expertise is turning your project requirements into the best standard design, to reduce your costs and lead times.

Options and accessories

Pilot plant, autoclave and lab-scale reactor systems and components, in carbon steel, stainless steel or most exotic alloys.

  • Interior finishes from standard mill, glass bead blasted, electropolished or several mechanical polish finishes
  • Flanged and bolted or quick-opening top closures
  • Stand options: fixed, raise and lower, swing-out
  • Rupture disk and holder
  • Explosion-proof AC motor with mounted gear reducer and explosion-proof variable frequency controller
  • Mixing baffles and internal cooling coil
  • Magnetically or mechanically coupled mixers with stainless steel shaft and a wide range of impellers
