Around Lake Luzerne

Kastner's sits in the heart of dude ranch country, inside the Adirondack Park, minutes from Lake George.

Close by

Riding is popular here, and saddle horses can be had nearby. There are bike routes and hiking trails in every direction, and public golf courses in Lake Luzerne and Lake George.

White water rafting runs from several spots in Lake Luzerne. Saratoga Springs, with its thoroughbred racing, is a thirty-minute drive.

Eating out

Delis and restaurants are within walking distance. On Route 9N it takes less than ten minutes to reach the well-known places to eat in Lake George and Lake Luzerne, and there is entertainment and dancing nearby every night.
