Paying

Fees are due on arrival. Pay at the office, with the camp host after hours, or online. We take credit and debit cards, checks with proper identification, and cash.

Cabins need a security deposit on a credit card, and a $200 damage deposit is taken at check-in. Cabin rates

Directions from I-70

  1. Take exit 295, US-77/KS-18 W, from either direction.
  2. Go south on US-77 for about 100 yards.
  3. Turn right (west) on Old Highway 40.
  4. We are on the right, just past Champions Car Wash and AA Self Storage.
