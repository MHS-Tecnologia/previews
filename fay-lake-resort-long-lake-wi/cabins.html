Off-season daily rates

  • Cabins: $100 a night for 2 people, plus $10 a night for each extra person
  • Townhouse: $125 a night for 2 people, plus $10 a night for each extra person

Peak season is Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend, plus gun deer hunting season.

Boats and extras

  • A 14-foot rowboat comes with every rental
  • Extra boat: $20 a day, $50 a week
  • Motor: $35 a day, $120 a week
  • Extra boat and motor: $45 a day, $145 a week
  • Pets: $60 a week or $10 a day

Pontoon rentals

  • 10 people, 1,750 lb: $30 an hour, $100 for 4 hours, $150 a day
  • 7 people, 1,400 lb: $25 an hour, $85 for 4 hours, $125 a day
  • 5 people, 1,125 lb: $25 an hour, $85 for 4 hours, $125 a day

Reduced rates for multiple days on the pontoons.

Good to know

  • Check in 2:00 pm, check out 9:00 am
  • Weekly rentals are Saturday to Saturday
  • 25% deposit. It is returned, less a $25 handling charge, if the vacancy is filled completely
  • Beds are made up with linens, blankets and pillows. Bring towels, soap and paper goods
  • Guests may be placed in a comparable unit to fit a longer stay
