Cottages & rates

All the cottages have fully equipped kitchens (refrigerator, stove, microwave, toaster, coffee-maker, dishes, utensils), bathrooms, linens, bath towels, porches and outdoor barbecue grills. All are hypoallergenic, smoke-free and pet-free, with non-feather pillows. The numbers match the camp map.

Open May 20th to November 1st. During the summer, weekly rentals only.

2024 rate schedule. Spring & fall: May 20 to June 21 and Sept. 1 to Oct. 27. Summer season: June 22 to August 31. $35 each additional person per night, $50 per week. No charge for children 10 and under. All prices may be subject to change without notice.

Good to know

Tax

New York State requires an 8% tax on stays of less than one week. No tax is required on stays of one week or longer.

Smoking and pets

For your health and comfort, smoking and pets are not permitted.

Cancellation policy, summer season

A reservation is firm once payment of one half the total amount has been received, due by January 31st. The balance is due upon arrival. For cancellations made before May 1st, your deposit will be refunded less a $50 handling fee. For cancellations made after May 1st, the deposit will be retained unless the unit can be rented. Guests will be charged the full amount of the reservation if they fail to cancel and don't arrive.

Cancellation policy, fall and spring seasons

A reservation is firm once payment of one half the total amount has been received, due upon receipt of your invoice. The balance is due upon arrival. For cancellations made more than 30 days prior to your reservation, your deposit will be refunded less a $50 handling fee. For cancellations of less than 30 days, your deposit will be retained unless the unit can be rented. Guests will be charged the full amount of the reservation if they fail to cancel and don't arrive.
