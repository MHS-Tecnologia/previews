Four rooms, each with its own bath

When you arrive, you are personally met and escorted to your room.

All room details and rates

Grape Harbor Room

Second floor. King-size bed, an electric fireplace with mantle, and a décor that honors the local marine and grape history.

Maple View Room

Overlooking the 180+ year-old sugar maples. King-size bed, antique furniture and an electric wood-fire stove.

Garden Room

Floral décor, a queen-size bed and an antique settee. You set the lighting bright and cheerful or low.

Heritage Room

First floor, with its own entrance from the portico. Queen-size pillow-top bed and an electric fireplace in the original 1860 fireplace box.

Need some ‘down’ time?

Whether you arrive frayed from a hectic schedule or just want a little rest, we want you to leave refreshed.

  • Curl up on the chaise couch with a good book or DVD
  • Browse our early 1900s magazines
  • Get cozy around the living room fireplace
  • Walk two acres of park-like grounds, or through the neighboring vineyards
  • Free wireless internet, board and card games

Mornings

A hot breakfast, served fresh at 8:30 in our dining room: farm-fresh eggs and bacon, fresh fruit, home-made breads and pastries. Our own signature-blend coffee, herbal teas and flavored cocoas are there all through your stay.

Two far infrared dry saunas

Commercial-spa-grade: you will not have an experience like this in any other area B&B. Each 20-minute session brings deep, penetrating heat to melt away stress and ease aches. Available year round, for adults 18 and older.
