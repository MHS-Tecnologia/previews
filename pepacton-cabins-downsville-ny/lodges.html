Two lodges and a loft

For bigger groups: Pakatakan and Waconia sleep up to 9 guests each, and Onteora Loft is great for 3 to 4. All on our private road, Hood Lane.

Pakatakan Lodge

A riverside log cabin lodge, 24 by 38 feet, with 2 bedrooms on a peaceful and serene acre, surrounded by the Catskill Mountains on our private road.

  • Kitchen with stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, pots, pans and dishes
  • Dining room with table and chairs, and a futon that makes into a double bed
  • Living room with sleeper sofa, couch and satellite TV
  • Creel bedroom and bedroom 2, each with a double bed and a rollaway bed
  • Bathroom with tub and shower, towels, soap and paper products
  • Screened in porch and covered porch with chairs
  • Picnic table, BBQ grill and fire-pit
  • Up to 9 guests. Pet friendly.

$225 a night for 2 guests. Each additional guest $50. Plus 10% tax.

Six night special: $1,200 for 2 guests. Each additional guest $300. Plus 10% tax. Check-in Mondays to Thursdays.

Waconia Lodge, 95 Hood Lane.

Waconia Lodge

A wooded cabin that overlooks the pond, with a great view of the mountains, a large deck and a screened back porch.

  • 3 bedrooms: a king size bed in the loft bedroom, a queen and a full downstairs
  • Kitchen with stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker and toaster
  • Living room with a gas log fireplace and a full size pullout sofa bed
  • Bathroom with a large shower
  • Deck with table, benches and chairs. Picnic table, charcoal BBQ and fire pit.
  • Up to 9 guests. Pet friendly.

$225 a night for up to 2 guests. Each additional guest $50. Plus 10% tax.

Six night special: $1,200 for up to 2 guests. Each additional guest $300. Plus 10% tax. Check-in Mondays to Thursdays.

The loft has wrap-around decks.

Winter, and fall over the pond.

Onteora Loft

“Land in the Sky” is our loft in the barn: log siding and a knotty pine interior, with antique stippled glass church windows and hand hewn hooks.

  • 3 separate sleeping areas with all the bedding and quilts, great for 3 to 4 guests
  • Downstairs bedroom with a double bed and sliding doors to the deck
  • Circular stairs to the loft bedroom, with a double bed
  • Living room with a convertible sofa double bed and a green enamel gas log stove, thermostatically controlled
  • Kitchen with island, stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave, toaster oven, toaster and coffee maker
  • Bathroom with a large shower. Satellite TV and ceiling fans.
  • Covered porch with swing and chairs, and a large deck with a view of the pond and the mountains

$175 a night for 2 guests. Each additional guest $50.

Six night special: $900 for 2 guests. Each additional guest $225. Additional nights at the single night rate. Check-ins Monday to Friday.

Lodge guests and pets

  • Daily guest fee $20. You must have permission.
  • We do not charge for children under the age of 3.
  • Dogs $20, $10 for each additional dog. Cats $10, $5 for each additional cat.
