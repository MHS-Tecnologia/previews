Started from cuttings, right here.

Most of our nursery stock is started from small cuttings, rooted and grown to saleable size at the nursery. Two plants on our list were developed here.

Developed by Schulte's

Uniform branching, so pruning is unnecessary. Intense blue needles for color wherever it's planted.

Something wrong with a plant? Bring us a leaf.

Bring in a sample of the affected leaves. One of our staff will help diagnose insect damage or disease and work out a treatment plan with you.

  • Planters. Bring your containers or pick our pottery, and we'll help design and plant them.
  • Delivery. Trees, shrubs and plants, for occasions or for convenience.
  • Old pots. Bring your plastic pots and flats. We recycle them.
  • Warranty. A tree, shrub or evergreen that fails in its first year is replaced once.
