Lakeside campground

Nestled in the woods along the back bay of South Manistique Lake, with its own dock. A variety of sites, from tent camping up to 40-foot RVs. Bring your kayaks, canoes or boats.

Call (906) 586-6761

$40.00 per day

Every site includes

  • Water and electric, 20 amp service
  • Bathhouse with bathrooms and showers
  • Dump station
  • Picnic table and fire pit
  • Sandy beach
  • Lake views

Also yours to use

  • Volleyball nets
  • Playground
  • Fish house
  • Campground dock

Swim, boat, jet ski, water ski, relax by the lake, cook out, and sit beside a crackling campfire. Cash and checks only.
