What they do for homes

  • Glass and screen

    New or repaired. Wood, aluminum and vinyl windows, single glaze and insulated units, tempered and annealed glass.

  • Custom shower doors

    New custom shower doors make a bathroom more beautiful and more useful, and add value to the home.

  • Mirrors

    Pick from the displays or have a custom piece made: plate, framed or beveled.

  • Windows

    Replacement glass and replacement windows.

  • Storm doors and windows

    Repair or replacement of storm inserts, or of the entire window or door.

  • Patio screens and patio doors

    Most vinyl, wood and aluminum-framed screens and doors, or a custom build.

  • Screened-in porch

    Re-screening on site, or bring the screens in. Custom screen enclosure systems, built to fit.

  • Furniture glass

    Table tops, desks and custom shapes.

  • Picture frame glass

    Non-glare reflection control glass, cut to fit.

  • Custom projects

    Light fixtures, smoothing out chips, specialty items and more. Call with the piece in mind.

  • Art glass

    Restoration and repair of stained glass and art glass, plus custom design. Koonse sends this work to Village Glass, which has been creating art glass in Columbia since 1976.
