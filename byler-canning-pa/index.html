Fresh·ol·o·gy

noun

The belief that the only ingredients worth putting in your body come straight from the farm, with no additives, preservatives or high sodium.

See also: pure, natural, goodness.

  1. The freshest ingredients

    Nothing frozen or pre-canned. Most of it comes straight from the fields of local growers.

  2. Inspected and cleaned by hand

    The team cleans the stems, cuts out any bad spots and washes every ingredient by hand. Every piece, every time.

  3. Cooked in small batches

    Big batches mean longer cook times and duller flavor. Small batches in an open kettle keep it bright.
