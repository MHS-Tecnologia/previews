A very cool campground

Sites right on Lake Superior, and many more tucked in the woods. Two beautiful beaches on half a mile of shoreline that everyone can use, and the Cross River running through the middle, in which kids and fish often swim.

At every site, and around the campground

On your site

A fire ring and a picnic table. There is no grate, so bring one if you plan to cook over the fire. Lamb's sells firewood and ice: wood is $7 a bundle plus tax in 2027.

Around the campground

A bathhouse with flush toilets, sinks and hot showers. Wi-Fi near the office, an arcade, a playground and a gift shop. Electricity in the campground is 30 amp.

Good to know

  • Some tent sites fit 2 tents, some only 1. An extra tent or car on a site costs $10 a night.
  • No sleeping tents on an RV site already rented by a trailer, pop-up or class A. Screen tents are fine.
  • Pets are welcome: register them, keep them leashed and never unattended, and clean up after them. $10 per stay.
  • Quiet time starts at 10 pm every night. No fireworks.
  • Payment in full when you reserve, by MasterCard, Visa or Discover. Reservations are for three nights or longer; same-day reservations for one night only.
  • Cancel with 30 days' notice for a full refund, less a $10 fee.
