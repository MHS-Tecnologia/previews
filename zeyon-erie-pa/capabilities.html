Supercritical fluid extraction

A supercritical fluid (SCF) is any substance at a temperature and pressure above its critical point, where distinct liquid and gas phases do not exist. It can effuse through solids like a gas, and dissolve materials like a liquid.

Zeyon builds custom SCF extraction equipment tailored to your needs: reliable, scalable, and an integral part of your continuous operation.

  • Designed and manufactured in accordance with ASME and CSA Standards for Boiler, Pressure Vessel and Pressure Piping Code
  • Vessels stamped with a CRN and NB registration number, confirming they meet code and have been examined by an Authorized Inspector
