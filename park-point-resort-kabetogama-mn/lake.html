Explore, fish or relax

Park Point Resort is on Kabetogama Lake in Voyageurs National Park, 219,000 acres of water and wilderness.

The lake

Lake Kabetogama is 20 miles long and 6 miles wide. It lies in the center of Voyageurs National Park, a roadless park accessible only by boat or hiking trails. Its shoreline is rugged and irregular and dotted with over 200 islands.

Namakan, Sand Point and Crane Lake are accessible by boat from Lake Kabetogama, with plenty of room for exploring, hiking, blueberry picking and watching wildlife in its natural habitat. Deer, bear and moose roam freely, and eagles soar above the tall pines.

For birders, 95 species inhabit the park, including osprey and pileated, black-backed and three-toed woodpeckers.

Fishing

Lake Kabetogama is great for fishing. Walleyes, northerns, smallmouth bass and crappies abound.

The Kabetogama peninsula is only minutes away by boat, with largemouth and muskie fishing, great hiking trails and blueberry picking.

"Outdoors Weekly ranked Kabetogama a top 10 walleye fishing destination in the Midwest!"As posted on the Park Point home page

At the resort

  • Fish cleaning and wrapping
  • Guide service
  • Guided boat tours
  • Water taxi to hiking trails, Ellsworth Rock Gardens and Kettle Falls Hotel
  • Bait, gas and ice
  • Firewood for purchase
  • Small protected sandy beach
  • Canoes and kayaks, free

Voyageurs National Park also runs boat trips to Kettle Falls, island cruises, campfire programs, guided canoe trips and beaver pond walks. The park's visitor center has the details.

When the snow flies

Aurora, cabin 2, is now winterized. Snowmobiling, ice fishing and cross country skiing are just a few of the things to do in Voyageurs National Park in winter.
