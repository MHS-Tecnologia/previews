Timber harvesting

For landowners who want to monetize the timber on their property, Metzler's professional foresters build a plan around your goals, for value, wildlife and recreation.

  1. Meet on siteTalk through your goals for the timber and how the process works.
  2. Cruise the landForesters measure the species, health and quantity of the timber.
  3. Plan the harvestA plan for a healthier forest and better road access, using Metzler's market relationships to maximize your return.
  4. Retire the job siteEvery landing is seeded and the property returned to its pre-harvesting state, or better.

Metzler now makes high-carbon biochar

Making biochar at Metzler sequesters carbon and cuts the propane used at the firewood heat-treating facility. In the ground, biochar helps soil hold water and nutrients; in a barn or coop, it reduces moisture and ammonia.

It is made for Central Pennsylvania's soils, to help farmers and growers build healthier soil.

Do it once, do it right.

Metzler's promise since 1986. That is forty growth rings, one for every year.

In 1986, Alan Metzler went to work with a pickup truck, a John Deere 640 cable skidder, a strong work ethic and a commitment to integrity. In the early 1990s, Metzler pioneered in-woods chipping in Pennsylvania, which led to land clearing, excavation, trucking, and mulch and firewood production.

Today Metzler employs more than 75 full-time people.
