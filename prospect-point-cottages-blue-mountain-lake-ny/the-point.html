Life on the Point

At Prospect Point, there is no regimen or set time; instead, an atmosphere of relaxation and warmth pervades.

At every cottage

  • Porch, grill, picnic table and campfire ring
  • Fully equipped kitchen: oven, stove, full-sized fridge, microwave, toaster, coffee maker, dishes, pots, pans and cutlery
  • All linens and bath towels
  • Free cable TV in your cottage, free wireless internet in designated areas
  • Some have fireplaces
  • Many cottages are wheelchair friendly; Moose Lodge is fully wheelchair accessible
  • Hypoallergenic, smoke-free and pet-free, with non-feather pillows

On the Point

  • Private sandy beach, 150 feet long
  • Canoes, rowboats and paddle boats, free to all guests
  • Badminton, horseshoes and lawn games on the hillside lawn
  • A map of nearby hiking trails, on request
  • Community room with piano, fireplace and library

Kick off your shoes, pull a book from the shelf, and enjoy an evening by the fire while friends tickle the ivories on the vintage 19th-century Steinway.

Around the lake

  • Tennis is right in town and free for all to enjoy: just bring your own balls and rackets.
  • The Adirondack Lakes Center for the Arts hosts concerts, classes, plays and exhibits.
  • The Adirondack Museum, called "the best of its kind in the world" by the NY Times, is just across the lake.
  • A guided historic cruise down the chain of lakes in an antique powerboat departs several times daily from the Boat Livery right in town. You can also rent a power boat there, a short walk up the road.
  • Great Camp Sagamore, the northwoods retreat of the Vanderbilts and a National Historic Landmark, offers tours.
  • Sightseeing, buffet or dinner cruises aboard the W W Durant on nearby Raquette Lake.
  • The Wild Center, natural history museum of the Adirondacks, on Tupper Lake: just 30 minutes.
  • Seaplane flights over the region with Helms Aero Service on Long Lake, only 11 miles away.

So come!

  • Swimthe spring-fed waters of a crystal-clear lake.
  • Fishfor bass, lake trout, brook trout and salmon.
  • Sailpast picturesque islands of birch and cedar.
  • Hikeup Castle Rock or Blue Mountain, or take a leisurely trail through the deep forest.
  • Dineat charming restaurants in the lovely hamlets of the park.
  • Shopfor rustic treasures.

At Prospect Point, the wilderness is never far away. It is just outside your cabin door . . .
