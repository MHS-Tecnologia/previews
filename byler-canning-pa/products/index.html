Also in the shop.

The rest of the line sits in these aisles of the Byler store: more relishes, hot and extra hot pepper mustard, savory spreads and sauce, fruit butters and marmalade, low-sodium pickled favorites and specialty products.

Low sodium, no MSG and no added preservatives. Unopened jars keep up to three years on the shelf; once open, keep them in the fridge and use within 6 to 8 weeks for the best flavor.
