Inside unit 612

Built in 2005, on the 6th floor at the west end: 9 ft ceilings, 1,950 sq ft of living area, 3 bedrooms and 2 baths. Tuscany Beige travertine in the French pattern throughout, no carpet, a TV and DVD player in the great room and every bedroom, and free wireless internet.

Great roomDiningKitchenMaster bedroomBedroom 2Bedroom 3Baths and laundry

Great room

Bright and open to the sugar-white sand and emerald water. A 55” 1080p TV, a Windham fireplace console with four flame settings, and a sofa that opens into a queen Tempur-Pedic bed.

Dining

Tuscany Beige travertine tile throughout, laid in the French pattern design.

Kitchen

A full kitchen with everything needed for cooking: stainless steel appliances and granite counter tops.

Master bedroom

Gulf front, with a door to the balcony. A king Tempur-Pedic bed with 700 thread count sheets, end tables, wall mirror, large flat screen TV and a full size armoire.

Bedroom 2

Decorated in an Italian Missoni design. King bed with 700 thread count sheets, end table, flat screen TV and chest. It opens onto a back balcony with a view of the bays and a private sitting area.

Bedroom 3

King bed with 700 thread count sheets, chest, flat screen TV and end table. It also opens onto the back balcony.

Baths and laundry

Two baths. The guest rooms each have their own entrance to the bath. A washer and dryer in the laundry room.
