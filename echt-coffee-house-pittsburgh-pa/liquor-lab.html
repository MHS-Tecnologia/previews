Lower level

Liquor Lab and Tasting Room

Head down the spiral staircase to the lower level and you will find the liquor lab. Stop by at the right moment and you can watch the distilling. The hand-crafted liquor and the cocktails are served upstairs in the lounge; the tasting room is available for events.

See the cocktailsBook the tasting room

Made in the lab

The bar keeper Brad, James and Jorg all contribute to the distilling. ECHT’s rum and whiskey are ageing in the liquor lab, in American oak barrels.

For the Pennsylvania Rye Whiskey, ECHT works with Nate, Jenna and Ryan of Vellum Fermentation on the South Side Flats and with Chris and Jason of Firewhistle Brewing in Elizabeth, PA; for the barley whiskey, with 11th Hour in Lawrenceville. The malted heritage rye and barley come from farmers and CNC Malting in Butler County, so there is a Pennsylvania Rye Whiskey and a matching Rye Beer from the same local farmer’s rye harvest.

The bitters and amaro are made here too, on ECHT’s Ohio and Pennsylvania grain vodka, and turn up in the cocktails.

Our interpretations of classic cocktails

  • ECHT New Orleans Fizz, and we do it whiskey based$15ECHT white whiskey, frozen lemon and lime ice cubes, brown sugar, heavy cream, egg white, a drop of our orange amaro and club soda. Dry-shaken for a minute to emulsify, wet-shaken for four, then the “rising soufflé”: the rest is poured gently through the middle of the foam until it rises over the rim without collapsing. Served with a straw laid on top.
