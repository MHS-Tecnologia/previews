Lemon Asparagus Hummus

Level
Beginner
Cook time
15 minutes
Servings
24 (2 tbsp each)

Made with Byler’s Pickled Asparagus. Serve it as a hummus duo with Beet Hummus.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed well
  • ½ to 1 jar Byler’s Relish House Pickled Asparagus
  • 2 medium cloves garlic
  • ½ cup fresh parsley
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp tahini (sesame paste)
  • 1 lemon, juice and zest
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Remove the asparagus from the jar and pat it dry with a lint-free towel or paper towel.
  2. Add all the ingredients to a food processor and process until smooth.
  3. Carefully move it from the food processor to a serving bowl.
  4. Garnish with asparagus spears and a slice of lemon.
