Where a kitchen budget goes

The average upscale kitchen remodel can cost anywhere from $50K to $80K or more, depending on features. Here is the approximate breakdown we give homeowners when they plan.

  • Cabinets & hardware30%
  • Design, labor & installation25%
  • Appliances15%
  • Countertops10%
  • Flooring10%
  • Lighting5%
  • Plumbing5%

Costly mistakes to plan around

  • Appliances: improper clearances and placement
  • Electrical: incorrect circuits, GFCIs and panels
  • Cabinets: obstacles due to wrong placement
  • Countertops: wrong traffic and seating clearance
  • Plumbing: code violations and fixture placement
  • Flooring: clear space errors
  • Design and labor: a poor quality quote and consult
