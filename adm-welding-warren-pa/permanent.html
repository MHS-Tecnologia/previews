Permanent vehicular bridges

Tailored to customer requirements.

  • Designed/fabricated to order in nearly any width, capacity, and lengths up through 100’.
  • Lower purchase price than a comparably sized portable bridge.
  • Treated timber deck system, typically 6” thick.
  • Minimal installation time compared to traditional stick-built bridges: typically less than one day for single-lane bridges under 70’ long, 2-3 days for bridges over 70’.

Pedestrian & light duty bridges

Designed for a variety of uses and aesthetic considerations.

Walking paths, hiking trails, golf course cart paths and multi-use trails. Widths and lengths below are from bridges ADM has installed.

Overlays of existing bridges

Quick, economical, effective.

Temporary overlays are ideal when a short-term capacity increase is needed, and allow roads to remain open when deterioration forces existing bridges to be closed.

Permanent overlays reduce time and expense compared to complete replacement of existing bridges.
