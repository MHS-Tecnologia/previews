Policies and what to pack

Stays

  • Check-in after 4pm, check-out before 9am
  • Last minute reservations, when available: 3 night minimum, within the Saturday to Saturday schedule
  • 50% deposit confirms a reservation; deposits are non-refundable if the vacancy cannot be refilled
  • All major credit cards accepted (4% convenience fee)
  • 7.375% sales tax, 3% lodging tax
  • Rates are subject to change as the cabins are updated

Extra guests and pets

  • Additional guests age 12+: $150/week, $40/day
  • Additional guests age 5 to 11: $75/week, $25/day
  • Pets welcome: $150/week, $30/day; more than one pet an additional $10/day; 2 pet maximum per cabin
  • No smoking in cabins. Smokers are welcome outside.

Things provided

  • Garbage bags, toilet tissue, hand soap
  • All bedding (pillows not supplied)
  • Fully equipped kitchen, coffee makers, large crock pots, microwave
  • Gas grill

Things to bring

  • Pillows (not supplied)
  • Bath towels, dish towels, paper towels
  • Binoculars and cameras
  • Camp chairs
Park Point dog policy

Dogs are leashed at all times, no exceptions. While we love our furry friends, some guests prefer not to see their happy faces running toward them.

A dog staying in while you are out stays in its kennel. Pets may not roam the cabin or be tied up outside unattended, and are not allowed on furniture or beds.

Please keep barking down, pick up after your dog, and dry and clean a dog that has been in the lake or the mud before it comes inside.

Aggressive pets will not be tolerated, and Park Point does not assume liability for a dog's actions on the resort grounds. A signed pet policy is required on arrival.

Ask about open weeks

Each cabin has its own availability calendar. Call or email and Jason & Robyn will tell you what is open.
