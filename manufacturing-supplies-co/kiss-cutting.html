Five ways to cut flexible material

Every line can be fitted with options for the job: laminate, change or add a liner, emboss, register, remove scrap, eject parts, slit, cross-cut into sets and rewind.

  • Flat-bed die cutting presses

    Guidolin Girotto flat-bed series

    Electro-mechanical presses for narrow web, from 30 to 150 cycles per minute.

  • Rotary die cutting machines

    Guidolin Girotto rotary series

    High-speed rotary converting for adhesive tapes, double-sided materials, foams, plastics, rubber, labels and electronic components.

  • Digital laser die cutting

    PLAS and RO series

    Simple and complex shapes with no hard tooling, and less material waste.

  • P1 plotter laser

    P1 series

    Built for converting 3M, Avery Dennison, Lohmann, NITTO and Tesa double-sided adhesives. Also used to make prototypes.

  • Guillotine cutting systems

    GDX-1

    Cuts sheets and blocks from a roll with a blade and counter blade, and can laminate, change a liner, slit, sheet and rewind.
