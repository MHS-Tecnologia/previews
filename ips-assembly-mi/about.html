From a small electronics operation to a certified EMS plant

To build a skilled engineering team and jumpstart growth, IPS acquired multiple competitors within its first year of operations. Over the decade since, it has expanded its capabilities, modernized its facility and earned ISO 9001, IATF 16949, ITAR and NMSDC credentials.

IPS operates 100% debt free and keeps investing in new equipment, training and capabilities. Its multiple high-output assembly lines handle prototypes, small runs and high-volume production.

The mission has stayed the same: unmatched quality, speed and reliability.

Why a supplier 20 minutes away changes a program

Prototypes are picked up the day they finish. First articles are reviewed on the floor with the engineer who designed the board. An engineering change is a conversation instead of a ticket.

Onshoring is also a supply-chain decision: tariffs, freight and lead-time risk disappear when the build is down the road.
