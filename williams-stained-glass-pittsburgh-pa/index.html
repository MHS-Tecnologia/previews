How a commission is priced

Custom glass is priced by its size in square feet and the intricacy of the design, so a firm price comes with a drawing.

  1. Tell the studio about the opening

    Call, email or stop by. Walk-ins are welcome; an appointment makes sure the right person is there to talk through your project.

  2. Get a scale drawing and a firm price

    Free, with no obligation. After five free designs or revisions, the studio asks for a deposit toward the project.

  3. Choose the glass

    The staff help you pick the design, the glass colours and the method of installation.

  4. Have it installed

    Expert installation across the Tri-State area, with Thermopane options on any project.

Ordering for the holidays? Custom pieces need to be ordered by late September or early October.
