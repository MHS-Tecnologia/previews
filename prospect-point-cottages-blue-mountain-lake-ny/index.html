Right on the water

No need to cross any highways to reach the beach.

Rising from the beach, the broad hillside lawn is perfect for lying in the sun, enjoying a book, having a picnic, or playing a game of ball, horseshoes or badminton. All the cottages have large picture windows and porches overlooking the lake. Most have been completely renovated, with cathedral ceilings, knotty-pine walls and skylights.

Canoes, rowboats and paddle boats are free for guests to use. When you're feeling adventurous, ask for a map of nearby hiking trails, or for their favorite canoe haunts.

Life on the Point

1881

The Prospect House stood on this point

Frederic Durant built it here in 1881: six stories, 300 rooms, a resident orchestra. With the help of Thomas Edison, it was the first hotel in the world with an electric light in every room.
