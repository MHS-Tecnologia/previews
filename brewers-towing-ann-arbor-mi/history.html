One truck in 1963

Now celebrating over 60 years of service.

Dennis Brewer, Ann Arbor native

Dennis Brewer started the company as a 20-year-old entrepreneur in 1963. He had a vision, a modest loan and one truck, and grew it into the road service and recovery business it is today.

In 2002 Brewer expanded the company’s footprint by acquiring Ann Arbor towing company Sakstrup’s Towing, an established operation. As Brewer’s sister company, Sakstrup’s provides an additional 18 tow trucks, flatbeds and service units, available to Brewer’s as needed.

Specializing in heavy duty towing, challenging jobs are routine jobs for Brewer’s.

Brewer’s today

Brewer’s Inc., located in Ann Arbor, is one of the premier road service and recovery companies in the state of Michigan. It is a 24-hour, seven-day-a-week operation for emergency road service and recovery, local and long distance towing, heavy duty towing, private property towing, specialized transporting and equipment moving.

Brewer’s employs 14 professional drivers and nine dispatchers. Drivers average 16 years of experience with the company, and the five most senior drivers average 36 years with Brewer’s. They operate a fleet of 25 road service units, from light duty tow trucks to a 75-ton rotator.
