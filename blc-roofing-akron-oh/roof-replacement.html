When it’s time for a new roof.

If your roof is nearing 20 to 25 years old, or another repair would only buy time, replacement is often the better value. Signs we look for:

  • Shingles that are curling, cracked or missing
  • Widespread hail or wind damage after a storm
  • Rusting or separating seams on metal roofing
  • Decking that feels soft, spongy or uneven
  • Higher heating or cooling bills from poor ventilation

Materials we install.

Roofing systems from Owens Corning, Malarkey, GAF and CertainTeed, chosen to match your home, your budget and Ohio’s changing weather.

Asphalt shingles

The most common choice for Ohio homes: a good balance of cost, durability and design. Traditional three-tab or upgraded architectural styles in many colors.

Metal roofing

Steel, aluminum, zinc or copper. Resists fire, wind and hail, saves energy, and can last 40 to 70 years when properly installed.

Look-alike materials

The look of slate, clay tile or wood shake, made in asphalt or metal, without the weight and freeze-thaw risk of the real thing in our climate.

Questions we’re often asked.

Straight answers from our own FAQ.

How much does a roof replacement cost?

In Ohio a typical replacement often falls between $9,000 and $15,000. Size, material, pitch, tear-off, ventilation upgrades and decking condition all move the number. We don’t price by square footage alone: every estimate is built from an on-site inspection and is fully itemized, so you can compare bids line by line.

Do you only install asphalt shingles?

No. We install asphalt shingles and metal roofs, plus asphalt and metal products made to look like slate, tile or shake.

How long does a replacement take?

Most residential replacements take one to two days. Weather, roof size and steepness, and access to the house can stretch that. We give you a time frame at the estimate and keep you updated if it changes.

How long does a roof last?

A properly installed and maintained asphalt shingle roof can last up to about 20 years. Metal roofs can last 50 years or more with proper care.

Will insurance cover a new roof?

Often, when the damage comes from a sudden event like hail, wind or a falling tree. Age and poor maintenance usually aren’t covered. We help with the claim from start to finish.
