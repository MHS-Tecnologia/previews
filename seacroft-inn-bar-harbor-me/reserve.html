How booking works here

  • Each guest makes their own reservation and deposit. The inn is small and has many repeat guests, so groups are not encouraged.
  • Stays are limited to two weeks a visit.
  • Guests already at the inn have first refusal on their room and dates. To keep them for next year, reserve before leaving.
  • Each month opens for reservations on the first day of the following month.
  • Open rooms are clearly marked on the availability chart, and anyone can reserve them.
  • A one-night reservation is taken only where there is a one-night opening.
