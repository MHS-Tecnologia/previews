The beach is across Highway 61. The forest starts out back.

Across the road is a sand and pebble beach on Lake Superior: walks, rock hunting, picnics, a beach bonfire. Behind the motel is the Superior National Forest.

  • A trail from The Outpost joins the Superior Hiking Trail
  • Kadunce Wayside Park, a short walk away, with its gorges and waterfalls
  • Four state parks and a national monument minutes away
  • A snowshoe trail right out the door
