Vacation rental apartment

Next to, but separate from our Bed and Breakfast Inn, the Twin Gables Vacation Rental Apartment is romantic enough for two and comfortable enough for four. Children are welcome. We also welcome small dogs.

Call 360-795-3942Email us

$140 per day

The unit can accommodate up to four adults and one child. Enjoy the Julia Butler wildlife refuge for the Columbia Whitetail Deer, just around the corner. Cross the street and stroll the sandy beaches of Skamokawa Vista Park, or hike the park’s nature trail.

Breakfast is NOT included in the vacation rental. If you would like to include a breakfast, please make arrangements ahead of time. There is an additional charge for breakfast of $20 per person.

Amenities

  • Wireless internet access
  • Flat screen Internet-ready TV with online movie subscription
  • Outdoor furniture
  • Queen-size four-poster bed in bedroom
  • Ceiling fan in bedroom
  • All sheets and towels
  • Hair dryer
  • Living room includes a queen sleeper sofa and child-size hide-a-bed upholstered chair
  • Dining table and chairs

The kitchen

  • Refrigerator
  • Small four burner range with oven
  • Microwave
  • Coffee maker, toaster, dishes and cooking utensils
  • Coffee and all paper products

Apartment policies

  • We would prefer reservations ahead of time, but not always necessary. Call to check availability.
  • Reservations require a one night’s rental rate deposit to hold your dates.
  • If you need to cancel, your deposit is fully refunded with three days notice.
  • Those responsible for making reservations must be over 21, with valid ID required.
  • Check-in is 3:00 PM; check-out 11:00 AM.
  • Maid service included. Linens changed every third day.
  • Smoking is permitted outside only. Any tobacco smoke detected in the apartment will result in an automatic $500 cleaning and painting fee.
  • We recycle plastics, glass and newspaper.
  • No overnight, non-registered guests allowed.
  • There is a no disturbance policy after 10 P.M.
  • Your security deposit will be returned when you leave after satisfactory inspections.
  • Except for laundry, the property and its contents should be returned to original condition.
  • Cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express accepted.
  • Tax rate: 7.6% sales tax plus 2% local room tax will be added to room rate.
  • Payment in full is required upon arrival. There will be no refund for an early departure.
