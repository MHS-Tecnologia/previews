echt/ɛçʈ/German

Authentic, genuine, substantial

That is what the German word echt means in English, and what ECHT aims for in its coffee roasting, liquor distilling and food preparation.

Built by the ECHT craftsmen

The coffee house showcases the craftsmanship of the ECHT design-build shops in the same building. The cabinetry, shelving, furniture, light fixtures, stairs, railings, windows and kitchen islands are all made in-house by ECHT craftsmen, from the wrought iron staircase to the tables you sit at.

The culinary offerings and the interior both blend the traditional with the modern, inspired by the European coffee houses that serve bistro-style food and liquor.

The people

James and Jorg established the association of ECHT Craftsmen. Their food is inspired by their travels in the US, Europe, Africa and the Middle East. Joe and Kyle serve the bistro-style dishes, in-house roasted espresso drinks and speciality cocktails, and Kyle brings baking into the team.
