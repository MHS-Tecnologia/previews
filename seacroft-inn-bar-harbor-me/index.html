The inn and the neighborhood

A small inn, mostly regulars

Many guests come back every year, so rooms are booked in a particular way.

  • Guests at the inn get first refusal on their room and dates for next year, if they reserve before leaving.
  • Each month opens for reservations on the first day of the following month.
  • Stays are limited to two weeks a visit. Groups are not encouraged.
  • Open rooms are marked on the inn's availability chart, and anyone can reserve them.
