How we handle roofing emergencies

1. Call us

We’ll take your call, listen to what’s happening, and provide immediate guidance.

2. Damage mitigation advice

We can walk you through temporary steps to reduce damage until repairs are made.

3. Scheduling repairs

Depending on severity and conditions, we work to get a crew on-site as soon as possible.

4. Lasting solutions

Once it’s safe, we make permanent repairs with quality materials and workmanship.

When to call right away

  • Active roof leaks or interior water damage
  • Storm damage from hail, wind, or fallen branches
  • Sagging or visibly unsafe areas of your roof
  • Large sections of missing shingles or flashing
  • Anything that looks like it won’t make it through another storm

What “same day” means here

Same-day emergency response is available Monday to Friday, and an emergency fee applies. You get a temporary repair to stop the leak, then a scheduled permanent repair once the roof is safely inspected.

We do not offer same-day emergency service on weekends. After hours or on weekends, we schedule the next available appointment for the following business day.
