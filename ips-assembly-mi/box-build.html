What goes into a box build

Boards, harnesses and final assembly under one roof in Livonia: one supplier and one quality system.

All 12 steps and services
  • PCBA mounting, standoffs and fastening to torque spec
  • Internal cable and harness routing and termination
  • Power supply, display, switch and connector integration
  • Labeling, serialization and barcode traceability
  • Programming and configuration
  • Conformal coating when specified

  • Custom packaging, foam and ESD protection
  • Drop-ship or Kanban delivery
  • Repair, rework and field return support

How a box build runs

A box build starts with a documentation review: mechanical drawings, wiring diagrams, BOM, test procedure and any labeling or packaging spec. The engineering team flags gaps before purchasing starts.

The build then follows a written work instruction with in-process inspection at each stage: PCBA install, harness routing, connector mating, closure, test and final inspection.

Units are serialized, so the board lot, harness lot and test result for any shipped unit can be pulled up later.

Who uses it

  • Industrial equipment makers
  • Automotive & mobility suppliers
  • Defense & government programs
  • Energy & lighting OEMs

Typical projects range from prototype quantities of one to five units through recurring production of hundreds per month.
