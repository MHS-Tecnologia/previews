Chicken Salad

Level
Beginner
Cook time
30 minutes
Servings
4

Made with Byler’s Banana Pepper Relish. Serve it on your favorite salad or bread, or with crackers.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • ½ red onion, small diced
  • 2 stalks celery, small diced
  • 2 carrots, small diced
  • ¼ cup Byler’s Banana Pepper Relish
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • ½ cup mayo
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ cup fresh dill
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Optional: dried cranberries, apples, grapes, nuts or anything else you’d like to add

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, add the mayo, yogurt, garlic powder, salt, pepper and fresh dill. Whisk to combine.
  2. Add the chicken to the same bowl.
  3. Small dice the carrots, celery, onion and banana peppers. A hand chopper or food processor makes it faster.
  4. Add the vegetables to the bowl and toss to combine.
  5. Serve atop your favorite salad or bread, or with crackers.
