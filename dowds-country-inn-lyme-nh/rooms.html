The rooms of the old farmhouse. All three are handicap accessible.

Emerson Room

Once the farmhouse library

Soft blue accents and a queen-size handmade star print quilt of mauve, blue and burgundy. The two-tone blue stencil border comes from the Emerson family home in Manchester, NH.

Sleeps 2Beds Queen

Handicap accessible

Shelburne Room

Once the living room, where the Inn held its first wedding

The former owner was married here at the age of 75. Country green, with a fan stencil from the Shelburne Museum in Vermont, hand-stitched square quilts, two sitting chairs, and a view over the Common.

Sleeps 3Beds King and twin

Handicap accessible

Manchester Room

Once the study

A vine stencil with two-tone berries, based on a design from an old farm in Manchester, NH, that is no longer standing. Burgundy quilts, floral country prints and wingback chairs.

Sleeps 4Beds Two queens

Handicap accessible

Front of house, second floor

Show the 5 rooms

Bowden Room

Once the master bedroom

Named for the previous owner of the property. Antique white with blue stencils, braided blue rugs on the old wooden floors, a country rocker and three large windows overlooking the Lyme Common.

Sleeps 3Beds Queen, plus a twin pullout loveseat

Presidential Suite

Once a bedroom in the original farmhouse

Paintings of former presidents and rich wood detailing, with brown and beige homemade quilts. Overlooks the town Common, with a Keurig for coffee or tea.

Sleeps Up to 6Beds Queen, pullout couch, daybed with trundle

Marshland Room

Named for its stencil

The pattern traces back to the old Marshland Farm in Quechee, VT. Tucked under the third-floor stairwell, with stenciled wildflower bouquets and a pale green and rose quilt.

Sleeps 2Beds Queen

Captain's Quarters

Shell stencils and shore paintings

Nautical memorabilia, a brown and maroon quilt, and a claw foot bathtub with a separate shower.

Sleeps 3Beds Queen, plus a twin pullout loveseat

Wildflower Room

The Innkeeper's first memory of the Inn

Looking out its windows at wildflowers covering the property. A cozy room above the kitchen, overlooking the breakfast room patio and courtyard.

Sleeps 1 or 2Beds Queen

Church Tower Room

Plush quilts in blues, greens and florals, and Norman Rockwell prints on the walls. Overlooks the breakfast room patio and the courtyard.

Back of house

Maxwell Room

Angled ceilings and a view of the front yard and the Lyme Common. Green and purple handmade quilts.

New Hampshire Room

Named, of course, for the state

Sloping ceilings, a view of the front patio and garden, an antique blanket chest and maple furniture.

Hitchcock Room

A corner room with black and white Hitchcock-style furniture, hand-stitched quilts of green vines and purple flowers, and hand-stenciled floral borders.

Dartmouth Room

Windows over the brick Smokehouse

Large windows overlooking the patio, the brick Smokehouse and the old stonewall off the Courtyard. Light green walls, lilac stencils and mahogany furniture.

A bedroom and a separate sitting room with two wingback chairs. Grand queen four-poster bed, matching mahogany armoire, and a Keurig.
