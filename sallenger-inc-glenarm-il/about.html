Our pride runs deep

Sallenger Inc. carries the family name, and the reputation that comes with it. Here is how the company came to be, in order.

  1. 1977

    On equipment from age seven

    Bill Sallenger grew up next to Gary Sandidge, owner of Sandidge Bulldozing & Excavating in Glenarm. From childhood he was on heavy equipment, and under Gary's guidance he was operating it on jobsites from the age of 7. The dream started there: some day he would own an excavating company.

  2. 1992

    Dirt foreman at 21

    Two weeks out of college, Bill went to work for a large, reputable heavy highway contractor as a dirt foreman. He worked his way up and became Vice President of Construction Operations at age 28.

  3. 2002

    Bill and Cathy start the company

    Bill and Cathy married and started a family in the late 1990s. Cathy, business-minded herself, saw his drive, and together they set a plan in motion. In 2002, a few months after 9/11, they formed an earthmoving company. It was a time of uncertainty for the country; the decision turned out to be the right one. They started with 3 pieces of heavy equipment.

  4. 2008

    The next one in the seat

    Billy Sallenger, seven years old, on a Caterpillar D6R.

  5. Today

    Over 40 machines, Illinois and Missouri

    Sallenger Inc. keeps growing each year through referrals and repeat business, filling a need in Illinois and Missouri for quality earthmoving delivered by honest, hard-working people.
