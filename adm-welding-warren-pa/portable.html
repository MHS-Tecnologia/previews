Portable bridges, ready to rent or buy.

Hinged, hybrid, “skidder” panel and long-span temporary bridges, designed for repeated use in different locations. Nearly all rentals include a purchase option.

Hinged portable bridges

Readily available, rapidly deployable.

  • Readily available: 30’ through 50’ lengths typically maintained in inventory for immediate rental, rental/purchase, or outright purchase.
  • 12’-6” travel width. 45-ton nominal capacity when used at “full” span. Capacity can be increased by shortening span.
  • Hinged along their length for transport as a single legal-width assembly.
  • Tapered ends, heavy-duty towbars, and additional reinforcing to withstand installation at multiple sites and handling with excavating/forestry equipment. Bridges can be “skidded” (pushed/pulled) for significant distances.
  • Pipe guardrail system and white oak plank deck system, pre-installed.
  • Typical installation time under one hour.
  • Other lengths, capacities, guardrail systems, and deck systems available upon request. Also suitable for permanent installations.

Hybrid portable bridges

Available in a variety of configurations.

Used instead of the hinged bridges when:

  • the desired width is greater than the hinged bridges can accommodate;
  • you want to reduce pick/handling weight;
  • the structure’s dimensions or weight mean it has to travel as multiple loads;
  • the guardrail system is more elaborate than the hinged bridges can take.

Partial-width by full-length modular units, with guardrail and deck pre-installed, that bolt together. Can be designed/fabricated to nearly any width, capacity, and lengths up through 70’. Also suitable for permanent installations.

“Skidder” panel bridges

Alternative to timber mat bridges.

Developed as a more durable, engineered alternative to timber mat bridges. Originally intended for timber skidding operations, but suitable for any shorter span, shorter duration, low traffic volume application where timber mats might otherwise be used. Can offer a lower price point than the hinged and hybrid bridges, depending on the options selected.

Two half-width by full-length steel-framed panels, with (optional) guardrail and timber deck pre-installed. Panels are butted together, and two threaded rods can be inserted to connect them.

Long-span temporary bridges

Modular solution for longer crossings.

  • For lengths from 75’ through 100’.
  • Typically install in 2-3 days with 4 laborers plus equipment/operators.
