The builders behind MSC’s machines

MSC sells and services cutting and converting technology for many industrial sectors, and has partnered with machine builders it calls the highest quality and most reputable in the world.

  • ATOM

    Vigevano, Italy

    Began building cutting machines for the Vigevano footwear district more than 65 years ago and moved from die cutting presses to automatic oscillating knife systems. ATOM and MSC formed ATOM-MSC USA in 2006, after nearly 45 years of working together.

    See FlashCut tables and die cutting presses

  • Chiesa Artorige

    Vigevano, Italy

    Building cutting presses for more than 50 years: the TTM full beam press and the Sanson F1 EDI CNC traveling head press. A market leader for abrasive, gasket, foam and automotive die cutting.

    See die cutting presses

  • RFS Co.Mec

    Vigevano footwear district, Italy

    More than 50 years of hydraulic presses, plating and printing machines. Over the past 20 years it added thermoforming units, vulcanizing presses and heated presses for the automotive sector.

    See die cutting presses

  • Guidolin Girotto

    Italy

    Electro-mechanical flat-bed and rotary die cutting presses, digital laser and dieless knife cutting, and guillotines, for kiss cutting and total cutting of material in rolls or sheets.

    See kiss cutting presses

  • FEBA

    Busto Arsizio and Bienate, Lombardy, Italy

    Founded in 1968. Lathe slitting systems, slitters-rewinders, log winders, packaging and sawing machines, and special laminating systems. MSC is its North American agent.

    See log slitting and slitting and rewinding

  • IMESA

    Italy

    Forty years in cutting, coating and converting rubber, fibres, foam, adhesive tapes, leather, textiles and other flexible materials, in rolls or sheets.

    See log slitting and laminating

  • International

    Zlín, Czech Republic

    Custom converting machinery and complete lines: laminating, slitting, sheeting, unwinding, rewinding and material handling, engineered, built and tested in-house.

    See laminating and slitting and sheeting

  • Antares

    Italy, since 1967

    Precision strip cutting machines for footwear, leather goods and mechanical sectors, cutting strips from 150 to 2000 mm.

    See slitting and rewinding

Not sure which builder fits?

MSC works across all of them. Describe the job and they will point you to the right machine.
