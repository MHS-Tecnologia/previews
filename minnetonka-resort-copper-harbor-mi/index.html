The resort

Use our main lodge for reading and relaxing by the fireplace. Every sized family will feel at home in these comfortable resort rooms.

  • Picnic tables and grills on-site
  • Direct TV in all units and main lodge
  • Free Wi-Fi and non-smoking facilities
  • A bike washing station, and rags for dirty bikes, just ask
  • Dogs are welcome in most cottages for a minimal fee

Spirit of the North Wellness Center

Our family hosts Spirit of the North Wellness Center. Located on the main grounds, the center offers massage and wellness treatments, yoga, lectures and workshops.

At the blinking light, we are on the southeast corner.

Take US Highway 41 or M-26 north to Copper Harbor. We're steps away from dining, shopping, ferry tours, world-class mountain biking, hiking, rock hunting and local berry sundaes.
