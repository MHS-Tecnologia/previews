The history of Meer Electric

  • 1981
    Rick and Judy Meer move from Cedarburg, Wisconsin to Roswell with their three young sons. Rick, a second-generation electrician, joins an Atlanta contracting firm.
  • 1984
    Rick and Judy found their own electrical business, working from home so Judy can balance the business with their growing family. The first focus is new commercial construction.
  • 1992
    Rick joins the Roswell Rotary Club. By now the Meer Electric Man is a familiar sight on trucks around town.
  • 2004
    Kelly Meer starts the Safety Town Program in Roswell, which has since taught more than 12,000 children aged 4 to 6 about street safety.
  • 2009
    Meer Electric begins powering the Taste of Alpharetta.
  • 2013
    Peter Meer retires as a Captain after 21 years as a firefighter and EMT with the Roswell Fire Department.
  • Today
    After 39 years, Rick and Judy have retired. Peter and Kelly are the second-generation owners, and Peter is Vice President of Operations.
