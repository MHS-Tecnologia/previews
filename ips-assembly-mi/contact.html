What to send for a PCB assembly

  • Bill of Materials with manufacturer part numbers
  • Gerber or ODB++ files
  • Assembly drawing or placement file
  • Quantities, including any prototype quantity
  • Your target date
  • Kitted (you supply parts) or turnkey (IPS buys them)

For a cable or wire harness

  • A drawing or wire list: gauge, length, color, connectors and terminals
  • Labeling or sleeving requirements
  • Quantity

Questions buyers ask first

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. Prototype quantities of one and recurring production lots run on the same lines. Pricing steps down with quantity, and IPS shows you the breaks.

How long does a build take?

Lead time is driven mostly by component availability, which IPS confirms at quote. Assembly time starts once boards and parts are received; quick-turn prototypes and same-day harness builds are available when material is on hand.

Kitted or turnkey: which is better?

Turnkey gives you one purchase order and one supplier managing availability and alternates. Kitted can make sense if you already hold inventory or have negotiated pricing. Many customers start kitted and move to turnkey as volumes grow.

What quality standards do you build to?

Boards are built and inspected to IPC-A-610 Class 2 or Class 3 as specified on your drawing; cables and harnesses to IPC/WHMA-A-620. IPS is ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949:2016 certified, with 3D SPI and 3D AOI standard and X-ray for hidden joints.

Can you handle defense work?

Yes. IPS is ITAR registered with Enhanced JCP certification, CAGE code 9VP58, and had delivered 46 unique NSNs with a 100% on-time record on DoD orders as of September 2026.

Do you provide first article inspection reports?

Yes. A first article inspection is part of every new build, and a formal FAI report is provided when your drawing or contract requires it.

How do you package and ship?

ESD-safe materials, protective foam and labeled cartons as standard; MIL-STD-129 labeling and ASTM D3951 packaging for government orders; custom packaging to your spec.

Can we visit the facility?

Yes. Customers are welcome to visit the Livonia facility and tour the floor. Contact your IPS representative or call (734) 391-0080 to schedule.
