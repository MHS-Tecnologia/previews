Any color you need

Powder coat in almost any custom color: your team, your college, your company. Textured or glossy finish.

Accessories

Start and end sections, inside and outside corners, universal corners, couplers, and one-piece tree rings in 3′, 4′ and 5′.

Blasted to near white metal before the powder coat goes on.

JDR is the first and only landscape edging manufacturer in the nation to use the Blastec shot blast system before powder coating. Every piece of DuraEdge goes through three stages.

  1. Detergent pre-wash

    Removes the oils, grease and contaminants left from making and handling the steel.

  2. Shot blasting

    A centrifugal wheel throws abrasive at the steel, stripping rust, mill scale and impurities, to SSPC preparation standards.

  3. Powder coating

    An ASTM-compliant TGIC powder coat, baked hard against humidity, salt spray and sunlight.

Powder coating can last up to three times longer than liquid paint. Specifying it? Specifier insert (PDF)
