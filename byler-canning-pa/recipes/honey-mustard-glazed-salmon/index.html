Honey Mustard Glazed Salmon

Level
Beginner
Cook time
45 minutes
Servings
4

Made with Byler’s Mild Pepper Mustard, or the Hot Pepper Mustard if you like heat.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets, 4 oz each
  • ½ cup Byler’s Relish House Mild or Hot Pepper Mustard
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ¼ cup olive or avocado oil, or your favorite neutral oil
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • 1 clove fresh garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, scrubbed and sliced thin
  • 1 large zucchini, or vegetables of your choice
  • Optional garnish: parsley, lemon slices
  • Parchment paper and foil

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Peel the potatoes and slice them ¼ inch thick. Toss them in a small bowl with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Divide the potatoes between 4 parchment packets. Fold up the parchment, then wrap each in foil.
  4. Bake on a small baking sheet for about 20 minutes, until the potatoes start to soften.
  5. Slice the vegetables into small pieces and toss them in the remaining oil, salt and pepper.
  6. In the food processor, pulse the garlic to mince. Add the mustard, honey and orange juice.
  7. Top the potatoes with the vegetables and one piece of salmon per packet.
  8. Brush the salmon with the sauce and top with fresh or dried parsley.
  9. Fold up the parchment and foil and bake 20 to 25 minutes, until the salmon reaches 140°F inside and flakes with a fork.
  10. Simmer the extra sauce in a saucepan on low heat until it thickens, and spoon it over the salmon to serve.
  11. Serve in the parchment packets on a plate, for easy cleanup.
