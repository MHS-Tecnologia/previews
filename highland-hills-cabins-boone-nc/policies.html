Rates & policies

Rates depend on your travel dates. Call for yours: 800-948-5276.

Rates

Please call for rates specific to your travel dates. Rates do not include tax and may change without notice. Rates may be higher on holidays and special events.

Weekdays are Sunday through Thursday. Weekends are Friday or Saturday.

Ask your reservation agent about discounts for AARP, military, large groups and multi-night stays.

Deposits

A deposit may be required for reservations. All deposits are non-refundable.

Cancellation

We need at least 1 day’s notice to cancel a reservation. Some reservations for multiple units may need 7 days’ notice to cancel without penalty.

Late cancellation fees: $25 for rooms, $50 for any cabin or cottage, $100 for the Cherokee or Stuart cabins. A no show, no call is charged one night and the reservation is forfeited.

Pets

Small dogs are permitted in some designated dog friendly units for an additional fee. Not all units are pet friendly, so ask your reservation agent about availability and the nightly fee.

Pool

Open Memorial Day to Labor Day, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily, weather permitting. Children under 18 must be with an adult, adults please do not swim alone, and no food or drinks in the pool area.

Barbecues

Local fire code keeps barbecues at least 15 feet from any structure and off every porch and balcony. Please use the designated areas.
