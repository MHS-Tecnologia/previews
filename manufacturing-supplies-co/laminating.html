Laminating and material handling lines

Laminators, flatbed laminators and unwind and rewind systems for foam, rubber, insulation, non-wovens, adhesives, composites, films, textiles and conveyor belting.

“A laminator defines the structure of the material before it ever reaches the cutting table.”

From MSC’s laminating page

Three parts of a line

Bond strength, layer alignment, adhesive consistency and dimensional stability are set at lamination, not after.

  • International laminators

    ELM series, LM series

    Working widths from 1300 to 2300 mm, and now up to 3000 mm.

  • Flatbed double belt laminators

    IMESA K-FB

    Uniform heat, controlled pressure and repeatable bonding for PSA and heat-activated films, under PLC control.

  • Unwinding and winding

    International ICZ UNW E, S, ARM and CR

    Standard and custom unwind and rewind stands for foams, rubber, conveyor belting, cork, textiles, tapes, laminates and composites.

IMESA K-FB in figures

A modular line: unwinding, laminating, slitting, cross-cutting, corona treatment, anti-static, inspection and conveyor units can be combined, moved, added or removed.

International, from Zlín

International engineers, builds and tests its machines in-house in Zlín, Czech Republic, and measures critical parts in 3D before assembly. Every system is tested for function, safety and production readiness before delivery.
